# Content Preservation & Cumulative Growth Rule

## 🚨 MANDATORY RULE: NEVER DELETE OR OVERWRITE EXISTING SENTENCES / CONTENT

1. **Strictly Cumulative Content**:
   - Never replace, reduce, or delete previously written examples, dialogues, exercises, vocabulary words, or explanations.
   - When the user asks for "more examples", "vlogging sentences", or "conversational scripts", **ALWAYS APPEND** new sections and examples while **PRESERVING 100%** of all existing content.

2. **Lesson Completeness Standards**:
   Every daily lesson must retain ALL of the following simultaneously:
   - `🌟 Subhāṣitam` (Verse, IAST, Translation, Context)
   - `📖 गद्य-पाठः / मूल-कथा` (Original prose or authentic classical reading passage)
   - `🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्` (Everyday situational dialogues: meeting friends, family introductions, street directions, market shopping, cafe ordering, phone etiquette, routines, etc.)
   - `🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्` (Vlogging scripts, camera cues, street interviews, video intros/outros, creator CTAs)
   - `🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः` (Word-by-word grammatical dissection and prose ordering)
   - `🗂️ शब्दकोशः` (Vocabulary tables)
   - `💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः` (Grammar rules, conjugation matrices, and declension grids)
   - `📝 अभ्यासः` (Exercises, fill-in-the-blanks, spoken translations)
   - `🔑 Answers to Exercises` (Complete answer keys)
