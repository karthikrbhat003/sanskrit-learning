# Day 006: प्रथमा विभक्तिः (Nominative Case - The Subject)

Welcome to **Phase 2: The 8 Noun Cases (विभक्तयः - Vibhaktis)**! In Sanskrit, every noun ending changes depending on its grammatical role (called **कारक - Kāraka**). 

Today, we dive deeply into **प्रथमा विभक्तिः (Prathamā Vibhakti - Nominative Case)**, the case of the independent doer of an action (**कर्ता - Kartā**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> आलस्यं हि मनुष्याणां शरीरस्थो महान् रिपुः ।
> नास्त्युद्यमसमो बन्धुः कृत्वा यं नावसीदति ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *ālasyaṃ hi manuṣyāṇāṃ śarīrastho mahān ripuḥ |*
> *nāsty udyama-samo bandhuḥ kṛtvā yaṃ nāvasīdati ||*

**English Translation:**
> Laziness is indeed the greatest enemy residing within human bodies. There is no friend equal to purposeful effort, by cultivating which one never suffers.

---

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read this morning scene describing various doers of actions (*Kartā*):

### **प्रातःकाले प्रबोधः (Awakening at Dawn)**

**सूर्यः उदेति।** अन्धकारः नश्यति।
> *Sūryaḥ udeti. Andhakāraḥ naśyati.*
> **The sun rises.** Darkness vanishes.

**खगाः कूजन्ति।** वृक्षात् पत्राणि पतन्ति।
> *Khagāḥ kūjanti. Vṛkṣāt patrāṇi patanti.*
> **Birds chirp.** Leaves fall from the tree.

**भक्तौ देवालयम् गच्छतः।** तौ ईश्वरम् नमतः।
> *Bhaktau devālayam gacchataḥ. Tau īśvaram namataḥ.*
> **Two devotees go to the temple.** They two bow to the Lord.

**माता भोजनम् पचति।** बालिका दुग्धम् पिबति।
> *Mātā bhojanam pacati. Bālikā dugdham pibati.*
> **The mother cooks food.** The girl drinks milk.

**सर्वे जनाः स्व-कार्याणि कुर्वन्ति।**
> *Sarve janāḥ sva-kāryāṇi kurvanti.*
> **All people do their respective duties.**

---

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Analysis)

1. **सूर्यः उदेति (sūryaḥ udeti)**
   * *Subject (Kartā)*: **सूर्यः** (Nominative Singular Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-aḥ*)
   * *Verb (Kriyā)*: **उदेति** (rises $\rightarrow$ 3rd person singular)

2. **खगाः कूजन्ति (khagāḥ kūjanti)**
   * *Subject (Kartā)*: **खगाः** (Nominative Plural Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-āḥ*)
   * *Verb (Kriyā)*: **कूजन्ति** (chirp $\rightarrow$ 3rd person plural)

3. **भक्तौ नमतः (bhaktau namataḥ)**
   * *Subject (Kartā)*: **भक्तौ** (Nominative Dual Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-au*)
   * *Verb (Kriyā)*: **नमतः** (bow $\rightarrow$ 3rd person dual)

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Grammatical Notes)

### 1. The Core Functions of प्रथमा विभक्तिः (Prathamā Vibhakti)
The Nominative case is used in two primary scenarios:
1. **The Subject of an Active Verb (कर्तृ-कारकम् - Kartṛ Kāraka)**: The entity that independently initiates or performs the action.
2. **Predicate Noun / Identity (समानाधिकरणम्)**: In "X is Y" sentences where both nouns refer to the same entity.
   - Example: *रामः राजा अस्ति।* (Rama is king $\rightarrow$ Both *Rāmaḥ* and *Rājā* are in Prathamā).

### 2. Complete Prathamā Vibhakti Reference Matrix

| Gender / Stem Type | Example Base | Singular (एक.) | Dual (द्वि.) | Plural (बहु.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masc. in -a (अकारान्त-पुल्लिङ्गः)** | देव (deva) | **देवः** (devaḥ) | **देवौ** (devau) | **देवाः** (devāḥ) |
| **Fem. in -ā (आकारान्त-स्त्रीलिङ्गः)** | बालिका (bālikā) | **बालिका** (bālikā) | **बालिके** (bālike) | **बालिकाः** (bālikāḥ) |
| **Fem. in -ī (ईकारान्त-स्त्रीलिङ्गः)** | नदी (nadī) | **नदी** (nadī) | **नद्यौ** (nadyau) | **नद्यः** (nadyaḥ) |
| **Neut. in -a (अकारान्त-नपुंसकलिङ्गः)** | पत्र (patra) | **पत्रम्** (patram) | **पत्रे** (patre) | **पत्राणि** (patrāṇi) |

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Convert the singular subject and verb into Plural (बहुवचनम्)
1. **बालकः नमति।** $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
2. **खगः कूजति।** $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
3. **पत्रम् पतति।** $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
4. **बालिका हसति।** $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
5. **भक्तः गच्छति।** $\rightarrow$ ___________________________

### Exercise 2: Translate into Sanskrit
1. Two devotees bow to God.
2. The sun rises in the morning.
3. Birds chirp and fly. (*Hint: fly = उत्पतन्ति / utpatanti*)
4. The girl drinks milk.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **बालकाः नमन्ति।** (*Bālakāḥ namanti.*)
2. **खगाः कूजन्ति।** (*Khagāḥ kūjanti.*)
3. **पत्राणि पतन्ति।** (*Patrāṇi patanti.*)
4. **बालिकाः हसन्ति।** (*Bālikāḥ hasanti.*)
5. **भक्ताः गच्छन्ति।** (*Bhaktāḥ gacchanti.*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. भक्तौ ईश्वरम् नमतः। (*Bhaktau īśvaram namataḥ.*)
2. सूर्यः प्रभाते उदेति। (*Sūryaḥ prabhāte udeti.*)
3. खगाः कूजन्ति उत्पतन्ति च। (*Khagāḥ kūjanti utpatanti ca.*)
4. बालिका दुग्धम् पिबति। (*Bālikā dugdham pibati.*)
