# Day 016: लङ्-लकारः - प्रथमभागः (Past Tense - Part 1: Talking About Yesterday)

Welcome to **Phase 3: Verb Extensions, Lakāras & Avyayas**! Up until now, we have communicated primarily in the Present Tense (*Laṭ Lakāra*). Today, we enter the world of history, memories, and talking about yesterday's activities with **लङ्-लकारः (Laṅ Lakāra - Imperfect Past Tense)**.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> गते शोको न कर्तव्यो भविष्यं नैव चिन्तयेत् ।
> वर्तमानेन कालेन वर्तयन्ति विचक्षणाः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *gate śoko na kartavyo bhaviṣyaṃ naiva cintayet |*
> *vartamānena kālena vartayanti vicakṣaṇāḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> One should not grieve for what has passed, nor should one worry about the future. Wise people live and act dynamically in the present moment!

---

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about what happened yesterday (*ह्यः किम् अभवत्?*):

### **ह्यस्तनी घटना (Yesterday's Events)**

**ह्यः रविवासरः आसीत्।** विद्यालयस्य अवकाशः अभवत्।
> *Hyaḥ ravivāsaraḥ āsīt. Vidyālayasya avakāśaḥ abhavat.*
> **Yesterday was Sunday.** There was a holiday for the school.

**रामः प्रातःकाले शीघ्रम् उदतिष्ठत्।** सः उद्यानम् अगच्छत्।
> *Rāmaḥ prātaḥkāle śīghram udatiṣṭhat. Saḥ udyānam agacchat.*
> **Rama woke up early in the morning.** He went to the park.

**तत्र तौ बालकौ कन्दुकेन अक्रीडताम्।**
> *Tatra tau bālakau kandukena akrīḍatām.*
> **There, those two boys played with a ball.**

**सर्वे बालकाः नदी-तीरे अधावन्, जले च अतरन्।**
> *Sarve bālakāḥ nadī-tīre adhāvan, jale ca ataran.*
> **All the boys ran along the riverbank and swam in the water.**

**सायङ्काले ते गृहम् प्रत्यागच्छन्, पाठम् च अपठन्।**
> *Sāyaṅkāle te gṛham pratyāgacchan, pāṭham ca apaṭhan.*
> **In the evening, they returned home and studied their lesson.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: ह्यः किम् अभवत्? (Discussing Yesterday's Activities)

In daily conversations, we frequently discuss what we did over the weekend, what someone said, or how yesterday went:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Past-Tense Actions (ह्यस्तन-कार्याणि)

| Daily Past Action | Sanskrit Past Sentence | IAST | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yesterday's Weather** | ह्यः **अतीव वृष्टिः आसीत्**। | *Hyaḥ atīva vṛṣṭiḥ āsīt.* | Yesterday there was heavy rain. |
| **Went Shopping** | सः ह्यः **आपणम् अगच्छत्**। | *Saḥ hyaḥ āpaṇam agacchat.* | He went to the market yesterday. |
| **Watched a Film** | वयम् ह्यः **चलचित्रम् (movie) अपश्याम**।| *Vayam hyaḥ calacitram apaśyāma.* | We watched a movie yesterday. |
| **Ate at Restaurant** | सा **उपहार-गृहे भोजनम् अखादत्**।| *Sā upahāra-gṛhe bhojanam akhādat.* | She ate food at the restaurant. |
| **Telephone Call** | मित्रम् मह्यम् **दूरवाणीम् अकरोत्**। | *Mitram mahyam dūravāṇīm akarot.* | My friend called me yesterday. |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Talking About the Weekend

* **प्रिया (Priya):** हरिः ओम् राधे! ह्यः रविवासरे भवती **कुत्र अगच्छत्**?
  > *Hariḥ om Rādhe! Hyaḥ ravivāsare bhavatī kutra agacchat?*
  > *Hello Radha! Where did you go yesterday on Sunday?*
* **राधा (Radha):** ह्यः अहम् परिवारेण सह **उद्यानम् अगच्छम्**। भवती किम् अकरोत्?
  > *Hyaḥ aham parivāreṇa saha udyānam agaccham. Bhavatī kim akarot?*
  > *Yesterday I went to the park with my family. What did you do?*
* **प्रिया:** ह्यः मम गृहे **उत्सवः आसीत्**। बहवः बान्धवाः (relatives) **आगच्छन्**।
  > *Hyaḥ mama gṛhe utsavaḥ āsīt. Bahavaḥ bāndhavāḥ āgacchan.*
  > *Yesterday there was a celebration at my house. Many relatives came.*
* **राधा:** अति-शोभनम्!
  > *Ati-śobhanam!*
  > *Very wonderful!*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Analysis)

1. **रामः उद्यानम् अगच्छत् (rāmaḥ udyānam agacchat)**
   * **अगच्छत्**: 3rd Person Singular Past Tense of root *gam $\rightarrow$ gacch*
   * *Formula*: **अ (a) + गच्छ् (gacch) + त् (t) = अगच्छत् (agacchat - he went)**

2. **तौ अक्रीडताम् (tau akrīḍatām)**
   * **तौ**: Subject (They two - Dual)
   * **अक्रीडताम्**: 3rd Person Dual Past Tense
   * *Formula*: **अ + क्रीड् + ताम् = अक्रीडताम् (akrīḍatām - they two played)**

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (The 3rd Person Past Tense Blueprint)

$$\text{Augment \textbf{अ- (a-)}} + \text{Verb Stem} + \text{Past Suffix}$$

| Number | Suffix | Example (root: पठ् - paṭh) | Example (root: गम् $\rightarrow$ गच्छ्) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Singular (एकवचनम्)** | **-त् (-t)** | **अपठत्** (*apaṭhat* - he read) | **अगच्छत्** (*agacchat* - he went) |
| **Dual (द्विवचनम्)** | **-ताम् (-tām)** | **अपठताम्** (*apaṭhatām* - they 2 read)| **अगच्छताम्** (*agacchatām* - they 2 went)|
| **Plural (बहुवचनम्)** | **-अन् (-an)** | **अपठन्** (*apaṭhan* - they read) | **अगच्छन्** (*agacchan* - they went) |

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Transform the Present tense sentences into Past tense (लङ् लकारः)
1. बालकः पुस्तकम् पठति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
2. तौ छात्रौ विद्यालयम् गच्छतः। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
3. बालकाः जले तरन्ति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
4. सीता पत्रम् लिखति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
5. तत्र पशवः सन्ति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________

### Exercise 2: Translate into Spoken Sanskrit
1. Yesterday was Sunday.
2. Where did you go yesterday? $\rightarrow$ I went to the market.
3. Yesterday my friend called me.
4. The children played with a ball in the garden.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. बालकः पुस्तकम् **अपठत्**। (*Bālakaḥ pustakam apaṭhat.*)
2. तौ छात्रौ विद्यालयम् **अगच्छताम्**। (*Tau chātrau vidyālayam agacchatām.*)
3. बालकाः जले **अतरन्**। (*Bālakāḥ jale ataran.*)
4. सीता पत्रम् **अलिखत्**। (*Sītā patram alikhat.*)
5. तत्र पशवः **आसन्**। (*Tatra paśavaḥ āsan.*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **ह्यः रविवासरः आसीत्।** (*Hyaḥ ravivāsaraḥ āsīt.*)
2. **ह्यः भवान् कुत्र अगच्छत्? $\rightarrow$ अहम् आपणम् अगच्छम्।**
3. **ह्यः मम मित्रम् माम् आह्वयत् (दूरवाणीम् अकरोत्)।**
4. **बालकाः उद्याने कन्दुकेन अक्रीडन्।** (*Bālakāḥ udyāne kandukena akrīḍan.*)
