# Day 014: कारक-समन्वयः (The 6 Kārakas & All-Case Synthesis)

Welcome to Day 14! Now that we have explored each of the 8 noun cases individually, today we synthesize them into a unified, interconnected system. 

In Paninian Sanskrit grammar, the concept of **कारक (Kāraka)** describes how a noun directly connects to the **action / verb (क्रियान्वयि कारकम्)**.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This foundational verse enumerates the **6 Classical Kārakas** of Sanskrit:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> कर्ता कर्म च करणं सम्प्रदानं तथैव च ।
> अपादानाधिकरणमित्याहुः कारकाणि षट् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *kartā karma ca karaṇaṃ sampradānaṃ tathaiva ca |*
> *apādānādhikaraṇam ity āhuḥ kārakāṇi ṣaṭ ||*

**English Translation:**
> The Agent (*Kartā*), the Object (*Karma*), the Instrument (*Karaṇa*), the Recipient (*Sampradāna*), the Source (*Apādāna*), and the Location (*Adhikaraṇa*)—these are declared to be the six Kārakas.

---

## 📖 The Master All-Case Sanskrit Sentence

Observe how **all eight cases** can be synthesized seamlessly into a single, beautiful Sanskrit sentence:

> **"हे नराः! अयोध्यायाः राजा रामः स्वहस्तेन कोशात् याचकेभ्यः नगरे धनम् ददाति।"**

*Transliteration:*
> *He narāḥ! Ayodhyāyāḥ rājā Rāmaḥ svahastena kośāt yācakebhyaḥ nagare dhanam dadāti.*

*English Translation:*
> **"O citizens! Rama, the king of Ayodhya, gives wealth with his own hand from the treasury to the needy in the city."**

---

## 🔍 Case-by-Case Anatomical Breakdown

Look at how every single word answers a specific grammatical question:

| Word in Sentence | Case (विभक्तिः) | Role / Kāraka | Question Answered | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **हे नराः!** | सम्बोधन (Vocative) | Address | *कस्य सम्बोधनम्?* | O people/citizens! |
| **रामः** | प्रथमा (Nominative) | **कर्ता (Kartā)** | *कः ददाति?* (Who gives?) | Rama (the doer) |
| **अयोध्यायाः** | षष्ठी (Genitive) | **सम्बन्ध (Sambandha)** | *कस्याः राजा?* (King of where?) | of Ayodhya |
| **धनम्** | द्वितीया (Accusative) | **कर्म (Karma)** | *किम् ददाति?* (What gives?) | wealth (object) |
| **स्वहस्तेन** | तृतीया (Instrumental) | **करण (Karaṇa)** | *केन ददाति?* (With what?) | with his own hand |
| **याचकेभ्यः** | चतुर्थी (Dative) | **सम्प्रदान (Sampradāna)**| *केभ्यः ददाति?* (To whom?) | to the seekers |
| **कोशात्** | पञ्चमी (Ablative) | **अपादान (Apādāna)** | *कस्मात् ददाति?* (From where?) | from the treasury |
| **नगरे** | सप्तमी (Locative) | **अधिकरण (Adhikaraṇa)** | *कुत्र/कस्मिन् ददाति?* (In where?) | in the city |
| **ददाति** | क्रिया (Verb) | Action | *किं करोति?* (What action?) | gives |

## 💡 Kāraka & Vibhakti Diagnostic Matrix

Use this chart to instantly determine which Vibhakti to use for any sentence:

```text
┌──────────────┬────────────────────────┬──────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┐
│ Case         │ Kāraka Role            │ Question Word    │ Helper Meaning              │
├──────────────┼────────────────────────┼──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. प्रथमा    │ कर्ता (Kartā)          │ कः / का / किम्   │ The Subject / Doer          │
│ 2. द्वितीया   │ कर्म (Karma)           │ कम् / काम् / किम् │ The Direct Object / Goal    │
│ 3. तृतीया    │ करण (Karaṇa)           │ केन / कया        │ By / With (Means & Co)      │
│ 4. चतुर्थी   │ सम्प्रदान (Sampradāna) │ कस्मै / कस्यै    │ To / For (Recipient/Purpose)│
│ 5. पञ्चमी    │ अपादान (Apādāna)       │ कस्मात् / कस्याः │ From / Than (Separation)    │
│ 6. षष्ठी     │ सम्बन्ध (Sambandha)    │ कस्य / कस्याः    │ Of / 's (Relation/Position) │
│ 7. सप्तमी    │ अधिकरण (Adhikaraṇa)    │ कस्मिन् / कुत्र   │ In / On / At (Time & Place) │
│ 8. सम्बोधन   │ सम्बोधनम्              │ हे ...!          │ O ...! (Calling / Invoking) │
└──────────────┴────────────────────────┴──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **लेखन्या** = तृतीया विभक्तिः (Instrumental - with a pen)
2. **पत्रम्** = द्वितीया विभक्तिः (Accusative - letter)
3. **वृक्षात्** = पञ्चमी विभक्तिः (Ablative - from the tree)
4. **भूतले** = सप्तमी विभक्तिः (Locative - on the ground)
5. **विप्राय** = चतुर्थी विभक्तिः (Dative - to the scholar/priest)
6. **धेनुम्** = द्वितीया विभक्तिः (Accusative - cow)
7. **रामस्य** = षष्ठी विभक्तिः (Genitive - of Rama)
8. **विद्यालये** = सप्तमी विभक्तिः (Locative - in the school)
