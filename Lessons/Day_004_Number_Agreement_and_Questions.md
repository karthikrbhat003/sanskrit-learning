# Day 004: वचनत्रयम् प्रश्नाव्ययानि च (Number Agreement, Questions & Market Food Vlog)

Welcome to Day 4! One of Sanskrit's most distinctive features is its **threefold number system** (*Vacanatrayam*):
1. **एकवचनम् (Ekavacanam)**: Singular (one entity)
2. **द्विवचनम् (Dvivacanam)**: Dual (exactly two entities)
3. **बहुवचनम् (Bahuvacanam)**: Plural (three or more entities)

Today, we will learn how nouns, pronouns, and verbs transform across all three numbers, master **the 6 essential Question Words**, and practice a real-life **Street Food & Market Shopping Vlog Script** (*आपण-भ्रमणम्*).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> प्रियवाक्यप्रदानेन सर्वे तुष्यन्ति जन्तवः ।
> तस्मात्तदेव वक्तव्यं वचने का दरिद्रता ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *priyavākyapradānena sarve tuṣyanti jantavaḥ |*
> *tasmāt tad eva vaktavyaṃ vacane kā daridratā ||*

**English Translation:**
> All living beings are delighted by kind and pleasant words. Therefore, only gentle and uplifting speech should be spoken; why should one ever be stingy in speech?

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read this conversation at the school grounds (*विद्यालय-प्राङ्गणम्*):

### **कक्षायाः बहिः (Outside the Classroom)**

**शिक्षकः:** छात्राः, यूयम् **कुत्र** गच्छथ?
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Chātrāḥ, yūyam kutra gacchatha?*
> **Teacher:** Students, where are you all going?

**बालकौ:** वयम् क्रीडाङ्गणम् गच्छावः।
> *Bālakau: Vayam krīḍāṅgaṇam gacchāvaḥ.*
> **Two Boys:** We two are going to the playground.

**शिक्षकः:** तत्र **कति** बालकाः सन्ति?
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Tatra kati bālakāḥ santi?*
> **Teacher:** How many boys are there?

**बालकौ:** तत्र पञ्च बालकाः, द्वे बालिके च खेलन्ति।
> *Bālakau: Tatra pañca bālakāḥ, dve bālike ca khelanti.*
> **Two Boys:** Five boys and two girls are playing there.

**शिक्षकः:** पाठः **कदा** आरभते?
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Pāṭhaḥ kadā ārabhate?*
> **Teacher:** When does the class begin?

**छात्राः:** पाठः दशवादने आरभते।
> *Chātrāḥ: Pāṭhaḥ daśavādane ārabhate.*
> **Students:** The class begins at 10 o'clock.

**शिक्षकः:** उत्तमम्! शीघ्रम् गच्छन्तु, समये आगच्छन्तु च।
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Uttamam! Śīghram gacchantu, samaye āgacchantu ca.*
> **Teacher:** Excellent! Go quickly, and come back on time.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: प्रश्नाव्ययानि (Daily Inquiry & Shopping)

In daily life, questioning is the heart of fluent communication:

### 1. The 6 Master Sanskrit Question Words (षट्-प्रश्नाव्ययानि)

| Question Word | IAST | Core Meaning | Common Spoken Scenario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **किम्** | *kim* | What? / Is it so? | भवान् **किम्** इच्छति? (What do you want?) |
| **कुत्र** | *kutra* | Where? | आपणः **कुत्र** अस्ति? (Where is the shop?) |
| **कदा** | *kadā* | When? | रेलयानं **कदा** आगच्छति? (When does the train arrive?) |
| **कथम्** | *katham* | How? | कार्यं **कथम्** अस्ति? (How is the work going?) |
| **कति** | *kati* | How many / How much? | एतस्य मूल्यं **कति**? (How much is the price of this?) |
| **किमर्थम्** | *kimartham* | Why / What for? | भवान् **किमर्थम्** विलम्बेन आगच्छति? (Why are you coming late?) |

## 🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्: आपण-भ्रमणम् (Market Tour & Street Food Vlog)

Filming an outdoor food tour or marketplace shopping vlog? Here is an authentic spoken Sanskrit creator script:

### 1. Market Tour Speaking Script (दृश्य-वृत्तान्तः)

> **"भो दर्शकाः! अद्यतन-दृश्यवृत्तान्ते वयम् आपण-भ्रमणं (market tour) कुर्मः!"**
> *Bho darśakāḥ! Adyatana-dṛśyavṛttānte vayam āpaṇa-bhramaṇaṃ kurmaḥ!*
> **"Hey viewers! In today's vlog we are going on a market tour!"**

> **"पश्यन्तु, अत्र कति आपणाः सन्ति! (Look how many shops are here!)"**
> *Paśyantu, atra kati āpaṇāḥ santi!*

> **"वयम् इदानीं फल-विक्रेतुः समीपम् गच्छामः। भ्रातः! एतस्य फलस्य मूल्यं कति?"**
> *Vayam idānīṃ phala-vikretuḥ samīpam gacchāmaḥ. Bhrātaḥ! Etasya phalasya mūlyaṃ kati?*
> **"Now we are approaching the fruit vendor. Brother! What is the price of this fruit?"**

> **"अहो! अत्र स्वादिष्टं खाद्यम् अस्ति! कियत् रुचिकरम् (delicious)!"**
> *Aho! Atra svādiṣṭaṃ khādyam asti! Kiyat rucikaram!*
> **"Wow! Delicious food is here! How tasty!"**

> **"भवते एतत् खाद्यं रोचते वा? कृपया टिप्पणीं (comment) लिखन्तु!"**
> *Bhavate etat khādyaṃ rocate vā? Kṛpayā ṭippaṇīṃ likhantu!*
> **"Do you like this food? Please leave a comment!"**

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Prose Order)

1. **तत्र द्वे बालिके खेलतः (tatra dve bālike khelataḥ)**
   * *Subject*: द्वे बालिके (*two girls* - Dual feminine)
   * *Verb*: खेलतः (*the two play* - Dual 3rd person)
   * *Agreement Rule*: The dual subject *bālike* demands the dual verb ending **-तः (-taḥ)**.

2. **बालकाः खेलन्ति (bālakāḥ khelanti)**
   * *Subject*: बालकाः (*boys* - Plural masculine)
   * *Verb*: खेलन्ति (*they play* - Plural 3rd person)
   * *Agreement Rule*: The plural subject *bālakāḥ* takes the plural verb ending **-न्ति (-nti)**.

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Number Grid Across Nouns & Verbs)

### 1. Nouns Across 3 Numbers:
* **Masc**: बालकः (1) $\rightarrow$ बालकौ (2) $\rightarrow$ बालकाः (3+)
* **Fem**: बालिका (1) $\rightarrow$ बालिके (2) $\rightarrow$ बालिकाः (3+)
* **Neut**: फलम् (1) $\rightarrow$ फले (2) $\rightarrow$ फलानि (3+)

### 2. Verbs Across 3 Numbers:
* **Singular**: बालकः **पठति** (*One boy reads*)
* **Dual**: बालकौ **पठतः** (*Two boys read*)
* **Plural**: बालकाः **पठन्ति** (*Boys read*)
