# Day 030: पञ्चतन्त्रम् - मूर्ख-पण्डित-कथा (Four Scholars & "Walking & Talking" Live Vlog)

Welcome to Day 30! Today, we read one of the most satirical fables in Sanskrit literature: **मूर्ख-पण्डित-कथा (The Four Scholars and the Lion)**.

We also master **शतृ-प्रत्ययः (Shatṛ Participles)** for expressing multi-tasking in everyday life and dynamic **"Walking & Talking" Street Vlogging and Live Streaming** (*मार्गे चलन् सम्भाषणं करोति / Walking while talking on camera*).

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> वरं बुद्धिर्न सा विद्या विद्याया बुद्धिरुत्तमा ।
> बुद्धिहीना विनश्यन्ति यथा ते सिंहकारकाः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *varaṃ buddhir na sā vidyā vidyāyā buddhir uttamā |*
> *buddhihīnā vinaśyanti yathā te siṃhakārakāḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> Practical common sense is far superior to mere bookish learning; wisdom is indeed greater than abstract scholarship. Those bereft of practical intellect perish, just like the creators of the lion!

## 📖 मूल-कथा (Original Panchatantra Prose)

### **सिंह-कारक-मूर्खाः (The Lion-Makers)**

**एकस्मिन् ग्रामे चत्वारः मित्राणि अवसन्।**
> *Ekasmin grāme catvāraḥ mitrāṇi avasan.*
> In a village lived four friends.

**तेषु त्रयः शास्त्र-पारङ्गताः (great book-scholars) आसन्, किन्तु बुद्धि-रहिताः (devoid of common sense)। चतुर्थः तु विद्या-हीनः किन्तु अति-बुद्धिमान् आसीत्। तस्य नाम सुबुद्धिः।**
> *Teṣu trayaḥ śāstra-pāraṅgatāḥ āsan, kintu buddhi-rahitāḥ. Caturthaḥ tu vidyā-hīnaḥ kintu ati-buddhimān āsīt. Tasya nāma Subuddhiḥ.*
> Among them, three were masters of theoretical scriptures but completely lacking in practical intellect. The fourth had no formal education, but was exceptionally wise. His name was Subuddhi.

**ते धन-सम्पादनाय अन्य-देशम् **गच्छन्तः** (while going) मार्गे एकस्मिन् वने कानिचित् अस्थीनि (bones) अपश्यन्।**
> *Te dhana-sampādanāya anya-deśam gacchantaḥ mārge ekasmin vane kānicit asthīni apaśyan.*
> **While traveling** to another land to earn wealth, they saw some skeletal bones in a forest along the way.

**प्रथमः अवदत्—"अहम् एतानि अस्थीनि **सङ्गृह्णन्** (assembling) कङ्कालम् रचयिष्यामि।"**
> *Prathamaḥ avadat—"Aham etāni asthīni saṅgṛhṇan kaṅkālam racayiṣyāmi."*
> The first scholar said—"**While assembling** these bones, I will reconstruct the skeleton."

**द्वितीयः अवदत्—"अहम् अस्मिन् चर्म, मांसम्, रुधिरम् च योजयिष्यामि।"**
> *Dvitīyaḥ avadat—"Aham asmin carma, māṃsam, rudhiram ca yojayiṣyāmi."*
> The second said—"I will add skin, flesh, and blood into it."

**तृतीयः अवदत्—"अहम् अस्मिन् प्राण-सञ्चारम् करिष्यामि।"**
> *Tṛtīyaḥ avadat—"Aham asmin prāṇa-sañcāram kariṣyāmi."*
> The third scholar said—"I will breathe life into it!"

**सुबुद्धिः एतत् **पश्यन्** अवदत्—"भोः! एषः सिंहः अस्ति! यदि एनम् जीवयिष्यथ, तर्हि एषः अस्मान् सर्वान् मारयिष्यति!"**
> *Subuddhiḥ etat paśyan avadat—"Bhoḥ! Eṣaḥ siṃhaḥ asti! Yadi enam jīvayiṣyatha, tarhi eṣaḥ asmān sarvān mārayiṣyati!"*
> Subuddhi, **watching** this, warned—"Beware! This is a lion! If you bring it to life, it will kill us all!"

**ते तम् **हसन्तः** अवदन्—"रे मूर्ख! वयम् स्व-विद्यायाः परीक्षणम् करिष्यामः।"**
> *Te tam hasantaḥ avadan—"Re mūrkha! Vayam sva-vidyāyāḥ parīkṣaṇam kariṣyāmaḥ."*
> **Laughing** at him, they retorted—"O fool! We will demonstrate the power of our learning!"

**सुबुद्धिः "तावत् क्षणम् प्रतीक्षध्वम्" इति **कथयन्** द्रुतम् समीपस्थम् वृक्षम् आरुहत्।**
> *Subuddhiḥ "Tāvat kṣaṇam pratīkṣadhvam" iti kathayan drutam samīpastham vṛkṣam āruhat.*
> **Saying** "Wait just a moment!", Subuddhi quickly climbed a nearby tree.

**तृतीयः यदा प्राणं प्रावेशयत्, तदा सः सिंहः जीवितः सन् तान् त्रीन् पण्डितान् तत्क्षणमेव अमारयत्।**
> *Tṛtīyaḥ yadā prāṇaṃ prāveśayat, tadā saḥ siṃhaḥ jīvitaḥ san tān trīn paṇḍitān tat-kṣaṇam eva amārayat.*
> As soon as the third infused life, the resurrected lion killed all three bookish scholars that very instant.

**सिंहस्य गमनानन्तरम् सुबुद्धिः वृक्षात् अवतीर्य सकुशलम् गृहम् अगच्छत्।**
> *Siṃhasya gamanānantaram Subuddhiḥ vṛkṣāt avatīrya sakuśalam gṛham agacchat.*
> After the lion walked away, Subuddhi climbed down from the tree and returned home safely.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: युगपद्-कार्याणि (Simultaneous Everyday Actions with Shatṛ)

In daily conversations, we frequently describe multi-tasking or doing two actions at the same time:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Shatṛ Templates

| Simultaneous Daily Actions | Sanskrit Spoken Formula | IAST | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Working while Listening** | अहम् **सङ्गीतं शृण्वन्** कार्यं करोमि। | *Aham saṅgītaṃ śṛṇvan kāryaṃ karomi.* | I work **while listening to music**. |
| **Talking while Eating** | सः **वार्तालापं कुर्वन्** भोजनं करोति। | *Saḥ vārtālāpaṃ kurvan bhojanaṃ karoti.* | He eats **while conversing**. |
| **Smiling while Answering**| सा **हसन्ती** उत्तरं ददाति। | *Sā hasantī uttaraṃ dadāti.* | She answers **while smiling**. |
| **Reading while Commuting**| छात्रः **चलन् / गच्छन्** पठति। | *Chātraḥ calan / gacchan paṭhati.* | The student reads **while walking**. |
| **Thinking while Deciding**| सः **विचारं कुर्वन्** तिष्ठति। | *Saḥ vicāraṃ kurvan tiṣṭhati.* | He stands **while pondering**. |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogue: Talking about Daily Multi-Tasking

* **माधवः (Madhav):** भवान् सङ्गणके कार्यं **कथम् करोति**?
  > *Bhavān saṅgaṇake kāryam katham karoti?*
  > *How do you do work on the computer?*
* **केशवः (Keshav):** अहम् **सङ्गीतं शृण्वन्** (while listening to music) आनन्देन कार्यं करोमि!
  > *Aham saṅgītaṃ śṛṇvan ānandena kāryaṃ karomi!*
  > *I work joyfully while listening to music!*
* **माधवः:** उत्तमम्! सा बालिका किम् करोति?
  > *Uttamam! Sā bālikā kim karoti?*
  > *Great! What is that girl doing?*
* **केशवः:** सा **हसन्ती** (while smiling) मात्रे कथां कथयति।
  > *Sā hasantī mātre kathāṃ kathayati.*
  > *She is telling a story to her mother while smiling.*

## 🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्: "चलन्-सम्भाषणम्" (Walk & Talk Outdoor Live Vlog Script)

When walking down the street, holding the selfie stick/camera, and talking live to your audience:

### 1. "Walk & Talk" Live Creator Script (शतृ-प्रयोगः)

> **"भो दर्शकाः! पश्यन्तु, अहम् अधुना नगरे **चलन्** (while walking) भवद्भिः सह सम्भाषे!"**
> *Bho darśakāḥ! Paśyantu, aham adhunā nagare calan bhavadbhiḥ saha sambhāṣe!*
> **"Hey viewers! Look, right now while walking through the city I am chatting with you!"**

> **"सूर्यस्य किरणान् **आस्वादयन्** (while enjoying the sunlight), शीतल-वायुम् **अनुभवन्** (while feeling the cool breeze) अहम् दृश्यवृत्तान्तं रचयामि।"**
> *Sūryasya kiraṇān āsvādayan, śītala-vāyum anubhavan aham dṛśyavṛttāntaṃ racayāmi.*
> **"While enjoying the sun rays and feeling the cool breeze, I am creating this vlog."**

> **"पश्यन्तु, तत्र बालकाः **क्रीडन्तः हसन्ति** (playing while laughing)!"**
> *Paśyantu, tatra bālakāḥ krīḍantaḥ hasanti!*
> **"Look, over there children are laughing while playing!"**

> **"वयम् अग्रे **गच्छन्तः** (while going forward) नूतनानि वस्तूनि द्रक्ष्यामः!"**
> *Vayam agre gacchantaḥ nūtanāni vastūni drakṣyāmaḥ!*
> **"As we continue moving forward, we will discover new things!"**

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

3. कृ (to do) $\rightarrow$ Masculine: _________ | Feminine: _________
4. चल् (to walk) $\rightarrow$ Masculine: _________ | Feminine: _________

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. धाव् $\rightarrow$ **धावन्** (*dhāvan*) | **धावन्ती** (*dhāvantī*)
2. लिख् $\rightarrow$ **लिखन्** (*likhan*) | **लिखन्ती** (*likhantī*)
3. कृ $\rightarrow$ **कुर्वन्** (*kurvan*) | **कुर्वती** (*kurvatī*)
4. चल् $\rightarrow$ **चलन्** (*calan*) | **चलन्ती** (*calantī*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **अहम् सङ्गीतं शृण्वन् कार्यं करोमि।** (*Aham saṅgītaṃ śṛṇvan kāryaṃ karomi.*)
2. **बालिका हसन्ती वदति।** (*Bālikā hasantī vadati.*)
3. **छात्रः चलन् पठति।** (*Chātraḥ calan paṭhati.*)
4. **बालकाः वार्तालापं कुर्वन्तः खादन्ति।** (*Bālakāḥ vārtālāpaṃ kurvantaḥ khādanti.*)

### Answers to Exercise 3:
1. **मार्गे चलन् अहम् यन्त्रं पश्यन् सम्भाषे।** (*Mārge calan aham yantraṃ paśyan sambhāṣe.*)
2. **सूर्यस्य प्रकाशम् आस्वादयन् अहम् दृश्यं गृह्णामि।** (*Sūryasya prakāśam āsvādayan aham dṛśyaṃ gṛhṇāmi.*)
3. **बालकाः हसन्तः क्रीडन्ति।** (*Bālakāḥ hasantaḥ krīḍanti.*)
