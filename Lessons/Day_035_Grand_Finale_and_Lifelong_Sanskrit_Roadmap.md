## 🌟 Graduation Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> भाषासु मुख्या मधुरा दिव्या गीर्वाणभारती ।
> तस्यां हि काव्यं मधुरं तस्मादपि सुभाषितम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *bhāṣāsu mukhyā madhurā divyā gīrvāṇabhāratī |*
> *tasyāṃ hi kāvyaṃ madhuraṃ tasmād api subhāṣitam ||*

**English Translation:**
> Among all languages, the divine Sanskrit tongue (*Gīrvāṇabhāratī*) is chief, sublime, and sweet. In it, poetry is especially sweet, and sweetest of all are its timeless maxims of wisdom!

## 🧭 The 4-Step Universal Sanskrit Reading Algorithm

Whenever you open a new classical text, follow this exact 4-step algorithm:

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. पदच्छेदः (Padaccheda - Word Segmentation)                               │
│    Split compound Sandhis and identify where each independent word begins.  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. प्रतिपदार्थः (Pratipadārtha - Morphological Identification)              │
│    Identify: Noun or Verb? What Case (विभक्तिः)? What Tense/Mood (लकारः)?    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. अन्वयः (Anvaya - Logical Syntactic Reordering)                           │
│    Rearrange into: [Subject] ──► [Modifiers/Context] ──► [Object] ──► [Verb]│
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. भावार्थः (Bhāvārtha - Philosophical & Poetic Synthesis)                   │
│    Combine words into fluid, contextually rich English meaning.             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 🌟 The Universal Shanti Mantra (शान्ति-मन्त्रः)

Let us conclude your structured course with the eternal prayer for universal peace and illumination:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> ॐ असतो मा सद्गमय ।
> तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय ।
> मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय ।
> ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *oṃ asato mā sad gamaya |*
> *tamaso mā jyotir gamaya |*
> *mṛtyor mā amṛtaṃ gamaya |*
> *oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> Lead me from the unreal to the Real;  
> Lead me from darkness into the Light of Knowledge;  
> Lead me from mortality into the Eternal Nectar of Immortality.  
> **Om Peace, Peace, Peace!**
