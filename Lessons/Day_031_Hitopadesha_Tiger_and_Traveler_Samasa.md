# Day 031: हितोपदेशः - वृद्ध-व्याघ्र-कथा (Tiger & Traveler: Samāsa in Daily Speech)

Welcome to Day 31! Today, we explore Narayana Pandita's classic from the **हितोपदेशः (Hitopadeśa - Friendly Advice)**: **वृद्ध-व्याघ्रः पथिकः च (The Old Tiger and the Greedy Traveler)**.

We also demystify how **समासः (Samāsa - Compound Words)** operates in everyday spoken Sanskrit (**रविवासरः, जलपानम्, उपहारगृहम्, समयपालनम्, मातापितरौ**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> अनिष्टादिष्टलाभेऽपि न गतिर्जायते शुभा ।
> यत्रास्ते विषसंसर्गोऽमृतं तदपि मृत्यवे ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *aniṣṭād iṣṭa-lābhe 'pi na gatir jāyate śubhā |*
> *yatrāste viṣa-saṃsargo 'mṛtaṃ tad api mṛtyave ||*

**English Translation:**
> Even if a desired object is obtained from an ominous or perilous source, the outcome is never auspicious. Wherever there is contact with poison, even nectar leads straight to death!

## 📖 मूल-कथा (Original Hitopadeśa Prose)

### **कङ्कण-लोभेन निमग्नः पथिकः (The Bangle-Greedy Traveler)**

**एकस्मिन् अरण्ये एकः वृद्धः व्याघ्रः सरस्तीरे कुश-हस्तः (with sacred grass in hand) स्नानं कृत्वा अतिष्ठत्।**
> *Ekasmin araṇye ekaḥ vṛddhaḥ vyāghraḥ saras-tīre kuśa-hastaḥ snānaṃ kṛtvā atiṣṭhat.*
> In a dense forest, an old tiger stood on a lake-shore holding sacred grass after bathing.

**सः मार्गे आगच्छन्तम् एकम् पान्थम् (पथिकम्) आह्वयत्—"भोः भोः पान्थ! इदम् सुवर्ण-कङ्कणम् (gold bangle) गृहाण!"**
> *Saḥ mārge āgacchantam ekam pāntham (pathikam) āhvayat—"Bhoḥ bhoḥ pāntha! Idam suvarṇa-kaṅkaṇam gṛhāṇa!"*
> He called out to a traveler coming along the path—"O traveler! Please accept this golden bangle!"

**पथिकः लोभेन अचिन्तयत्—"भाग्येन एतत् लभ्यते। किन्तु अस्मिन् प्राण-सङ्शये प्रवृत्तिः न युक्ता।"**
> *Pathikaḥ lobhena acintayat—"Bhāgyena etat labhyate. Kintu asmin prāṇa-saṅśaye pravṛttiḥ na yuktā."*
> The traveler thought with greed—"Only through fortune is this obtained! But risking one's life is not prudent."

**व्याघ्रः अवदत्—"भोः पान्थ! अहम् पूर्वजन्मनि दुराचारी आसम्। अधुना तु अहम् दान-धर्म-रतः, वृद्धः, गलित-नख-दन्तः (with fallen claws and teeth) च अस्मि। मयि कथं न विश्वसिषि?"**
> *Vyāghraḥ avadat—"Bhoḥ pāntha! Aham pūrvajanmani durācārī āsam. Adhunā tu aham dāna-dharma-rataḥ, vṛddhaḥ, galita-nakha-dantaḥ ca asmi. Mayi kathaṃ na viśvasiṣi?"*
> The tiger spoke—"O traveler! In my youth I was wicked. But now I am devoted to charity and virtue, old, with my claws and teeth worn away. Why do you not trust me?"

**"सरसि स्नात्वा इदम् सुवर्ण-कङ्कणम् स्वीकुरु।"**
> *"Sarasi snātvā idam suvarṇa-kaṅkaṇam svīkuru."*
> "Bathe in the lake and accept this golden bangle."

**तस्य कपट-वचनम् श्रुत्वा लोभाकृष्टः पथिकः यदा सरोवरे प्रविशति, तदा सः महागम्भीरे महापङ्के (deep mud) निमग्नः अभवत्।**
> *Tasya kapaṭa-vacanam śrutvā lobhākṛṣṭaḥ pathikaḥ yadā sarovare praviśati, tadā saḥ mahāgambhīre mahāpaṅke nimagnaḥ abhavat.*
> Hearing his deceitful words and swayed by greed, when the traveler stepped into the lake, he became hopelessly trapped in deep mud.

**पङ्के पतितम् तम् **दृष्ट्वा** व्याघ्रः मन्दम् मन्दम् उपगम्य तम् अगृह्णात्, अखादत् च।**
> *Paṅke patitam tam dṛṣṭvā vyāghraḥ mandam mandam upagamya tam agṛhṇāt, akhādat ca.*
> Seeing him stuck in the mire, the tiger approached slowly, seized him, and devoured him.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: सम्भाषणे समासाः (High-Frequency Daily Compounds)

In everyday spoken Sanskrit, we use compound words (*Samāsas*) constantly without even realizing it:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Sanskrit Compounds

| Everyday Compound | Samāsa Type | Vigraha (Breakdown) | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **उपहार-गृहम्** | Tatpuruṣa | उपहाराणां गृहम् | Restaurant / Snack House |
| **जल-पानम्** | Tatpuruṣa | जलस्य पानम् | Drinking water / Refreshments |
| **गृह-कार्यम्** | Tatpuruṣa | गृहस्य कार्यम् | Homework / House chores |
| **समय-पालनम्** | Tatpuruṣa | समयस्य पालनम् | Punctuality / Time management |
| **माता-पितरौ** | Dvandva | माता च पिता च | Parents (Mother & Father) |
| **राम-लक्ष्मणौ** | Dvandva | रामः च लक्ष्मणः च | Rama and Lakshmana |
| **महा-नगरम्** | Karmadhāraya | महत् नगरम् | Metropolis / Big City |
| **सुन्दर-वाटिका** | Karmadhāraya | सुन्दरी वाटिका | Beautiful Garden |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogue: Heading for Refreshments

* **रोहनः (Rohan):** भो मित्र! किम् भवतः **गृह-कार्यम्** (homework) समाप्तम्?
  > *Bho mitra! Kim bhavataḥ gṛha-kāryam samāptam?*
  > *Friend! Is your homework finished?*
* **सोहनः (Sohan):** आम्! मम गृहकार्यं समाप्तम्। अधुना वयं **जल-पानाय** (for refreshments) **उपहार-गृहम्** (to the restaurant) गच्छामः वा?
  > *Ām! Mama gṛhakāryaṃ samāptam. Adhunā vayam jala-pānāya upahāra-gṛham gacchāmaḥ vā?*
  > *Yes! My homework is finished. Shall we now go to the restaurant for refreshments?*
* **रोहनः:** अवश्यम्! मम **माता-पितरौ** (parents) अपि अनुमतिम् अदत्ताम्।
  > *Avaśyam! Mama mātā-pitarau api anumatim adattām.*
  > *Certainly! My parents also gave permission.*
* **सोहनः:** चलतु, शीघ्रं गच्छामः!
  > *Calatu, śīghraṃ gacchāmaḥ!*
  > *Let's go, let's head out quickly!*

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (The 4 Major Sanskrit Samāsa Classes)

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Samāsa Type} & \textbf{Core Rule} & \textbf{Vigraha (Breakdown)} & \textbf{Compound Result} \\
\hline
\textbf{1. तत्पुरुष (Tatpuruṣa)} & \text{2nd word is primary} & \text{स्वर्णस्य कङ्कणम्} \text{ (bangle of gold)} & \textbf{स्वर्ण-कङ्कणम्} \\
\text{(Determinative)} & & \text{देवस्य आलयः} \text{ (abode of God)} & \textbf{देवालयः} \\
\hline
\textbf{2. कर्मधारय (Karmadhāraya)}& \text{Adjective + Noun}& \text{महान् पङ्कः} \text{ (great mud)} & \textbf{महापङ्कः} \\
\text{(Descriptive)} & & \text{नीलं कमलम्} \text{ (blue lotus)} & \textbf{नीलकमलम्} \\
\hline
\textbf{3. द्वन्द्व (Dvandva)} & \text{Both words equal ('and')}& \text{माता च पिता च} \text{ (mother & father)}& \textbf{मातापितरौ} \\
\text{(Copulative)} & & \text{सुखं च दुःखं च} & \textbf{सुखदुःखे} \\
\hline
\textbf{4. बहुव्रीहि (Bahuvrīhi)} & \text{Refers to 3rd entity} & \text{गलितौ नखदन्तौ यस्य सः}& \textbf{गलित-नख-दन्तः} \\
\text{(Relative)} & & \text{पीतं अम्बरं यस्य सः} \text{ (Vishnu)} & \textbf{पीताम्बरः} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Identify the type of Samāsa
1. **मातापितरौ** $\rightarrow$ Samāsa: _________
2. **उपहारगृहम्** $\rightarrow$ Samāsa: _________
3. **नीलकमलम्** $\rightarrow$ Samāsa: _________
4. **देवालयः** $\rightarrow$ Samāsa: _________

### Exercise 2: Translate into Spoken Sanskrit
1. My homework is finished.
2. We go to the restaurant for refreshments.
3. My parents are at home.
4. He lives in a big city (*mahā-nagaram*).

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **द्वन्द्व-समासः** (माता च पिता च)
2. **तत्पुरुष-समासः** (उपहाराणां गृहम्)
3. **कर्मधारय-समासः** (नीलं कमलम्)
4. **तत्पुरुष-समासः** (देवस्य आलयः)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **मम गृहकार्यं समाप्तम्।** (*Mama gṛhakāryaṃ samāptam.*)
2. **वयम् जलपानाय उपहारगृहं गच्छामः।** (*Vayam jalapānāya upahāragṛhaṃ gacchāmaḥ.*)
3. **मम मातापितरौ गृहे स्तः।** (*Mama mātāpitarau gṛhe staḥ.*)
4. **सः महानगरे निवसति।** (*Saḥ mahānagare nivasati.*)
