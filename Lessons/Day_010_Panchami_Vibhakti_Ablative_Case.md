# Day 010: पञ्चमी विभक्तिः (Ablative Case - Origins, Commuting & Separation)

Welcome to Day 10! Today, we explore **पञ्चमी विभक्तिः (Pañcamī Vibhakti - Ablative Case)**. In Sanskrit grammar, this case marks the **अपादान-कारकम् (Apādāna Kāraka)**—the point of origin, separation, or departure ("*from where / out of what*"), fear (**बिभेति**), protection (**रक्षति**), and comparative superiority ("*better than X*").

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This celebrated patriotic verse comes from the Ramayana:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> अपि स्वर्णमयी लङ्का न मे लक्ष्मण रोचते ।
> जननी जन्मभूमिश्च स्वर्गादपि गरीयसी ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *api svarṇamayī laṅkā na me lakṣmaṇa rocate |*
> *jananī janmabhūmiś ca svargād api garīyasī ||*

**English Translation:**
> Even though Lanka is built entirely of gold, O Lakshmana, it does not delight me. Mother and Motherland are greater and more sacred **even than heaven** (*svargād api*)!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about nature's sources and a student's daily commute:

### **उद्भवः निर्गमनम् च (Origins & Departures)**

**गङ्गा **हिमालयात्** (from the Himalayas) प्रभवति, **सागरात्** प्रति प्रवहति च।**
> *Gaṅgā Himālayāt prabhavati, sāgarāt prati pravahati ca.*
> **The Ganga springs forth from the Himalayas, and flows towards the ocean.**

**वृक्षात् **पक्वं फलम्** पतति, शाखायाः च **पत्रम्** पतति।**
> *Vṛkṣāt pakvaṃ phalam patati, śākhāyāḥ ca patram patati.*
> **A ripe fruit falls from the tree, and a leaf falls from the branch.**

**छात्रः **गृहात्** बहिः गच्छति, **विद्यालयात्** च ज्ञानम् प्राप्नोति।**
> *Chātraḥ gṛhāt bahiḥ gacchati, vidyālayāt ca jñānam prāpnoti.*
> **The student goes out from home, and obtains knowledge from the school.**

**सज्जनः **पापात्** (from sin) विरमति, दीनान् च **कष्टात्** (from suffering) रक्षति।**
> *Sajjanaḥ pāpāt viramati, dīnān ca kaṣṭāt rakṣati.*
> **The noble person refrains from misdeeds, and protects the distressed from misery.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: कुतः आगच्छति? (Daily Commuting & Origins)

In daily life, when we meet someone, one of the most common questions is **"Where are you coming from?" (कुतः आगच्छति?)**:

### 1. Commuting & Everyday Points of Departure

| Departure Place | Sanskrit Form (Pañcamī) | IAST | Example Spoken Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **From Office** | **कार्यालयात्** | *kāryālayāt* | अहम् **कार्यालयात्** आगच्छामि। (I am coming from office.) |
| **From Home** | **गृहात्** | *gṛhāt* | सः प्रातः **गृहात्** निर्गच्छति। (He leaves from home in morning.) |
| **From Market** | **आपणात्** | *āpaṇāt* | माता **आपणात्** शाकं क्रीणाति। (Mother buys vegetables from market.) |
| **From Station** | **स्थानकात्** | *sthānakāt* | रेलयानं **स्थानकात्** चलति। (The train departs from station.) |
| **From City** | **नगरात्** | *nagarāt* | वयम् **नगरात्** ग्रामं गच्छामः। (We go from city to village.) |
| **From Library** | **ग्रन्थालयात्** | *granthālayāt* | छात्राः **ग्रन्थालयात्** पुस्तकानि आनयन्ति। (Students bring books from library.)|

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Meeting on the Street

* **अमितः (Amit):** हरिः ओम्! भवान् **कुतः** (where from) आगच्छति?
  > *Hariḥ om! Bhavān kutaḥ āgacchati?*
  > *Hello! Where are you coming from?*
* **सुमितः (Sumit):** अहम् **कार्यालयात्** आगच्छामि। भवान् कुतः आगच्छति?
  > *Aham kāryālayāt āgacchāmi. Bhavān kutaḥ āgacchati?*
  > *I am coming from the office. Where are you coming from?*
* **अमितः:** अहम् **आपणात्** आगच्छामि।
  > *Aham āpaṇāt āgacchāmi.*
  > *I am coming from the market.*
* **सुमितः:** अत्रत्यः देवालयः (the temple here) **स्थानात्** कियत् दूरम्?
  > *Atratyaḥ devālayaḥ sthānāt kiyat dūram?*
  > *How far is the temple from this place?*
* **अमितः:** इतः (from here) पञ्च-निमेषाणां मार्गः (5-minute walk).
  > *Itaḥ pañca-nimeṣāṇāṃ mārgaḥ.*
  > *From here it is a 5-minute walk.*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **गङ्गा हिमालयात् प्रभवति (gaṅgā himālayāt prabhavati)**
   * **गङ्गा**: Subject (Prathamā Sg. Fem.)
   * **हिमालयात्**: Point of Origin (Pañcamī Sg. Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-āt*)
   * **प्रभवति**: Verb (originates / springs forth)

2. **छात्रः गृहात् आगच्छति (chātraḥ gṛhāt āgacchati)**
   * **गृहात्**: Point of departure (Pañcamī Sg. Neut. $\rightarrow$ *-āt*)

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Transform the noun into Pañcamī Vibhakti
1. फलम् _________ (वृक्ष) पतति।
2. सः _________ (कार्यालय) आगच्छति।
3. बालकः _________ (सर्प) बिभेति।
4. माता _________ (आपण) शाकं क्रीणाति।
5. जलम् _________ (कूपी - fem) स्रवति (leaks)。

### Exercise 2: Translate into Sanskrit for Spoken Practice
1. Where are you coming from? $\rightarrow$ I am coming from home.
2. The train leaves from the station.
3. The teacher protects the student from errors.
4. Leaves fall from the branches.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. फलम् **वृक्षात्** पतति।
2. सः **कार्यालयात्** आगच्छति।
3. बालकः **सर्पात्** बिभेति।
4. माता **आपणात्** शाकं क्रीणाति।
5. जलम् **कूप्याः** स्रवति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **भवान् कुतः आगच्छति? $\rightarrow$ अहम् गृहात् आगच्छामि।**
2. **रेलयानं स्थानकात् चलति।** (*Relayānaṃ sthānakāt calati.*)
3. **शिक्षकः छात्रम् दोषात् रक्षति।** (*Śikṣakaḥ chātram doṣāt rakṣati.*)
4. **शाखाभ्यः पत्राणि पतन्ति।** (*Śākhābhyaḥ patrāṇi patanti.*)
