## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

Let us begin with a celebrated verse illustrating how true learning brings character, capability, and happiness:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> विद्या ददाति विनयं विनयाद्याति पात्रताम् ।
> पात्रत्वाद्धनमाप्नोति धनाद्धर्मं ततः सुखम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *vidyā dadāti vinayaṃ vinayād yāti pātratām |*
> *pātratvād dhanam āpnoti dhanād dharmaṃ tataḥ sukham ||*

**English Translation:**
> Knowledge bestows humility; through humility one attains worthiness; through worthiness one gains wealth; through wealth one performs righteousness (*dharma*), and from that arises lasting happiness!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read the following description of a serene garden (*वाटिका*) and school setting.

### **वाटिकायाम् (In the Garden)**

**एषा वाटिका।** वाटिका सुन्दरी अस्ति।
> *Eṣā vāṭikā. Vāṭikā sundarī asti.*
> **This is a garden.** The garden is beautiful.

**अत्र एका बालिका अस्ति।** तस्याः नाम राधा। राधा हसति। सा खेलति।
> *Atra ekā bālikā asti. Tasyāḥ nāma Rādhā. Rādhā hasati. Sā khelati.*
> **Here is a girl.** Her name is Radha. Radha smiles. She plays.

**एषा अध्यापिका।** अध्यापिका आगच्छति। सा पुस्तकम् पठति, लेखन्या लिखति च।
> *Eṣā adhyāpikā. Adhyāpikā āgacchati. Sā pustakam paṭhati, lekhanyā likhati ca.*
> **This is a female teacher.** The teacher comes. She reads a book and writes with a pen.

**तत् किम्? तत् एकम् फलम् अस्ति।**
> *Tat kim? Tat ekam phalam asti.*
> **What is that? That is a fruit.**

**वृक्षात् फलम् पतति।** तत्र पुष्पम् विकसति। पुष्पम् सुन्दरम् अस्ति।
> *Vṛkṣāt phalam patati. Tatra puṣpam vikasati. Puṣpam sundaram asti.*
> **A fruit falls from the tree.** A flower blooms there. The flower is beautiful.

## 🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्: प्रकोष्ठ-प्रदर्शनम् (Room Tour & Desk Setup Vlog)

Use this complete speaking script when filming a room tour or studio setup in Sanskrit:

### 1. Studio / Room Tour Dialogue Script

> **"भो मित्राणि! पश्यन्तु, एषः मम कार्य-प्रकोष्ठः (my workspace / room)!"**
> *Bho mitrāṇi! Paśyantu, eṣaḥ mama kārya-prakoṣṭhaḥ!*
> **"Hey friends! Look, this is my workspace / studio!"**

> **"एषा मम उत्पीठिका (table), इदम् च मम आसनम् (chair)।"**
> *Eṣā mama utpīṭhikā, idam ca mama āsanam.*
> **"This is my table, and this is my chair."**

> **"उत्पीठिकायाम् इदम् मम सङ्गणकम् (laptop) अस्ति। एषा मम जल-कूपी (water bottle)।"**
> *Utpīṭhikāyām idam mama saṅgaṇakam asti. Eṣā mama jala-kūpī.*
> **"On the table is my laptop. This is my water bottle."**

> **"इदम् मम चलचित्राङ्कन-यन्त्रम् (camera)! एतेन अहम् दृश्यवृत्तान्तं रचयामि!"**
> *Idam mama calacitrāṅkana-yantram! Etena aham dṛśyavṛttāntaṃ racayāmi!*
> **"This is my camera! With this I create vlogs!"**

> **"कथम् अस्ति मम प्रकोष्ठः? कृपया टिप्पणी-भागे (in comments) वदन्तु!"**
> *Katham asti mama prakoṣṭhaḥ? Kṛpayā ṭippaṇī-bhāge vadantu!*
> **"How is my room? Please tell in the comment section!"**

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Prose Order)

1. **वाटिका सुन्दरी अस्ति (vāṭikā sundarī asti)**
   * *Padavibhāga*: वाटिका (Garden [Fem.]) $+$ सुन्दरी (Beautiful [Fem.]) $+$ अस्ति (is)
   * *Grammar Rule*: An adjective matches the gender of its noun. Since *vāṭikā* is feminine, the feminine form *sundarī* is used instead of *sundaraḥ* (masc.) or *sundaram* (neut.).

2. **सा पुस्तकम् पठति, लेखन्या लिखति च (sā pustakam paṭhati, lekhanyā likhati ca)**
   * *Padavibhāga*: सा (She) $+$ पुस्तकम् (Book) $+$ पठति (reads) $+$ लेखन्या (with pen) $+$ लिखति (writes) $+$ च (and)
   * *Rule on 'च' (ca)*: In Sanskrit, **च (ca)** means "and" and is placed *after* the words/clauses it joins.

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Demonstrative & Interrogative Master Grid)

| Gender | "This" (Nearby) | "That" (Distant) | "Who / What?" |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masculine** | **एषः** (eṣaḥ - this male) | **सः** (saḥ - that male) | **कः** (kaḥ - who?) |
| **Feminine** | **एषा** (eṣā - this female) | **सा** (sā - that female) | **का** (kā - who?) |
| **Neuter** | **एतत्** (etat - this object) | **तत्** (tat - that object) | **किम्** (kim - what?) |

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **एषा** बालिका हसति।
2. **एतत्** सङ्गणकम् अस्ति।
3. **एषा** घटी अस्ति।
4. **एतत्** पुष्पम् विकसति।
5. **एतत्** आसनम् अस्ति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **पश्यन्तु, एषा मम उत्पीठिका, इदम् च मम आसनम्।** (*Paśyantu, eṣā mama utpīṭhikā, idam ca mama āsanam.*)
2. **इदम् मम चलचित्राङ्कन-यन्त्रम्!** (*Idam mama calacitrāṅkana-yantram!*)
3. **मम जलकूपी कुत्र अस्ति? $\rightarrow$ सा उत्पीठिकायाम् अस्ति।** (*Mama jalakūpī kutra asti? $\rightarrow$ Sā utpīṭhikāyām asti.*)
4. **मम प्रकोष्ठः कथम् अस्ति? कृपया टिप्पणीं लिखन्तु!** (*Mama prakoṣṭhaḥ katham asti? Kṛpayā ṭippaṇīṃ likhantu!*)
