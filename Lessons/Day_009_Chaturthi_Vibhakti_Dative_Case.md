# Day 009: चतुर्थी विभक्तिः (Dative Case - Giving, Purposes & Daily Preferences)

Welcome to Day 9! Today, we explore **चतुर्थी विभक्तिः (Caturthī Vibhakti - Dative Case)**. In Sanskrit grammar, this case marks the **सम्प्रदान-कारकम् (Sampradāna Kāraka)**—the recipient of giving ("*to whom something is given*"), the ultimate purpose of an action ("*for what purpose*"), salutations (**नमः**), and expressing personal likes/preferences (**रोचते**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> पिबन्ति नद्यः स्वयमेव नाम्भः स्वयं न खादन्ति फलानि वृक्षाः ।
> नादन्ति सस्यं खलु वारिवाहाः परोपकाराय सतां विभूतयः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *pibanti nadyaḥ svayam eva nāmbhaḥ svayaṃ na khādanti phalāni vṛkṣāḥ |*
> *nādanti sasyaṃ khalu vārivāhāḥ paropakārāya satāṃ vibhūtayaḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> Rivers do not drink their own water; trees do not eat their own fruits; clouds do not consume the harvest they water. Truly, the wealth and talents of the noble are solely **for the benefit of others** (*paropakārāya*)!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about charity, gifts, and temple worship in a community:

### **दानम् सत्कारः च (Generosity & Honor)**

**धनिकः निर्धनाय **धनाय** (money), वस्त्रम् च **ददाति**।**
> *Dhanikaḥ nirdhanāya dhanam, vastram ca dadāti.*
> **The wealthy person gives money and clothing to the poor.**

**माता पुत्राय **दुग्धम्**, बालिकायै च **मिष्टान्नम्** (sweets) यच्छति।**
> *Mātā putrāya dugdham, bālikāyai ca miṣṭānnam yacchati.*
> **Mother gives milk to the son, and sweets to the girl.**

**शिष्यः ज्ञानाय **विद्यालये** पठति, आरोग्याय च **व्यायामम्** करोति।**
> *Śiṣyaḥ jñānāya vidyālaye paṭhati, ārogyāya ca vyāyāmam karoti.*
> **The student studies in school for knowledge, and exercises for health.**

**भक्ताः गणेशाय **मोदकम्** समर्पयन्ति। श्रीगणेशाय **नमः**!**
> *Bhaktāḥ Gaṇeśāya modakam samarpayanti. Śrī-Gaṇeśāya namaḥ!*
> **Devotees offer sweet dumplings to Ganesha. Salutations unto Lord Ganesha!**

**बालकाय **क्रीडनम्** (playing) रोचते, मह्यम् तु **संस्कृत-पठनम्** रोचते।**
> *Bālakāya krīḍanam rocate, mahyam tu saṃskṛta-paṭhanam rocate.*
> **The boy likes playing, but I like studying Sanskrit.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम् (Daily Purposes, Gifts & Preferences)

In daily conversations, we use the Dative Case (*Caturthī*) to explain *why* we are doing something, give gifts, and express what food or activities we like!

### 1. Stating Purpose of Daily Actions (किमर्थम्?)

| Daily Action | Sanskrit Purpose Phrase (Caturthī) | IAST | Example Spoken Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **For Health** | **आरोग्याय / स्वास्थ्याय** | *ārogyāya / svāsthyāya* | अहम् **स्वास्थ्याय** योगाभ्यासं करोमि। (I do yoga for health.) |
| **For Studying** | **पठनाय / ज्ञानाय** | *paṭhanāya / jñānāya* | छात्राः **पठनाय** ग्रन्थालयम् गच्छन्ति। (Students go to library for study.) |
| **For Food** | **भोजनाय** | *bhojanāya* | वयम् **भोजनाय** उपहार-गृहं (restaurant) गच्छामः। (We go to restaurant for food.) |
| **For Conversation** | **सम्भाषणाय** | *sambhāṣaṇāya* | अहम् मित्रेण सह **सम्भाषणाय** आगच्छामि। (I come to converse with friend.) |
| **For Purchasing** | **क्रयणाय** | *krayaṇāya* | माता वस्तु **क्रयणाय** आपणं गच्छति। (Mother goes to shop for buying.) |

---

### 2. Expressing Likes & Preferences (रुच् $\rightarrow$ रोचते)

In Sanskrit, to say "X likes Y", the person who likes takes **Caturthī (Dative)** and the liked object takes **Prathamā**:

| English Meaning | Sanskrit Spoken Formula | IAST Transliteration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I like sweets.** | **मह्यम्** मिष्टान्नम् रोचते। | *Mahyam miṣṭānnam rocate.* |
| **He likes tea.** | **तस्मै** चायम् रोचते। | *Tasmai cāyam rocate.* |
| **She likes reading.** | **तस्यै** पठनम् रोचते। | *Tasyai paṭhanam rocate.* |
| **What do you like?** | **भवते / भवत्यै** किम् रोचते? | *Bhavate / Bhavatyai kim rocate?* |

---

### 3. Conversational Mini-Dialogue: In a Restaurant / Cafe

* **प्रबन्धकः (Server):** हरिः ओम्! भवते किम् रोचते?
  > *Hariḥ om! Bhavate kim rocate?*
  > *Hello! What would you like?*
* **ग्राहकः (Customer):** **मह्यम्** काफी (coffee) रोचते।
  > *Mahyam kāphī rocate.*
  > *I like coffee.*
* **प्रबन्धकः:** भवतः मित्राय किम् रोचते?
  > *Bhavataḥ mitrāya kim rocate?*
  > *What does your friend like?*
* **ग्राहकः:** **तस्मै** चायं, किञ्चित् मधुरं च रोचते।
  > *Tasmai cāyaṃ, kiñcit madhuraṃ ca rocate.*
  > *He likes tea and some sweets.*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **माता पुत्राय दुग्धम् ददाति (mātā putrāya dugdham dadāti)**
   * **माता**: Subject (Prathamā Sg. Fem.)
   * **पुत्राय**: Recipient of Giving (Caturthī Sg. Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-āya*)
   * **दुग्धम्**: Object Given (Dvitīyā Sg. Neut.)
   * **ददाति**: Verb (gives)

2. **मह्यम् संस्कृतम् रोचते (mahyam saṃskṛtam rocate)**
   * **मह्यम्**: Dative form of *aham* ("to me / I like").
   * *Rule*: The verb **रोचते (rocate)** strictly governs **Caturthī** on the person who experiences pleasure.

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Transform into Caturthī Vibhakti
1. पिता _________ (पुत्र) पुस्तकं ददाति।
2. अहम् _________ (आरोग्य) व्यायामं करोमि।
3. श्री _________ (गणेश) नमः।
4. _________ (अहम् $\rightarrow$ मह्यम्) फलानि रोचन्ते।
5. भक्ताः _________ (माता) फलानि समर्पयन्ति।

### Exercise 2: Translate into Sanskrit for Spoken Practice
1. I like tea.
2. She gives a book to her sister.
3. We go to school for knowledge.
4. What do you like to eat?

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. पिता **पुत्राय** पुस्तकं ददाति।
2. अहम् **आरोग्याय** व्यायामं करोमि।
3. श्री **गणेशाय** नमः।
4. **मह्यम्** फलानि रोचन्ते।
5. भक्ताः **मात्रे (मात्रायै)** फलानि समर्पयन्ति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **मह्यम् चायं रोचते।** (*Mahyam cāyaṃ rocate.*)
2. **सा भगिन्यै पुस्तकं ददाति।** (*Sā bhaginyai pustakaṃ dadāti.*)
3. **वयम् ज्ञानाय विद्यालयं गच्छामः।** (*Vayam jñānāya vidyālayaṃ gacchāmaḥ.*)
4. **भवते खादनाय किम् रोचते?** (*Bhavate khādanāya kim rocate?*)
