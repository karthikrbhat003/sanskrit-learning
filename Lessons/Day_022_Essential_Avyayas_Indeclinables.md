# Day 022: अव्ययानि (Indeclinables & Fluent Conversational Connectors)

Welcome to Day 22! Today, we explore **अव्ययानि (Avyayas - Indeclinable Words)**. In Sanskrit, an Avyaya **never changes its form** regardless of gender, number, or case (*सदृशं त्रिषु लिङ्गेषु...*). 

Mastering these words is the secret to moving from robotic isolated sentences to **fluid, natural, continuous everyday conversation**.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This classic grammatical verse defines what an Avyaya is:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> सदृशं त्रिषु लिङ्गेषु सर्वासु च विभक्तिषु ।
> वचनेषु च सर्वेषु यन्न व्येति तदव्ययम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *sadṛśaṃ triṣu liṅgeṣu sarvāsu ca vibhaktiṣu |*
> *vacaneṣu ca sarveṣu yan na vyeti tad avyayam ||*

**English Translation:**
> That which remains identical across all **three genders**, throughout all **seven cases**, and across all **three numbers**, and never undergoes any change—that is called an **Avyaya**!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about a rainy morning in the countryside (*वृष्टि-दिने जीवनम्*):

### **मेघ-गर्जनम् (The Thundering of Clouds)**

**अद्य प्रातःकाले सर्वत्र मेघाः सन्ति।**
> *Adya prātaḥkāle sarvatra meghāḥ santi.*
> **Today in the morning, clouds are everywhere.**

**यदा मेघः गर्जति, तदा मयूरः आनन्देन नृत्यति।**
> *Yadā meghaḥ garjati, tadā mayūraḥ ānandena nṛtyati.*
> **When the cloud thunders, then the peacock dances with joy.**

**यदि वृष्टिः भविष्यति, तर्हि कृषकः प्रसन्नः भविष्यति।**
> *Yadi vṛṣṭiḥ bhaviṣyati, tarhi kṛṣakaḥ prasannaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
> **If it rains, then the farmer will be delighted.**

**यथा माता पुत्रम् पालयति, तथैव पृथिवी सर्वान् पालयति।**
> *Yathā mātā putram pālayati, tathaiva pṛthivī sarvān pālayati.*
> **Just as a mother protects her son, so too the Earth sustains everyone.**

**अधुना वृष्टिः आरभते। बालकाः बहिः धावन्ति, आनन्देन च खेलन्ति।**
> *Adhunā vṛṣṭiḥ ārabhate. Bālakāḥ bahiḥ dhāvanti, ānandena ca khelanti.*
> **Now rain begins. Boys run outside and play joyously.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: संलाप-संयोजकाः (Daily Conversational Connectors)

In everyday speech, correlative pairs (*नित्य-सम्बन्ध-अव्ययानि*) connect your thoughts seamlessly:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Correlative Pairs

| Correlative Pair | Meaning | Example in Everyday Dialogue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **यदि ... तर्हि ...** | If ... then ... | **यदि** समयः अस्ति, **तर्हि** आगच्छतु। (If you have time, then please come.) |
| **यदा ... तदा ...** | When ... then ... | **यदा** कार्यं समाप्यते, **तदा** वयं मेलिष्यामः। (When work finishes, then we will meet.) |
| **यथा ... तथा ...** | As / Just as ... so ... | **यथा** भवान् इच्छति, **तथा** भवतु। (As you wish, so let it be!) |
| **यत्र ... तत्र ...** | Where ... there ... | **यत्र** वृष्टिः अस्ति, **तत्र** छत्रम् (umbrella) आवश्यकम्। (Where there is rain, there an umbrella is needed.) |
| **यावत् ... तावत् ...** | As long as / Until ... till then | **यावत्** अहम् आगच्छामि, **तावत्** उपविशतु। (Until I arrive, please sit here.) |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Making Conditional Plans

* **सतीशः (Satish):** भो मित्र! किम् भवान् अद्य सायं आपणं गमिष्यति?
  > *Bho mitra! Kim bhavān adya sāyaṃ āpaṇaṃ gamiṣyati?*
  > *Friend! Will you go to the market today evening?*
* **प्रकाशः (Prakash):** **यदि** वृष्टिः न भविष्यति, **तर्हि** अहम् गमिष्यामि।
  > *Yadi vṛṣṭiḥ na bhaviṣyati, tarhi aham gamiṣyāmi.*
  > *If it does not rain, then I will go.*
* **सतीशः:** **यदा** भवान् प्रस्थितः भवति (set out), **तदा** माम् आह्वयतु (call me)।
  > *Yadā bhavān prasthitaḥ bhavati, tadā mām āhvayatu.*
  > *When you set out, then please call me.*
* **प्रकाशः:** अवश्यम्, **यथा** भवान् वदति, **तथा** करिष्यामि!
  > *Avaśyam, yathā bhavān vadati, tathā kariṣyāmi!*
  > *Certainly, as you say, so I will do!*

## 🗂️ The Comprehensive Master Avyaya Catalog

### 1. Logic & Relation Connectors
* **च** (*ca* = and) | **अपि** (*api* = also/too) | **एव** (*eva* = only/indeed)
* **तु / किन्तु** (*tu / kintu* = but/however) | **इति** (*iti* = quotation end marker)

### 2. Time (कालवाचकाव्ययानि)
* **अद्य** (*adya* = today) | **ह्यः** (*hyaḥ* = yesterday) | **श्वः** (*śvaḥ* = tomorrow)
* **परश्वः** (*paraśvaḥ* = day after tomorrow) | **अधुना / इदानीम्** (*adhunā/idānīm* = now)
* **सर्वदा / सदा** (*sarvadā/sadā* = always) | **कदापि** (*kadāpi* = ever / *na kadāpi = never*)

### 3. Space & Direction (स्थानवाचकाव्ययानि)
* **अत्र** (*atra* = here) | **तत्र** (*tatra* = there) | **कुत्र** (*kutra* = where?)
* **सर्वत्र** (*sarvatra* = everywhere) | **अन्यत्र** (*anyatra* = elsewhere)
* **एकत्र** (*ekatra* = in one place / together) | **बहिः** (*bahiḥ* = outside) | **अन्तः** (*antaḥ* = inside)

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Fill in the matching correlative pair
1. _________ (यदि / यदा) त्वं सत्यं वदसि, _________ (तर्हि / तदा) अहम् विश्वसिमि।
2. _________ (यथा / यत्र) राजा, _________ (तथा / तत्र) प्रजा।
3. _________ (यदा / यदि) सूर्यः उदेति, _________ (तदा / तर्हि) अन्धकारः नश्यति।
4. _________ (यत्र / यथा) धर्मः अस्ति, _________ (तत्र / तथा) जयः भवति।

### Exercise 2: Translate into Spoken Sanskrit
1. If you study, then you will pass.
2. When the teacher enters, then students stand up.
3. As you wish, so let it be!
4. God is everywhere.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **यदि** त्वं सत्यं वदसि, **तर्हि** अहम् विश्वसिमि।
2. **यथा** राजा, **तथा** प्रजा।
3. **यदा** सूर्यः उदेति, **तदा** अन्धकारः नश्यति।
4. **यत्र** धर्मः अस्ति, **तत्र** जयः भवति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **यदि त्वम् पठसि, तर्हि उत्तीर्णः भविष्यसि।** (*Yadi tvam paṭhasi, tarhi uttīrṇaḥ bhaviṣyasi.*)
2. **यदा शिक्षकः प्रविशति, तदा छात्राः उत्तिष्ठन्ति।** (*Yadā śikṣakaḥ praviśati, tadā chātrāḥ uttiṣṭhanti.*)
3. **यथा भवान् इच्छति, तथा भवतु!** (*Yathā bhavān icchati, tathā bhavatu!*)
4. **ईश्वरः सर्वत्र अस्ति।** (*Īśvaraḥ sarvatra asti.*)
