## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> सत्यं ब्रूयात् प्रियं ब्रूयात् न ब्रूयात् सत्यमप्रियम् ।
> प्रियं च नानृतं ब्रूयात् एष धर्मः सनातनः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *satyaṃ brūyāt priyaṃ brūyāt na brūyāt satyam apriyam |*
> *priyaṃ ca nānṛtaṃ brūyāt eṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> Speak the truth, speak what is kind; do not speak truth that is hurtful. Nor should one speak pleasant falsehoods; this is the eternal path of righteousness (*dharma*).

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read this conversation between Guru (teacher), Shishya (student), and fellow students:

### **गुरुकुले प्रभातम् (Morning in the Gurukula)**

**आचार्यः:** शिष्याः! प्रभात-वेला अस्ति। **यूयम् किम् कुरुथ?**
> *Ācāryaḥ: Śiṣyāḥ! Prabhāta-velā asti. Yūyam kim kurutha?*
> **Teacher:** Disciples! It is morning time. **What are you all doing?**

**शिष्यौ:** आचार्य! **आवाम् सूर्य-नमस्कारम् कुर्वः**, वेद-मन्त्राणि च **पठावः**।
> *Śiṣyau: Ācārya! Āvām sūrya-namaskāram kurvaḥ, veda-mantrāṇi ca paṭhāvaḥ.*
> **Two Disciples:** Teacher! **We two are doing Sun Salutations** and **reading Vedic hymns.**

**आचार्यः:** शोभनम्! राम! **त्वम् कुत्र गच्छसि?**
> *Ācāryaḥ: Śobhanam! Rāma! Tvam kutra gacchasi?*
> **Teacher:** Splendid! Rama! **Where are you going?**

**रामः:** गुरुदेव! **अहम् जलम् आनेतुम् नदीम् गच्छामि।**
> *Rāmaḥ: Gurudeva! Aham jalam ānetum nadīm gacchāmi.*
> **Rama:** Respected teacher! **I am going to the river to fetch water.**

**आचार्यः:** अन्ये बालकाः किम् कुर्वन्ति?
> *Ācāryaḥ: Anye bālakāḥ kim kurvanti?*
> **Teacher:** What are the other boys doing?

**रामः:** **ते वाटिकायाम् कार्यम् कुर्वन्ति**, पुष्पाणि च **सङ्गृह्णन्ति**।
> *Rāmaḥ: Te vāṭikāyām kāryam kurvanti, puṣpāṇi ca saṅgṛhṇanti.*
> **Rama:** **They are doing work in the garden** and **gathering flowers.**

**सर्वे शिष्याः:** **वयम् सर्वदा समयेन पठामः, अनुशासनम् पालयामः च।**
> *Sarve Śiṣyāḥ: Vayam sarvadā samayena paṭhāmaḥ, anuśāsanam pālayāmaḥ ca.*
> **All Disciples:** **We always study on time and observe discipline.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: मम दिनचर्या (My Daily Routine in Spoken Sanskrit)

Here is how you can describe your entire daily life schedule from morning to night in authentic spoken Sanskrit:

### 1. Daily Schedule Template (दिनचर्या-क्रमः)

| Time of Day | Sanskrit Action Sentence | IAST Transliteration | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **6:00 AM** | अहम् प्रातः षड्वादने **उत्तिष्ठामि**। | *Aham prātaḥ ṣaḍvādane uttiṣṭhāmi.* | I wake up at 6 o'clock in the morning. |
| **6:15 AM** | अहम् दन्तधावनम्, मुख-प्रक्षालनम् च **करोमि**। | *Aham dantadhāvanam, mukha-prakṣālanam ca karomi.* | I brush my teeth and wash my face. |
| **6:45 AM** | अहम् योगाभ्यासं **करोमि**, स्नानं च **करोमि**। | *Aham yogābhyāsaṃ karomi, snānaṃ ca karomi.* | I practice yoga and take a bath. |
| **7:30 AM** | अहम् अल्पाहारं (breakfast) **खादामि**, चायं **पिबामि**। | *Aham alpāhāraṃ khādāmi, cāyaṃ pibāmi.* | I eat breakfast and drink tea. |
| **8:30 AM** | अहम् कार्यालयं / विद्यालयं **गच्छामि**। | *Aham kāryālayaṃ / vidyālayaṃ gacchāmi.* | I go to the office / school. |
| **1:00 PM** | मध्याह्ने अहम् भोजनं **करोमि**। | *Madhyāhne aham bhojanaṃ karomi.* | At noon, I eat lunch. |
| **5:30 PM** | सायङ्काले अहम् गृहं **प्रत्यागच्छामि**। | *Sāyaṅkāle aham gṛhaṃ pratyāgacchāmi.* | In the evening, I return back home. |
| **7:00 PM** | अहम् मित्रेण सह सम्भाषणं **करोमि**। | *Aham mitreṇa saha sambhāṣaṇaṃ karomi.* | I converse with my friend. |
| **8:30 PM** | वयम् परिवारेण सह रात्रि-भोजनं **कुर्मः**। | *Vayam parivāreṇa saha rātri-bhojanaṃ kurmaḥ.* | We eat dinner together with family. |
| **10:00 PM** | अहम् दशवादने शयनं **करोमि**। | *Aham daśavādane śayanaṃ karomi.* | I go to sleep at 10 o'clock. |

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogue: Talking About Your Daily Schedule

* **आनन्दः (Anand):** भो मित्र! भवान् प्रातः कति वादने उत्तिष्ठति?
  > *Bho mitra! Bhavān prātaḥ kati vādane uttiṣṭhati?*
  > *Friend! At what time do you wake up in the morning?*
* **विकासः (Vikas):** अहम् प्रातः पञ्चवादने (at 5 AM) उत्तिष्ठामि। भवान् कदा उत्तिष्ठति?
  > *Aham prātaḥ pañcavādane uttiṣṭhāmi. Bhavān kadā uttiṣṭhati?*
  > *I wake up at 5 o'clock in the morning. When do you wake up?*
* **आनन्दः:** अहम् षड्वादने उत्तिष्ठामि। तदनन्तरं भवान् किं करोति?
  > *Aham ṣaḍvādane uttiṣṭhāmi. Tadanantaraṃ bhavān kiṃ karoti?*
  > *I wake up at 6 o'clock. What do you do after that?*
* **विकासः:** अहम् भ्रमनाय (for a walk) उद्यानं गच्छामि, ततः पठामि।
  > *Aham bhramanāya udyānaṃ gacchāmi, tataḥ paṭhāmi.*
  > *I go to the park for a walk, and then I study.*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Analysis)

1. **आवाम् वेद-मन्त्राणि पठावः (āvām veda-mantrāṇi paṭhāvaḥ)**
   * *Subject*: आवाम् (*We two* - 1st Person Dual)
   * *Verb*: पठावः (*We two read* - Dual ending **-आवः / -āvaḥ**)

2. **त्वम् कुत्र गच्छसि? (tvam kutra gacchasi?)**
   * *Subject*: त्वम् (*You* - 2nd Person Singular)
   * *Verb*: गच्छसि (*You go* - Singular ending **-सि / -si**)

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (The Complete 3x3 Present Tense Matrix)

Every regular Sanskrit verb root follows this $3 \times 3$ grid in the present tense (*Laṭ Lakāra*):

$$\begin{array}{|l|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Person (पुरुषः)} & \textbf{Singular (एकवचनम्)} & \textbf{Dual (द्विवचनम्)} & \textbf{Plural (बहुवचनम्)} \\
\hline
\textbf{3rd Person (प्रथमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{-ति} \text{ (-ti)} & \textbf{-तः} \text{ (-taḥ)} & \textbf{-न्ति} \text{ (-nti)} \\
\text{(He / She / It / They)} & \text{पठति (reads)} & \text{पठतः (two read)} & \text{पठन्ति (they read)} \\
\hline
\textbf{2nd Person (मध्यमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{-सि} \text{ (-si)} & \textbf{-थः} \text{ (-thaḥ)} & \textbf{-थ} \text{ (-tha)} \\
\text{(You / You all)} & \text{पठसि (you read)} & \text{पठथः (you two read)} & \text{पठथ (you all read)} \\
\hline
\textbf{1st Person (उत्तमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{-आमि} \text{ (-āmi)} & \textbf{-आवः} \text{ (-āvaḥ)} & \textbf{-आमः} \text{ (-āmaḥ)} \\
\text{(I / We)} & \text{पठामि (I read)} & \text{पठावः (we two read)} & \text{पठामः (we all read)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. अहम् प्रातः **उत्तिष्ठामि**।
2. अहम् दुग्धम् **पिबामि**।
3. वयम् विद्यालये **पठामः**।
4. युवाम् कुत्र **गच्छथः**?

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **अहम् प्रातः षड्वादने उत्तिष्ठामि।** (*Aham prātaḥ ṣaḍvādane uttiṣṭhāmi.*)
2. **वयम् प्रातःकाले चायं पिबामः।** (*Vayam prātaḥkāle cāyaṃ pibāmaḥ.*)
3. **भवान् अधुना कुत्र गच्छति?** (*Bhavān adhunā kutra gacchati?*)
4. **ते सायङ्काले क्रीडन्ति।** (*Te sāyaṅkāle krīḍanti.*)
