# Day 024: सन्धि-प्रकरणम् (Foundational Sandhi Rules - Sound Junctions)

Welcome to Day 24! In Sanskrit, when two sounds meet at word boundaries, they naturally blend together for smooth, musical pronunciation. This phonetic junction is called **सन्धिः (Sandhi)**.

Understanding Sandhi is the secret key that unlocks the ability to split compound phrases (*Padaccheda*) and read classical verses fluently.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

Notice the famous Sandhi **वसुधा $+$ एव $=$ वसुधैव (vasudhaiva)** in this verse:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> अयं निजः परो वेति गणना लघुचेतसाम् ।
> उदारचरितानां तु **वसुधैव** कुटुम्बकम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *ayaṃ nijaḥ paro veti gaṇanā laghucetasām |*
> *udāracaritānāṃ tu **vasudhaiva** kuṭumbakam ||*

**English Translation:**
> "This person is mine, and that one is a stranger"—such calculation is of the narrow-minded. For the noble-hearted, the entire Earth **is verily** (*vasudhā + eva = vasudhaiva*) one single family!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read this dialogue at a school celebration containing live Sandhi examples:

### **विद्यालये महोत्सवे स्वागतम् (Welcome to the School Festival)**

**अद्य विद्यालये (विद्या + आलये) महान् उत्सवः अस्ति।**
> *Adya vidyālaye mahān utsavaḥ asti.*
> **Today in the school, there is a grand celebration.**

**आचार्यः वदति—"भोः सर्वेभ्यः **स्वागतम्** (सु + आगतम्)!"**
> *Ācāryaḥ vadati—"Bhoḥ sarvebhyaḥ svāgatam!"*
> **The teacher says—"Welcome to everyone!"**

**अत्र **कवीन्द्राः** (कवि + इन्द्राः), **महर्षयः** (महा + ऋषयः) च समागताः।**
> *Atra kavīndrāḥ, maharṣayaḥ ca samāgatāḥ.*
> **Here, chief poets and great sages have assembled.**

****यद्यपि** (यदि + अपि) सूर्यस्य तापः अस्ति, **तथापि** (तथा + अपि) जनाः आनन्दिताः सन्ति।**
> *Yadyapi sūryasya tāpaḥ asti, tathāpi janāḥ ānanditāḥ santi.*
> **Even though there is heat of the sun, nevertheless people are delighted.**

**सर्वे **सदैव** (सदा + एव) विद्या-प्राप्तये यत्नम् कुर्वन्तु।**
> *Sarve sadaiva vidyā-prāptaye yatnam kurvantu.*
> **May everyone always strive for the attainment of knowledge.**

## 💡 The 4 Essential Vowel Sandhis (स्वर-सन्धयः)

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Sandhi Type} & \textbf{Rule Formula} & \textbf{Result} & \textbf{Classic Examples} \\
\hline
\textbf{1. सवर्ण-दीर्घ} & \text{Short/Long Vowel} + \text{Same Vowel} & \text{Long Vowel} & \text{हिम} + \text{आलयः} = \textbf{हिमालयः} \\
\text{(Lengthening)} & a/ā + a/ā \rightarrow \mathbf{\bar{a}} & \mathbf{\bar{a}, \bar{i}, \bar{u}} & \text{कवि} + \text{इन्द्रः} = \textbf{कवीन्द्रः} \\
& i/\bar{i} + i/\bar{i} \rightarrow \mathbf{\bar{i}} & & \text{भानु} + \text{उदयः} = \textbf{भानूदयः} \\
\hline
\textbf{2. गुण-सन्धिः} & a/\bar{a} + i/\bar{i} \rightarrow \mathbf{e} & \mathbf{e, o, ar} & \text{गण} + \text{ईशः} = \textbf{गणेशः} \\
\text{(Guṇa - e, o, ar)} & a/\bar{a} + u/\bar{u} \rightarrow \mathbf{o} & & \text{सूर्य} + \text{उदयः} = \textbf{सूर्योदयः} \\
& a/\bar{a} + \text{ṛ} \rightarrow \mathbf{ar} & & \text{महा} + \text{ऋषिः} = \textbf{महर्षिः} \\
\hline
\textbf{3. वृद्धि-सन्धिः} & a/\bar{a} + e/ai \rightarrow \mathbf{ai} & \mathbf{ai, au} & \text{एक} + \text{एकम्} = \textbf{एकैकम्} \\
\text{(Vṛddhi - ai, au)}& a/\bar{a} + o/au \rightarrow \mathbf{au} & & \text{सदा} + \text{एव} = \textbf{सदैव} \\
& & & \text{वसुधा} + \text{एव} = \textbf{वसुधैव} \\
\hline
\textbf{4. यण्-सन्धिः} & i/\bar{i} + \text{different vowel} \rightarrow \mathbf{y} & \mathbf{y, v, r} & \text{यदि} + \text{अपि} = \textbf{यद्यपि} \\
\text{(Semi-vowel)} & u/\bar{u} + \text{different vowel} \rightarrow \mathbf{v} & & \text{इति} + \text{आदि} = \textbf{इत्यादि} \\
& & & \text{सु} + \text{आगतम्} = \textbf{स्वागतम्} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

## 🔍 How to Join and Split Sandhi (सन्धि-विच्छेदः)

### Joining (सन्धि-करणम्):
* देव $+$ आलयः $\rightarrow$ **देवालयः** (अ $+$ आ $=$ आ $\rightarrow$ Dīrgha)
* महा $+$ उत्सवः $\rightarrow$ **महोत्सवः** (आ $+$ उ $=$ ओ $\rightarrow$ Guṇa)
* प्रति $+$ एकम् $\rightarrow$ **प्रत्येकम्** (इ $+$ ए $=$ ये $\rightarrow$ Yaṇ)

### Splitting (सन्धि-विच्छेदः):
* **महेशः** $\rightarrow$ महा $+$ ईशः (Guṇa)
* **सदैव** $\rightarrow$ सदा $+$ एव (Vṛddhi)
* **यद्यपि** $\rightarrow$ यदि $+$ अपि (Yaṇ)
* **विद्यार्थी** $\rightarrow$ विद्या $+$ अर्थी (Dīrgha)

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. हिम $+$ आलयः $=$ **हिमालयः** (*Himālayaḥ* - Dīrgha)
2. सूर्य $+$ उदयः $=$ **सूर्योदयः** (*Sūryodayaḥ* - Guṇa)
3. सदा $+$ एव $=$ **सदैव** (*Sadaiva* - Vṛddhi)
4. सु $+$ आगतम् $=$ **स्वागतम्** (*Svāgatam* - Yaṇ)
5. इति $+$ आदि $=$ **इत्यादि** (*Ityādi* - Yaṇ)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **गणेशः** $=$ **गण $+$ ईशः**
2. **वसुधैव** $=$ **वसुधा $+$ एव**
3. **कवीन्द्रः** $=$ **कवि $+$ इन्द्रः**
4. **प्रत्येकम्** $=$ **प्रति $+$ एकम्**
