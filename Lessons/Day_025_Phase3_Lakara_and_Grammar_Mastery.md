## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This verse is the famous moral of today's story:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> सुहृदां हित-कामानां न शृणोतीह यो वचः ।
> स कूर्म इव दुर्बुद्धिः काष्ठाद् भ्रष्टो विनश्यति ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *suhṛdāṃ hita-kāmānāṃ na śṛṇotīha yo vacaḥ |*
> *sa kūrma iva durbuddhiḥ kāṣṭhād bhraṣṭo vinaśyati ||*

**English Translation:**
> He who does not heed the well-wishing advice of true friends perishes like the foolish turtle that fell from the wooden stick!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Classic Panchatantra Prose)

### **हंस-कूर्म-कथा (The Swans and the Turtle)**

**मगध-देशे फुल्लोत्पल-नाम्नि सरोवरे सङ्कट-विकटौ नाम हंसौ निवसावः स्म।**
> *Magadha-deśe Phullotpala-nāmni sarovare Saṅkaṭa-Vikaṭau nāma haṃsau nivasataḥ sma.*
> In the land of Magadha, in a lake named Phullotpala, two swans named Sankata and Vikata used to live.

**तयोः मित्रम् कम्बुग्रीवः नाम एकः कूर्मः (कच्छपः) अपि तत्रैव अवसत्।**
> *Tayoḥ mitram Kambugrīvaḥ nāma ekaḥ kūrmaḥ (kacchapaḥ) api tatraiva avasat.*
> Their friend, a turtle named Kambugriva, also lived right there.

**एकदा ग्रीष्मकाले सरोवरे जलम् शुष्कम् अभवत्।**
> *Ekadā grīṣmakāle sarovare jalam śuṣkam abhavat.*
> Once during summer, the water in the lake dried up.

**हंसौ अवदताम्—"मित्र! अत्र जलम् नास्ति। आवाम् अन्यम् विशालम् सरोवरम् **गमिष्यावः** (Future)।"**
> *Haṃsau avadatām—"Mitra! Atra jalam nāsti. Āvām anyam viśālam sarovaram gamiṣyāvaḥ."*
> The two swans said—"Friend! There is no water here. We two **will go** to another large lake."

**कूर्मः अवदत्—"अहम् अपि भवद्भ्याम् सह **आगन्तुम्** इच्छामि। आवाम् किम् **कुर्याव**?"**
> *Kūrmaḥ avadat—"Aham api bhavadbhyām saha āgantum icchāmi. Āvām kim kuryāva?"*
> The turtle said—"I too wish to come along with you two. What **should we do**?"

**हंसौ एकम् काष्ठ-दण्डम् आनयताम्, अवदताम् च—**
> *Haṃsau ekam kāṣṭha-daṇḍam ānayatām, avadatām ca—*
> The two swans brought a wooden stick and said—

**"त्वम् मुखेन दण्डस्य मध्य-भागम् **धारय** (Imperative)! आवाम् आकाश-मार्गेण त्वाम् **नेष्यावः** (Future)।"**
> *"Tvam mukhena daṇḍasya madhya-bhāgam dhāraya! Āvām ākāśa-mārgeṇa tvām neṣyāvaḥ."*
> "**Hold** the middle part of the stick with your mouth! We two **will carry** you by the sky route."

**"किन्तु सावधान! मार्गे किमपि मा **वद**! मौनम् **तिष्ठ**!"**
> *"Kintu sāvadhāna! Mārge kim api mā vada! Maunam tiṣṭha!"*
> "But beware! **Do not speak** anything on the way! **Remain** silent!"

**यदा ते आकाशे अगच्छन्, तदा नगर-जनाः अपश्यन्, कोलाहलेन अवदन् च—"पश्यत! आश्चर्यम्! हंसाभ्याम् सह कूर्मः अपि उत्पतति!"**
> *Yadā te ākāśe agacchan, tadā nagara-janāḥ apaśyan, kolāhalena avadan ca—"Paśyata! Āścaryam! Haṃsābhyām saha kūrmaḥ api utpatati!"*
> When they were travelling in the sky, the townspeople saw and shouted with noise—"Look! A wonder! A turtle is flying with two swans!"

**एतत् श्रुत्वा क्रुद्धः कूर्मः अवदत्—"यूयम् भस्म **खादत**!"**
> *Etat śrutvā kruddhaḥ kūrmaḥ avadat—"Yūyam bhasma khādata!"*
> Hearing this, the enraged turtle shouted—"You all **eat** ashes!"

**वचन-समकालमेव सः दण्डात् भूतले अपतत्, विनष्टः च अभवत्।**
> *Vacana-samakālam eva saḥ daṇḍāt bhūtale apatat, vinaṣṭaḥ ca abhavat.*
> The very moment he spoke, he fell from the stick onto the ground and was destroyed.

## 💡 Master 4-Lakāra Cross-Comparison Matrix

Observe how the same verb root **पठ् (to study)** shifts across all major Lakāras in the 3rd Person:

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Lakāra (लकारः)} & \textbf{Tense / Mood} & \textbf{Singular (सः)} & \textbf{Plural (ते)} \\
\hline
\textbf{1. लट् (Laṭ)} & \text{Present Tense} & \textbf{पठति} \text{ (reads)} & \textbf{पठन्ति} \text{ (they read)} \\
\textbf{2. लङ् (Laṅ)} & \text{Past Tense} & \textbf{अपठत्} \text{ (read)} & \textbf{अपठन्} \text{ (they read)} \\
\textbf{3. लृट् (Lṛṭ)} & \text{Future Tense} & \textbf{पठिष्यति} \text{ (will read)} & \textbf{पठिष्यन्ति} \text{ (will read)} \\
\textbf{4. लोट् (Loṭ)} & \text{Imperative Mood} & \textbf{पठतु} \text{ (let him read!)} & \textbf{पठन्तु} \text{ (let them read!)} \\
\textbf{5. विधिलिङ् (Vidhi)}& \text{Potential Mood} & \textbf{पठेत्} \text{ (should read)} & \textbf{पठेयुः} \text{ (should read)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

## 🏆 Phase 3 Mastery Milestone Checklist

You have successfully mastered the complete verb extension system of Sanskrit!
- [x] **Laṭ Lakāra (Present)**: Express everyday current actions across all 3 persons and numbers.
- [x] **Laṅ Lakāra (Past)**: Narrate stories and describe past occurrences (*apaṭhat, agacchat, āsīt*).
- [x] **Lṛṭ Lakāra (Future)**: Formulate future plans and forecasts (*paṭhiṣyati, gamiṣyati*).
- [x] **Loṭ Lakāra (Imperative)**: Give polite commands, invitations, and blessings (*paṭhatu, bhavatu*).
- [x] **Vidhiliṅ Lakāra (Potential)**: Express advice and moral duty (*vadet, caret, kuryāt*).
- [x] **Avyayas**: Connect fluid clauses with *yathā-tathā, yadi-tarhi, yadā-tadā, adya, śvaḥ, hyaḥ*.
- [x] **Upasargas**: Recognize prefix shifts (*pra, apa, sam, anu, ā, ud, upa*).
- [x] **Sandhi**: Join and split foundational vowel combinations (*Dīrgha, Guṇa, Vṛddhi, Yaṇ*).

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **Present (लट्)**: सः **लिखति** | अहम् **लिखामि**
2. **Past (लङ्)**: सः **अलिखत्** | अहम् **अलिखम्**
3. **Future (लृट्)**: सः **लेखिष्यति** | अहम् **लेखिष्यामि**
4. **Imperative (लोट्)**: सः **लिखतु** | अहम् **लिखानि**

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **सरोवरस्य** जलम् शुष्कम् अभवत्।
2. हंसौ कूर्मम् **आकाश-मार्गेण** अनयताम्।
3. कूर्मः दण्डात् **भूतले** अपतत्।
