# Day 023: उपसर्गाः (Verbal Prefixes - Power Transformers of Sanskrit Roots)

Welcome to Day 23! In Sanskrit, **उपसर्गाः (Upasargas - Verbal Prefixes)** are 22 powerful prefixes that attach directly to the front of a verb root, radically altering, intensifying, or completely reversing its meaning.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This legendary verse demonstrates how a single root **हृ (hṛ - to take)** transforms into 5 completely distinct verbs when combined with different Upasargas:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> उपसर्गेण धात्वर्थो बलादन्यत्र नीयते ।
> प्रहाराहारसंहारविहारपरिहारवत् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *upasargeṇa dhātv-artho balād anyatra nīyate |*
> *prahārāhāra-saṃhāra-vihāra-parihāravat ||*

**English Translation:**
> By the power of an **Upasarga**, the meaning of a verb root is forcefully transported elsewhere—just as seen in:
> * **प्रहार** (*prahāra* = striking/hitting),
> * **आहार** (*āhāra* = eating/food),
> * **संहार** (*saṃhāra* = destruction),
> * **विहार** (*vihāra* = pleasure stroll/recreation), and
> * **परिहार** (*parihāra* = avoidance/remedy)!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about an inspiring morning at the hermitage (*आश्रमस्य दिनचर्या*):

### **आश्रमे अनुशासनम् (Discipline in the Ashram)**

**सूर्योदये शिष्याः शयनात् **उत्तिष्ठन्ति** (उत् + तिष्ठन्ति)।**
> *Sūryodaye śiṣyāḥ śayanāt uttiṣṭhanti.*
> **At sunrise, disciples wake up from sleep.**

**ते गुरुम् **प्रणमन्ति** (प्र + नमन्ति), गुरुकुलम् च **प्रविशन्ति** (प्र + विशन्ति)।**
> *Te gurum praṇamanti, gurukulam ca praviśanti.*
> **They bow deeply to the teacher and enter into the school.**

**गुरुः ज्ञानम् **उपदिशति** (उप + दिशति)। छात्राः सर्वम् **अवगच्छन्ति** (अव + गच्छन्ति)।**
> *Guruḥ jñānam upadiśati. Chātrāḥ sarvam avagacchanti.*
> **The teacher imparts instruction. The students understand everything thoroughly.**

**सत्पुरुषाः दीनानाम् **उपकुर्वन्ति** (उप + कुर्वन्ति), दुर्जनाः च **अपकुर्वन्ति** (अप + कुर्वन्ति)।**
> *Satpuruṣāḥ dīnānām upakurvanti, durjanāḥ ca apakurvanti.*
> **Noble people help the needy, while wicked people harm others.**

**सायङ्काले बालकाः उद्याने **विहरन्ति** (वि + हरन्ति), गृहम् च **प्रत्यागच्छन्ति** (प्रति + आ + गच्छन्ति)।**
> *Sāyaṅkāle bālakāḥ udyāne viharanti, gṛham ca pratyāgacchanti.*
> **In the evening, boys stroll in the park and return back home.**

## 🔍 The 22 Classical Sanskrit Upasargas (द्वाविंशतिः उपसर्गाः)

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Upasarga} & \textbf{Core Sense} & \textbf{Example Verb \& Meaning} \\
\hline
\textbf{प्र (pra)} & \text{Forward / Excellence / Intensity} & \text{प्रणमति (bows deeply), प्रविशति (enters)} \\
\textbf{परा (parā)} & \text{Away / Reverse / Defeat} & \text{पराजयते (is defeated)} \\
\textbf{अप (apa)} & \text{Away / Bad / Harm} & \text{अपकरोति (harms), अपहरति (steals/abducts)} \\
\textbf{सम् (sam)} & \text{Together / Thorough / Perfect} & \text{संस्करोति (purifies/refines), सङ्गच्छते (unites)} \\
\textbf{अनु (anu)} & \text{Following / After / Along} & \text{अनुगच्छति (follows), अनुकरोति (imitates)} \\
\textbf{अव (ava)} & \text{Down / Deep understanding} & \text{अवगच्छति (understands), अवतरति (descends)} \\
\textbf{निर् (nir)} & \text{Out / Without / Free from} & \text{निर्गच्छति (exits / goes out)} \\
\textbf{दुर् (dur)} & \text{Difficult / Bad / Trouble} & \text{दुरवगच्छति (difficult to comprehend)} \\
\textbf{वि (vi)} & \text{Special / Separation / Distinct} & \text{विहरति (strolls/recreates), विस्मयते (is amazed)} \\
\textbf{आ / आङ् (ā)} & \text{Hither / Towards (Reverses motion)}& \text{आगच्छति (comes - opp. of gacchati), आनयति (brings)} \\
\textbf{नि (ni)} & \text{Down / In / Into} & \text{निवसति (resides), निपतति (falls down)} \\
\textbf{अधि (adhi)} & \text{Above / Over / Master} & \text{अधिगच्छति (attains/masters)} \\
\textbf{अति (ati)} & \text{Excess / Beyond} & \text{अतिक्रामति (transgresses / exceeds)} \\
\textbf{सु (su)} & \text{Good / Well / Easily} & \text{सुशोभते (shines gracefully)} \\
\textbf{उत् / उद् (ud)}& \text{Up / Upwards / High} & \text{उत्तिष्ठति (stands up / rises), उत्पतति (flies up)} \\
\textbf{अभि (abhi)} & \text{Towards / In front of / Facing} & \text{अभिनन्दति (greets / welcomes)} \\
\textbf{प्रति (prati)} & \text{Back / Towards / In return} & \text{प्रतिवदति (replies), प्रत्यागच्छति (returns)} \\
\textbf{परि (pari)} & \text{Around / All sides / Complete} & \text{परिवर्तते (transforms), परिहरति (avoids)} \\
\textbf{उप (upa)} & \text{Near / Subordinate / Service} & \text{उपविशति (sits down), उपकरोति (helps)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

## 💡 Case Study: Transforming the Root **गम् (to go)**

Look at how one simple root **गच्छति (goes)** changes with different Upasargas:

* **गच्छति** (*gacchati*) $\rightarrow$ goes
* **आ $+$ गच्छति $=$ आगच्छति** (*āgacchati*) $\rightarrow$ **comes** (*motion reversed!*)
* **अनु $+$ गच्छति $=$ अनुगच्छति** (*anugacchati*) $\rightarrow$ **follows** (*goes after*)
* **अव $+$ गच्छति $=$ अवगच्छति** (*avagacchati*) $\rightarrow$ **understands** (*mental grasping*)
* **निर् $+$ गच्छति $=$ निर्गच्छति** (*nirgacchati*) $\rightarrow$ **exits / goes out**
* **प्रति $+$ आ $+$ गच्छति $=$ प्रत्यागच्छति** (*pratyāgacchati*) $\rightarrow$ **returns back**

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Identify the Upasarga in each verb
1. **प्रविशति** $\rightarrow$ Upasarga: _________
2. **उपविशति** $\rightarrow$ Upasarga: _________
3. **अनुकरोति** $\rightarrow$ Upasarga: _________
4. **उत्तिष्ठति** $\rightarrow$ Upasarga: _________
5. **आहरति** $\rightarrow$ Upasarga: _________

### Exercise 2: Fill in the blank with the appropriate prefixed verb
1. शिक्षकः कक्षायाम् _________ (enters $\rightarrow$ प्रविशति / निर्गच्छति)।
2. छात्रः आसने _________ (sits down $\rightarrow$ उपविशति / उत्तिष्ठति)।
3. सज्जनः दीनानाम् _________ (helps $\rightarrow$ उपकरोति / अपकरोति)।
4. शिष्यः गुरुम् _________ (bows respectfully $\rightarrow$ प्रणमति)।

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **प्र** (pra) $+$ विशति
2. **उप** (upa) $+$ विशति
3. **अनु** (anu) $+$ करोति
4. **उत् / उद्** (ud) $+$ तिष्ठति
5. **आ** (ā) $+$ हरति

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. शिक्षकः कक्षायाम् **प्रविशति**।
2. छात्रः आसने **उपविशति**।
3. सज्जनः दीनानाम् **उपकरोति**।
4. शिष्यः गुरुम् **प्रणमति**।
