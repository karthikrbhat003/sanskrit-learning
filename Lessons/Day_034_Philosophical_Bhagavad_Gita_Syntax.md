# Day 034: श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता-प्रवेशः (Bhagavad Gita: Philosophical Syntactic Analysis)

Welcome to Day 34! Today, we study the crown jewel of Sanskrit philosophical literature: the **श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता (Śrīmad Bhagavad Gītā)**.

By applying all the grammatical tools you have mastered—**Noun Cases, Lakāras, Sandhi, Samāsa, and Anvaya**—you will directly read and parse two of the most celebrated verses in world philosophy.

## 🌟 Verse 1: The Principle of Selfless Action (कर्मयोगः - BG 2.47)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
> मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana |*
> *mā karmaphalahetur bhūr mā te saṅgo 'stv akarmaṇi ||*

---

### 🔍 Complete Anatomical Dissection:

#### 1. पदच्छेदः (Word-by-Word Segmentation):
$$\text{कर्मणि} + \text{एव} + \text{अधिकारः} + \text{ते} + \text{मा} + \text{फलेषु} + \text{कदाचन} \mid$$
$$\text{मा} + \text{कर्म-फल-हेतुः} + \text{भूः} + \text{मा} + \text{ते} + \text{सङ्गः} + \text{अस्तु} + \text{अकर्मणि} \parallel$$

#### 2. व्याकरण-विवरणम् (Grammar Analysis of Every Word):
* **कर्मणि** $\rightarrow$ *Saptamī Sg. Neut.* = In action / duty
* **एव** $\rightarrow$ *Avyaya* = alone / only
* **अधिकारः** $\rightarrow$ *Prathamā Sg. Masc.* = rightful claim / entitlement
* **ते** $\rightarrow$ *Ṣaṣṭhī Sg. (तव)* = your / of you
* **मा** $\rightarrow$ *Prohibitive Avyaya* = never / let not
* **फलेषु** $\rightarrow$ *Saptamī Pl. Neut.* = in the fruits / results
* **कदाचन** $\rightarrow$ *Avyaya* = at any time / ever
* **कर्म-फल-हेतुः** $\rightarrow$ *Samāsa (Bahuvrīhi/Tatpuruṣa)* = motivated by the fruits of action
* **भूः** $\rightarrow$ *Aorist/Vidhiliṅ form of भू* = let you become (*मा भूः = do not become!*)
* **सङ्गः** $\rightarrow$ *Prathamā Sg. Masc.* = attachment / clinging
* **अस्तु** $\rightarrow$ *Loṭ Lakāra 3rd Sg. of अस्* = let there be
* **अकर्मणि** $\rightarrow$ *Saptamī Sg. Neut.* = in inaction / lethargy

#### 3. अन्वयः & Meaning:
> *ते अधिकारः कर्मणि एव (अस्ति), फलेषु कदाचन मा (अस्तु)। त्वं कर्म-फल-हेतुः मा भूः, अकर्मणि ते सङ्गः मा अस्तु।*
> **"Your rightful entitlement is to the action alone, never to its fruits. Let not the fruits of action be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction!"**

## 🌟 Verse 2: The Cosmic Promise of Incarnation (अवतार-प्रतिज्ञा - BG 4.7)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।
> अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānir bhavati bhārata |*
> *abhyutthānam adharmasya tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmy aham ||*

---

### 🔍 Complete Anatomical Dissection:

#### 1. पदच्छेदः (Word Segmentation):
$$\text{यदा} + \text{यदा} + \text{हि} + \text{धर्मस्य} + \text{ग्लानिः} + \text{भवति} + \text{भारत} \mid$$
$$\text{अभ्युत्थानम्} + \text{अधर्मस्य} + \text{तदा} + \text{आत्मानम्} + \text{सृजामि} + \text{अहम्} \parallel$$

#### 2. व्याकरण-विवरणम् (Grammar Analysis):
* **यदा यदा** $\rightarrow$ *Correlative Avyaya* = whenever / wherever
* **हि** $\rightarrow$ *Avyaya* = indeed / surely
* **धर्मस्य** $\rightarrow$ *Ṣaṣṭhī Sg. Masc.* = of righteousness / cosmic order
* **ग्लानिः** $\rightarrow$ *Prathamā Sg. Fem.* = decline / decay
* **भवति** $\rightarrow$ *Laṭ Lakāra 3rd Sg.* = happens / arises
* **भारत** $\rightarrow$ *Sambodhana (Vocative)* = O Arjuna (descendant of Bharata)!
* **अभ्युत्थानम्** $\rightarrow$ *Prathamā Sg. Neut. (अभि $+$ उत् $+$ थानम्)* = rise / ascendancy
* **अधर्मस्य** $\rightarrow$ *Ṣaṣṭhī Sg. Masc.* = of unrighteousness
* **तदा** $\rightarrow$ *Correlative Avyaya* = then / at that time
* **आत्मानम्** $\rightarrow$ *Dvitīyā Sg. Masc.* = Myself (the Self)
* **सृजामि** $\rightarrow$ *Laṭ Lakāra 1st Sg. (सृज्)* = I manifest / project forth
* **अहम्** $\rightarrow$ *Prathamā Sg.* = I

#### 3. अन्वयः & Meaning:
> *हे भारत! यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिः, अधर्मस्य च अभ्युत्थानं भवति, तदा अहम् आत्मानं सृजामि।*
> **"Whenever there is a decline of righteousness, O Bharata, and a rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself into the world!"**

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **कर्मणि** $\rightarrow$ सप्तमी विभक्तिः (Locative) | एकवचनम् (Singular)
2. **धर्मस्य** $\rightarrow$ षष्ठी विभक्तिः (Genitive) | एकवचनम् (Singular)
3. **आत्मानम्** $\rightarrow$ द्वितीया विभक्तिः (Accusative) | एकवचनम् (Singular)
4. **फलेषु** $\rightarrow$ सप्तमी विभक्तिः (Locative) | बहुवचनम् (Plural)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **कर्मणि $+$ एव $+$ अधिकारः $+$ ते**
2. **सृजामि $+$ अहम्**
