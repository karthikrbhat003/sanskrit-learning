# Day 029: पञ्चतन्त्रम् - लोभाविष्ट-कथा (The Greed-Stricken Traveler: Lyap Gerunds)

Welcome to Day 29! Today, we read a thought-provoking warning against unchecked greed from the Panchatantra: **लोभाविष्ट-चक्रधर-कथा (The Wheel on the Head of the Greedy)**.

Today's core grammar concept is **ल्यप् प्रत्ययः (Lyap Suffix)**: the prefixed twin of the *Ktvā* gerund, used whenever a verb root carries an **Upasarga (Prefix)** (**प्रविश्य, आगत्य, प्रणम्य, विज्ञाय**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> अतिलोभो न कर्तव्यो लोभं नैव परित्यजेत् ।
> अतिलोभाभिभूतस्य चक्रं भ्रमति मस्तके ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *atilobho na kartavyo lobhaṃ naiva parityajet |*
> *atilobhābhibhūtasya cakraṃ bhramati mastake ||*

**English Translation:**
> Excessive greed should never be indulged; nor should legitimate ambition be abandoned. For one overpowered by unbounded greed, a whirling wheel of pain spins upon his head!

---

## 📖 मूल-कथा (Original Panchatantra Prose)

### **लोभाविष्टः चक्रधरः (The Wheel-Bearer)**

**एकस्मिन् नगरे चत्वारः दरिद्राः ब्राह्मणाः अवसन्। ते दारिद्र्यम् **विहाय** (वि + हा + ल्यप् = having abandoned) धन-प्राप्तये हिमालये अगच्छन्।**
> *Ekasmin nagare catvāraḥ daridrāḥ brāhmaṇāḥ avasan. Te dāridryam vihāya dhana-prāptaye Himālaye agacchan.*
> In a city lived four impoverished Brahmanas. **Having left behind** their poverty, they traveled to the Himalayas in search of wealth.

**तत्र भैरवानन्दं नाम सिद्ध-योगिनम् **प्रणम्य** (प्र + नम् + ल्यप् = having bowed) ते अवदन्—"अस्माकम् दारिद्र्यम् दूरीकुरु।"**
> *Tatra Bhairavānandaṃ nāma siddha-yoginam praṇamya te avadan—"Asmākam dāridryam dūrīkuru."*
> **Having bowed** before a perfected sage named Bhairavananda, they pleaded—"Remove our poverty."

**योगी तेभ्यः चतस्रः वर्तिकाः (magic wicks) **प्रदाय** (प्र + दा + ल्यप् = having given) अवदत्—"यत्र यस्य वर्तिका पतिष्यति, तत्र सः धनम् **प्राप्स्यति**।"**
> *Yogī tebhyaḥ catasraḥ vartikāḥ pradāya avadat—"Yatra yasya vartikā patiṣyati, tatra saḥ dhanam prāpsyati."*
> The yogi, **having handed** four magic wicks to them, said—"Wherever one's wick drops, there he will find treasure."

**मार्गे प्रथमस्य वर्तिका अपतत्। सः भूमिम् **खनित्वा** ताम्रम् (copper) **प्राप्य** (प्र + आप् + ल्यप् = having obtained) सन्तुष्टः अभवत्।**
> *Mārge prathamasya vartikā apatat. Saḥ bhūmim khanitvā tāmram prāpya santuṣṭaḥ abhavat.*
> On the path, the first man's wick fell. **Having dug** the earth and **having obtained** copper, he was satisfied.

**अग्रे गत्वा द्वितीयस्य वर्तिका अपतत्। सः रौप्यम् (silver) **लब्ध्वा** प्रत्यागच्छत्। तृतीयः सुवर्णम् (gold) **आदाय** (आ + दा + ल्यप् = having taken) परावृत्तः।**
> *Agre gatvā dvitīyasya vartikā apatat. Saḥ raupyam labdhvā pratyāgacchat. Tṛtīyaḥ suvarṇam ādāya parāvṛttaḥ.*
> Going further, the second wick fell. He returned **having gotten** silver. The third turned back **having taken** gold.

**चतुर्थः अतिलुब्धः आसीत्। सः "अग्रे रत्नानि भविष्यन्ति" इति **विचार्य** (वि + चर् + ल्यप् = having thought) एकाकी अग्रे अगच्छत्।**
> *Caturthaḥ atilubdhaḥ āsīt. Saḥ "Agre ratnāni bhaviṣyanti" iti vicārya ekākī agre agacchat.*
> The fourth was excessively greedy. **Having contemplated** "Ahead there must be diamonds and gems!", he went ahead all alone.

**तत्र एकम् पुरुषम् **दृष्ट्वा** सः अपश्यत् यत् तस्य मस्तके एकम् तीक्ष्णम् चक्रम् भ्रमति स्म।**
> *Tatra ekam puruṣam dṛṣṭvā saḥ apaśyat yat tasya mastake ekam tīkṣṇam cakram bhramati sma.*
> Seeing a man there, he noticed that a razor-sharp wheel was spinning on his head.

**लोभी ब्राह्मणः तस्य समीपम् **उपगम्य** (उप + गम् + ल्यप् = having approached) अपृच्छत्—"भोः! त्वम् कः? तव मस्तके इदम् चक्रम् किमर्थम्?"**
> *Lobhī brāhmaṇaḥ tasya samīpam upagamya apṛcchat—"Bhoḥ! Tvam kaḥ? Tava mastake idam cakram kimartham?"*
> The greedy Brahmana, **having approached** near him, asked—"O sir! Who are you? Why is this wheel on your head?"

**वचन-समकालमेव तत् चक्रम् तस्य मस्तकात् **उत्थाय** (उत् + स्था + ल्यप् = having risen) चतुर्थस्य लोभिनः मस्तके स्थापितम् अभवत्!**
> *Vacana-samakālam eva tat cakram tasya mastakāt utthāya caturthasya lobhinaḥ mastake sthāpitam abhavat!*
> That very moment, that wheel, **having lifted** from that man's head, placed itself directly on the head of the fourth greedy Brahmana!

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (The Magic of ल्यप् प्रत्ययः)

The Paninian rule states:
$$\textbf{Root alone} \longrightarrow \textbf{क्त्वा (-ktvā)} \quad \text{vs} \quad \textbf{Upasarga} + \textbf{Root} \longrightarrow \textbf{ल्यप् (-ya)}$$

Notice the exact correspondence:

| Base Root | With Ktvā (No Prefix) | Prefixed Root | With Lyap (With Prefix) | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **गम् (gam)** | **गत्वा** (gatvā) | **आ $+$ गम्** | **आगत्य / आगम्य** (*āgatya*) | having arrived / come |
| **विश् (viś)** | **विष्ट्वा** (viṣṭvā) | **प्र $+$ विश्** | **प्रविश्य** (*praviśya*) | having entered |
| **नम् (nam)** | **नत्वा** (natvā) | **प्र $+$ नम्** | **प्रणम्य** (*praṇamya*) | having bowed deeply |
| **दा (dā)** | **दत्त्वा** (dattvā) | **प्र $+$ दा** | **प्रदाय** (*pradāya*) | having handed / given |
| **स्था (sthā)** | **स्थित्वा** (sthitvā) | **उत् $+$ स्था** | **उत्थाय** (*utthāya*) | having stood up / arisen |
| **ज्ञा (jñā)** | **ज्ञात्वा** (jñātvā) | **वि $+$ ज्ञा** | **विज्ञाय** (*vijñāya*) | having recognized / known |
| **आप् (āp)** | **आप्त्वा** (āptvā) | **प्र $+$ आप्** | **प्राप्य** (*prāpya*) | having reached / obtained |
| **हृ (hṛ)** | **हृत्वा** (hṛtvā) | **वि $+$ हृ** | **विहृत्य** (*vihṛtya*) | having strolled / relaxed |

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. प्र $+$ विश् $+$ ल्यप् $=$ **प्रविश्य** (*praviśya*)
2. आ $+$ गम् $+$ ल्यप् $=$ **आगत्य** (*āgatya*)
3. प्र $+$ नम् $+$ ल्यप् $=$ **प्रणम्य** (*praṇamya*)
4. उत् $+$ स्था $+$ ल्यप् $=$ **उत्थाय** (*utthāya*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. मन्दिरम् **प्रविश्य** भक्तः ईश्वरम् **प्रणनाम (प्रणमति)**। (*Mandiram praviśya bhaktaḥ īśvaram praṇamati.*)
2. गृहम् **आगत्य** सा पाठम् **अपठत्**। (*Gṛham āgatya sā pāṭham apaṭhat.*)
3. **उत्थाय** छात्राः श्लोकम् **अगायन्**। (*Utthāya chātrāḥ ślokam agāyan.*)
