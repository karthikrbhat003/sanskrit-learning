# Day 021: लोट्-लकारः (Imperative Mood - Vlogger CTA, Polite Requests & Restaurant Orders)

Welcome to Day 21! Today, we explore **लोट्-लकारः (Loṭ Lakāra - Imperative Mood)**. In Sanskrit, this mood is used constantly in spoken communication to give polite requests, invitations, permissions, restaurant orders, and **Vlogger Call-to-Actions (दर्शक-निवेदनानि: Like, Share, Comment, Subscribe)** (**भवतु, ददातु, उपविशतु, लिखन्तु**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This universal benediction from the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad uses **Loṭ Lakāra** across every line:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः ।
> सर्वे भद्राणि पश्यन्तु मा कश्चिद् दुःखभाग् भवेत् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *sarve bhavantu sukhinaḥ sarve santu nirāmayāḥ |*
> *sarve bhadrāṇi paśyantu mā kaścid duḥkhabhāg bhavet ||*

**English Translation:**
> **May all beings be happy!** **May all be free from illness!** **May all behold what is auspicious!** May no one suffer from sorrow!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read these polite interactions inside a classroom and home:

### **सदादेशः प्रार्थना च (Polite Requests & Guidance)**

**अध्यापकः:** छात्राः! सर्वे **उत्तिष्ठन्तु**, ईश्वर-वन्दनं च **कुर्वन्तु**।
> *Adhyāpakaḥ: Chātrāḥ! Sarve uttiṣṭhantu, īśvara-vandanaṃ ca kurvantu.*
> **Teacher:** Students! May all **stand up** and **offer prayers** to God.

**अध्यापकः:** बालक! तव पुस्तकम् **उद्घाटय**, उच्चैः च **पठ**।
> *Adhyāpakaḥ: Bālaka! Tava pustakam udghāṭaya, uccaiḥ ca paṭha.*
> **Teacher:** Boy! **Open** your book, and **read** aloud.

**माता:** पुत्रि! अत्र **आगच्छ**, फलम् **खाद**, दुग्धम् च **पिब**।
> *Mātā: Putri! Atra āgaccha, phalam khāda, dugdham ca piba.*
> **Mother:** Daughter! **Come** here, **eat** the fruit, and **drink** the milk.

**अतिथिः:** कृपया मह्यम् किञ्चित् जलम् **ददातु**।
> *Atithiḥ: Kṛpayā mahyam kiñcit jalam dadātu.*
> **Guest:** Please **give** me a little water.

**गृहस्थः:** अवश्यम्, अत्र **उपविशतु**, जलं च **स्वीकरोतु**।
> *Gṛhasthaḥ: Avaśyam, atra upaviśatu, jalaṃ ca svīkarotu.*
> **Host:** Certainly, please **sit** here and **accept** the water.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: दैनन्दिन-प्रार्थनाः (Daily Requests & Dining)

In day-to-day life, knowing how to make polite requests is essential:

### 1. Essential Polite Request Formulas (with भवान् / भवती $\rightarrow$ -तु / -tu)

| Daily Situation | Sanskrit Polite Request (-तु) | IAST | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Please sit down** | कृपया अत्र **उपविशतु**। | *Kṛpayā atra upaviśatu.* | Please sit here. |
| **Please give** | कृपया तत् **ददातु**। | *Kṛpayā tat dadātu.* | Please give that. |
| **Please speak** | भवान् किञ्चित् **वदतु**। | *Bhavān kiñcit vadatu.* | Please speak something. |
| **Please listen** | ध्यानेन **शृणोतु**। | *Dhyānena śṛṇotu.* | Please listen attentively. |
| **Please open the door**| द्वारम् **उद्घाटयतु**। | *Dvāram udghāṭayatu.* | Please open the door. |
| **Please close the door**| द्वारम् **पिदधातु**। | *Dvāram pidadhātu.* | Please close the door. |
| **Please accept / take**| भोजनं / चायं **स्वीकरोतु**। | *Bhojanaṃ / cāyaṃ svīkarotu.* | Please accept food / tea. |
| **Alright / May it be**| **भवतु** / **अस्तु**। | *Bhavatu / Astu.* | Alright / Let it be so / Okay. |

## 🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्: दर्शक-निवेदनम् (Creator Call-to-Action Scripts)

When speaking directly to your viewers at the climax or conclusion of your video:

### 1. The Ultimate Vlogger Call-to-Action Formula (Loṭ Lakāra)

> **"यदि भवतः एषः दृश्यवृत्तान्तः अरोचत (If you liked this vlog):"**
> 
> 1. **"कृपया दृश्यं लाइक कुर्वन्तु (Please like the video)!"**
>    * *Kṛpayā dṛśyaṃ like kurvantu!*
> 2. **"अधः टिप्पणी-भागे स्व-विचारान् लिखन्तु (Write your thoughts below in the comments)!"**
>    * *Adhaḥ ṭippaṇī-bhāge sva-vicārān likhantu!*
> 3. **"मित्रेभ्यः एतं दृश्यवृत्तान्तं प्रेषयन्तु / साझां कुर्वन्तु (Share this vlog with your friends)!"**
>    * *Mitrebhyaḥ etaṃ dṛśyavṛttāntaṃ preṣayantu!*
> 4. **"नूतन-दृश्यवृत्तान्तानां कृते अस्माकं वाहिनीं सब्सक्राइब कुर्वन्तु (Subscribe to our channel for new vlogs)!"**
>    * *Nūtana-dṛśyavṛttāntānāṃ kṛte asmākaṃ vāhinīṃ subscribe kurvantu!*

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (The Complete 3x3 Loṭ Lakāra Matrix)

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Person (पुरुषः)} & \textbf{Singular (एक.)} & \textbf{Dual (द्वि.)} & \textbf{Plural (बहु.)} \\
\hline
\textbf{3rd (प्रथमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{पठतु} \text{ (let him read!)} & \textbf{पठताम्} \text{ (let two read!)} & \textbf{पठन्तु} \text{ (let them read!)} \\
\textbf{2nd (मध्यमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{पठ} \text{ (you read!)} & \textbf{पठतम्} \text{ (you two read!)} & \textbf{पठत} \text{ (you all read!)} \\
\textbf{1st (उत्तमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{पठानि} \text{ (let me read!)} & \textbf{पठाव} \text{ (let us two read!)} & \textbf{पठाम} \text{ (let us read!)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

### Polite Plural for Audience:
* **कुर्वन्तु** (*All of you please do!*)
* **लिखन्तु** (*All of you please write!*)
* **पश्यन्तु** (*All of you please watch / look!*)
* **शृण्वन्तु** (*All of you please listen!*)

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. सर्वे **लिखन्तु**!
2. सर्वे **पश्यन्तु**!
3. सर्वे **कुर्वन्तु**!
4. सर्वे **शृण्वन्तु**!

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **कृपया अधः टिप्पणी-भागे टिप्पणीं लिखन्तु।** (*Kṛpayā adhaḥ ṭippaṇī-bhāge ṭippaṇīṃ likhantu.*)
2. **कृपया इदं दृश्यं मित्रेभ्यः प्रेषयन्तु।** (*Kṛpayā idaṃ dṛśyaṃ mitrebhyaḥ preṣayantu.*)
3. **कृपया अस्माकं वाहिनीं सब्सक्राइब कुर्वन्तु।** (*Kṛpayā asmākaṃ vāhinīṃ subscribe kurvantu.*)
4. **सर्वे सुखिनः भवन्तु!** (*Sarve sukhinaḥ bhavantu!*)
