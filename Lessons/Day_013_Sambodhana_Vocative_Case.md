# Day 013: सम्बोधन-विभक्तिः (Vocative Case - Direct Address & Telephone Etiquette)

Welcome to Day 13! Today, we study **सम्बोधन-विभक्तिः (Sambodhana Vibhakti - Vocative Case)**. This case is used in direct human communication whenever we call out to someone, address a listener, get someone's attention ("*O Rama! O Friend! Hey Brother!*"), or speak on the telephone (**दूरवाणी-सम्भाषणम्**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> अयं निजः परो वेति गणना लघुचेतसाम् ।
> उदारचरितानां तु वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *ayaṃ nijaḥ paro veti gaṇanā laghucetasām |*
> *udāracaritānāṃ tu vasudhaiva kuṭumbakam ||*

**English Translation:**
> "This one is mine, and that one is an outsider"—such calculation belongs to the narrow-minded. For the noble-hearted, the entire Earth is verily one single family!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read these real-life conversational addresses and classroom exchanges:

### **प्रत्यक्ष-आह्वानम् (Direct Address)**

**माता:** **हे बाल!** (O child!) अत्र आगच्छ, दुग्धम् पिब।
> *Mātā: He bāla! Atra āgaccha, dugdham piba.*
> **Mother:** **O child!** Come here, drink milk.

**बालकः:** **मातः!** (O mother!) अहम् आगच्छामि।
> *Bālakaḥ: Mātaḥ! Aham āgacchāmi.*
> **Boy:** **O mother!** I am coming.

**छात्रः:** **आचार्य!** (O teacher!) श्लोकस्य अर्थं कृपया वदतु।
> *Chātraḥ: Ācārya! Ślokasya arthaṃ kṛpayā vadatu.*
> **Student:** **O teacher!** Please tell the meaning of the verse.

**शिक्षकः:** **भोः छात्राः!** (O students!) ध्यानेन शृणुत।
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Bhoḥ chātrāḥ! Dhyānena śṛṇuta.*
> **Teacher:** **O students!** Listen with full attention.

**भक्तः:** **हे प्रभो!** (O Lord!) अस्माकम् कल्याणम् कुरु।
> *Bhaktaḥ: He Prabho! Asmākam kalyāṇam kuru.*
> **Devotee:** **O Lord!** Grant auspicious welfare unto us.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: दूरवाणी-वार्तालापः (Telephone Etiquette & Spoken Formulas)

In spoken Sanskrit, phone calls and polite summons have clear, musical formulas:

### 1. Daily Vocative Calling Formulas (सम्बोधन-पदानि)

| Base Word | Meaning | Vocative Form (Direct Call) | Example Spoken Call |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **रामः** | Rama (Masc. -a) | **हे राम! / राम!** | **राम!** अत्र आगच्छतु। (Rama! Please come here.) |
| **बालिका** | Girl (Fem. -ā) | **हे बालिके!** | **बालिके!** पुस्तकं पठ। (O girl! Read the book.) |
| **भगिनी** | Sister (Fem. -ī) | **हे भगिनि!** | **भगिनि!** जलं ददातु। (Sister! Please give water.) |
| **माता** | Mother | **हे मातः!** | **मातः!** भोजनं सिद्धम् वा? (Mother! Is food ready?) |
| **पिता** | Father | **हे पितः!** | **पितः!** प्रणामः। (Father! Salutations.) |
| **मित्रम्** | Friend | **हे मित्र! / भो मित्र!** | **भो मित्र!** किम् वार्ता? (O friend! What is the news?) |
| **महोदयः** | Respected Sir | **महोदय! / महाभाग!** | **महोदय!** क्षम्यताम्। (Respected sir! Excuse me.) |
| **मान्या** | Respected Madam | **मान्ये!** | **मान्ये!** स्वागतम्। (Madam! Welcome.) |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogue: Telephone Conversation (दूरवाणी-सम्भाषणम्)

* **रमेशः (Calling):** हरिः ओम्!
  > *Hariḥ om!*
  > *Hello!*
* **सुरेशः (Answering):** हरिः ओम्! कः वदति?
  > *Hariḥ om! Kaḥ vadati?*
  > *Hello! Who is speaking?*
* **रमेशः:** अहम् रमेशः वदामि। भो सुरेश! किम् अहम् आनन्देन सह सम्भाषणं कर्तुं शक्नोमि?
  > *Aham Rameshḥ vadāmi. Bho Sureśa! Kim aham Ānandena saha sambhāṣaṇaṃ kartuṃ śaknomi?*
  > *I am Ramesh speaking. O Suresh! Can I speak with Anand?*
* **सुरेशः:** आम्, आनन्दः गृहे एव अस्ति। कृपया क्षणम् प्रतीक्षताम् (Please wait a moment)। आनन्द! तव दूरवाणी अस्ति!
  > *Ām, Ānandaḥ gṛhe eva asti. Kṛpayā kṣaṇam pratīkṣatām. Ānanda! Tava dūravāṇī asti!*
  > *Yes, Anand is right at home. Please wait a moment. Anand! There is a phone call for you!*
* **आनन्दः:** हरिः ओम् रमेश! वदतु, किम् वार्ता?
  > *Hariḥ om Rameśa! Vadatu, kim vārtā?*
  > *Hello Ramesh! Tell me, what's the news?*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **हे बालिके! अत्र आगच्छ (he bālike! atra āgaccha)**
   * **बालिके**: Vocative Singular of *bālikā* ($\bar{a} \rightarrow e$).
   * *Rule*: Feminine nouns ending in long $-\bar{a}$ change to $-e$ in the Vocative singular.

2. **हे भगिनि! (he bhagini!)**
   * **भगिनि**: Vocative Singular of *bhaginī* (long $-\bar{i}$ shortens to $-i$).

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Sambodhana Declension Rules)

| Stem Type | Prathamā (Subject) | Sambodhana (Direct Call) | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masc. in -a** | **रामः** (rāmaḥ) | **हे राम!** (he rāma - drop visarga!) | *हे राम! शृणु।* |
| **Fem. in -ā** | **बालिका** (bālikā) | **हे बालिके!** (he bālike - $-\bar{a} \rightarrow -e$) | *हे बालिके! आगच्छ।* |
| **Fem. in -ī** | **नदी** (nadī) | **हे नदि!** (he nadi - $-\bar{i} \rightarrow -i$) | *हे भगिनि! वद।* |
| **Neut. in -a** | **मित्रम्** (mitram) | **हे मित्र!** (he mitra - drop $-m$) | *हे मित्र! पश्य।* |

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. हे **कृष्ण**!
2. हे **राधे**!
3. हे **जननि**!
4. हे **मित्र**!
5. हे **अध्यापक**!

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **हरिः ओम्! कः वदति? $\rightarrow$ अहम् सुरेशः वदामि।**
2. **हे भगिनि! कृपया जलम् ददातु।** (*He bhagini! Kṛpayā jalam dadātu.*)
3. **भो मित्र! किम् वार्ता?** (*Bho mitra! Kim vārtā?*)
4. **महोदय! क्षम्यताम्।** (*Mahodaya! Kṣamyatām.*)
