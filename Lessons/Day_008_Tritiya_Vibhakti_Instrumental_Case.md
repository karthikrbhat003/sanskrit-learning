# Day 008: तृतीया विभक्तिः (Instrumental Case - Travel, Tools & Vlogging Gear)

Welcome to Day 8! Today, we explore **तृतीया विभक्तिः (Tṛtīyā Vibhakti - Instrumental Case)**. In Sanskrit grammar, this case marks the **करण-कारकम् (Karaṇa Kāraka)**—the instrument or means by which an action is achieved ("*by / with / using X*"), means of travel, tools, video recording equipment, and accompaniment with **सह (saha = together with)**.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> दानेन पाणिर्न तु कङ्कणेन स्नानेन शुद्धिर्न तु चन्दनेन ।
> मानेन तृप्तिर्न तु भोजनेन ज्ञानेन मुक्तिर्न तु मण्डनेन ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *dānena pāṇir na tu kaṅkaṇena snānena śuddhir na tu candanena |*
> *mānena tṛptir na tu bhojanena jñānena muktir na tu maṇḍanena ||*

**English Translation:**
> The hand is truly adorned **by giving charity** (*dānena*), not **by a golden bangle** (*kaṅkaṇena*); purity is attained **by bathing** (*snānena*), not **by sandalwood paste** (*candanena*); inner fulfillment comes **from self-respect** (*mānena*), not **from mere food** (*bhojanena*); and spiritual liberation comes **through knowledge** (*jñānena*), not **through outer decoration** (*maṇḍanena*)!

### **कर्म-साधनानि (The Tools of Action)**

**शिल्पकारः **हस्तेन** कार्यम् करोति, **तूलिकया** (with a brush) च सुन्दरम् चित्रम् रचयति।**
> *Śilpakāraḥ hastena kāryam karoti, tūlikayā ca sundaram citram racayati.*
> **The artist works with his hand, and paints a beautiful picture with a paintbrush.**

**अहम् **नेत्राभ्याम्** (with two eyes) जगत् पश्यामि, **कर्णाभ्याम्** (with two ears) मधुरम् सङ्गीतम् शृणोमि।**
> *Aham netrābhyām jagat paśyāmi, karṇābhyām madhuram saṅgītam śṛṇomi.*
> **I see the world with both eyes, and listen to sweet music with both ears.**

**बालकः **पाद-कन्दुकेन** (with a football) खेलति, **पादैः** (with feet) च वेगेन धावति।**
> *Bālakaḥ pāda-kandukena khelati, pādaiḥ ca vegena dhāvati.*
> **The boy plays with a football, and runs fast with his feet.**

**जनकः **कार-यानेन** (by car) कार्यालयम् गच्छति। सः **मित्रेण सह** (along with friend) कार्यम् करोति।**
> *Janakaḥ kāra-yānena kāryālayam gacchati. Saḥ mitreṇa saha kāryam karoti.*
> **Father goes to office by car. He works together with his friend.**

**अलम् **विवादेन** (enough with arguing)! सर्वे **स्नेहेन** (with affection) जीवन्तु।**
> *Alam vivādena! Sarve snehena jīvantu.*
> **Enough of dispute! May everyone live with love.**

### 3. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Daily Companionship (*सहाध्यायी & मित्रम्*)

#### Scenario A: Planning a Trip / Commute
* **दीपकः (Deepak):** भवान् कार्यालयं कथम् (by what means) गच्छति?
  > *Bhavān kāryālayaṃ katham gacchati?*
  > *How do you go to the office?*
* **सुरेशः (Suresh):** अहम् **मेट्रो-रेलयानेन** (by metro) गच्छामि। भवान् कथम् गच्छति?
  > *Aham meṭro-relayānena gacchāmi. Bhavān katham gacchati?*
  > *I go by metro train. How do you go?*
* **दीपकः:** अहम् **द्विचक्रिकया** (by cycle / two-wheeler) गच्छामि।
  > *Aham dvicakrikayā gacchāmi.*
  > *I go by two-wheeler.*

#### Scenario B: Asking Who Is Accompanying (केन सह?)
* **माता (Mother):** पुत्र! त्वं **केन सह** आपणं गच्छसि?
  > *Putra! Tvam kena saha āpaṇaṃ gacchasi?*
  > *Son! With whom are you going to the market?*
* **पुत्रः (Son):** अहम् **मित्रेण सह** आपणं गच्छामि।
  > *Aham mitreṇa saha āpaṇaṃ gacchāmi.*
  > *I am going to the market with my friend.*
* **माता:** उत्तमम्, **सावधानेन** (with care) गच्छ।
  > *Uttamam, sāvadhānena gaccha.*
  > *Great, go carefully!*

## 🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्: यात्रा-वृत्तान्तः & यन्त्र-साधनानि (Travel & Gear Vlog Script)

Use this creator script when filming travel adventures or explaining your creator equipment:

### 1. Travel & Creator Gear Vlogging Script

> **"भो दर्शकाः! अद्य वयम् **रेल-यानेन** (by train) नूतनं नगरं गच्छामः!"**
> *Bho darśakāḥ! Adya vayam rela-yānena nūtanaṃ nagaraṃ gacchāmaḥ!*
> **"Hey viewers! Today we are traveling to a new city by train!"**

> **"अहम् **मित्रेण सह** (along with my friend) यात्रां करोमि।"**
> *Aham mitreṇa saha yātrāṃ karomi.*
> **"I am traveling together with my friend."**

> **"अहम् **चलचित्राङ्कन-यन्त्रेण** (with my video camera) दृश्यं गृह्णामि (capturing footage)।"**
> *Aham calacitrāṅkana-yantreṇa dṛśyaṃ gṛhṇāmi.*
> **"I am capturing footage with my video camera."**

> **"वयम् **सङ्गणकेन** (using computer) दृश्यवृत्तान्तस्य सम्पादनं (editing) कुर्मः!"**
> *Vayam saṅgaṇakena dṛśyavṛttāntasya sampādanaṃ kurmaḥ!*
> **"We edit the vlog using our computer!"**

> **"भवान् यात्रायै **केन यानेन** (by which vehicle) गन्तुम् इच्छति? कृपया वदतु!"**
> *Bhavān yātrāyai kena yānena gantum icchati? Kṛpayā vadatu!*
> **"By which vehicle do you like to travel? Please tell in comments!"**

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **शिल्पकारः तूलिकया चित्रम् रचयति (śilpakāraḥ tūlikayā citram racayati)**
   * **शिल्पकारः**: Subject (Prathamā Sg. Masc.)
   * **तूलिकया**: Instrument (Tṛtīyā Sg. Fem. $\rightarrow$ *-ayā*)
   * **चित्रम्**: Direct Object (Dvitīyā Sg. Neut.)
   * **रचयति**: Verb (creates/paints)

2. **जनकः मित्रेण सह गच्छति (janakaḥ mitreṇa saha gacchati)**
   * **सहा-योगे तृतीया**: Whenever **सह (saha = with)** is used, the accompanying person takes the **Tṛtīyā Case** (*mitreṇa saha*).

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. सः **बस-यानेन** नगरं गच्छति।
2. अहम् **लेखन्या** लिखामि।
3. बालकः **मित्रेण** सह क्रीडति।
4. सा **चमसेन** खादति।
5. व्लॉगरः **चलचित्राङ्कन-यन्त्रेण** दृश्यं गृह्णाति।
6. वयम् **नेत्राभ्याम्** पश्यामः।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **अहम् द्विचक्रिकया विद्यालयं गच्छामि।** (*Aham dvicakrikayā vidyālayam gacchāmi.*)
2. **सा दूरवाण्या मात्रा सह सम्भाषते।** (*Sā dūravāṇyā mātrā saha sambhāṣate.*)
3. **पिता रेल-यानेन प्रयाणं करोति।** (*Pitā rela-yānena prayāṇaṃ karoti.*)
4. **वयम् कर्णाभ्याम् सङ्गीतं शृणुमः।** (*Vayam karṇābhyām saṅgītaṃ śṛṇumaḥ.*)

### Answers to Exercise 3:
1. **अहम् मित्रेण सह रेल-यानेन यात्रां करोमि।** (*Aham mitreṇa saha rela-yānena yātrāṃ karomi.*)
2. **वयम् चलचित्राङ्कन-यन्त्रेण दृश्यं गृह्णीमः।** (*Vayam calacitrāṅkana-yantreṇa dṛśyaṃ gṛhṇīmaḥ.*)
3. **सा सङ्गणकेन दृश्यवृत्तान्तं सम्पादयति।** (*Sā saṅgaṇakena dṛśyavṛttāntaṃ sampādayati.*)
4. **वयम् पादाभ्याम् चलामः।** (*Vayam pādābhyām calāmaḥ.*)
