# Day 005B: दैनिक-क्रिया-सङ्ग्रहः (Master Directory of Daily Actions: Swimming, Walking, Dancing, Bathing & Beyond)

Welcome to this special masterclass lesson! To speak Sanskrit fluently in real life, you need ready access to the verbs that describe everything human beings do from the moment they wake up until they fall asleep.

Today, we explore an exhaustive, categorized directory of **everyday physical actions, bodily movements, self-care routines, eating habits, sports, and communications** in Sanskrit (**तरति, चलति, नृत्यति, स्नानं करोति, पचति, कूर्दति**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This inspiring verse reminds us that true nobility lies in constant, purposeful action:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> प्रारभ्यते न खलु विघ्नभयेन नीचैः
> प्रारभ्य विघ्नविहता विरमन्ति मध्याः ।
> विघ्नैः पुनः पुनरपि प्रतिहन्यमानाः
> प्रारब्धमुत्तमगुणा न परित्यजन्ति ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *prārabhyate na khalu vighnabhayena nīcaiḥ |*
> *prārabhya vighnavihatā viramanti madhyāḥ ||*
> *vighnaiḥ punaḥ punar api pratihanyamānāḥ |*
> *prārabdham uttamaguṇā na parityajanti ||*

**English Translation:**
> Timid people never start an action out of fear of obstacles; average people begin an action but stop when struck by obstacles. But noble-minded people of excellence, even when repeatedly struck by countless hurdles, never abandon an action once begun!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः: एकस्य दिनस्य सर्वाः क्रियाः (A Day of Continuous Actions)

Read how different family members perform daily actions throughout the day:

### **सक्रियं जीवनम् (An Active Day)**

**प्रातःकाले सूर्योदये सर्वे **उत्तिष्ठन्ति** (wake up)।**
> *Prātaḥkāle sūryodaye sarve uttiṣṭhanti.*
> In the morning at sunrise, everyone wakes up.

**देवदत्तः दन्तधावनं **करोति**, शीतल-जलेन च स्नानं **करोति** (takes bath)।**
> *Devadattaḥ dantadhāvanaṃ karoti, śītala-jalena ca snānaṃ karoti.*
> Devadatta brushes his teeth, and takes a bath with cool water.

**सः स्वच्छं वस्त्रं **धारयति** (wears clothes), केशान् **प्रसाधयति** (combs hair) च।**
> *Saḥ svacchaṃ vastraṃ dhārayati, keśān prasādhayati ca.*
> He wears clean clothes and combs his hair.

**तस्य भ्राता सरोवरे **तरति** (swims), उद्याने च **धावति** (runs)।**
> *Tasya bhrātā sarovare tarati, udyāne ca dhāvati.*
> His brother swims in the lake and runs in the park.

**भगिनी सङ्गीतेन **नृत्यति** (dances), मधुरं गीतं च **गायति** (sings)।**
> *Bhaginī saṅgītena nṛtyati, madhuraṃ gītaṃ ca gāyati.*
> The sister dances to music and sings a sweet song.

**माता पाकशालायां स्वादिष्टं भोजनं **पचति** (cooks), प्रीत्या च **परिवेषयति** (serves)।**
> *Mātā pākaśālāyāṃ svādiṣṭaṃ bhojanaṃ pacati, prītyā ca pariveṣayati.*
> Mother cooks delicious food in the kitchen, and serves it with love.

**सर्वे आसने **उपविशन्ति** (sit down), भोजनं **खादन्ति** (eat), जलं च **पिबन्ति** (drink)।**
> *Sarve āsane upaviśanti, bhojanaṃ khādanti, jalaṃ ca pibanti.*
> Everyone sits on chairs, eats food, and drinks water.

**शिशुः आनन्देन **कूर्दति** (jumps), उच्चैः **हसति** (laughs), मार्गे च **चलति** (walks)।**
> *Śiśuḥ ānandena kūrdati, uccaiḥ hasati, mārge ca calati.*
> The toddler jumps with joy, laughs aloud, and walks on the path.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: दैनन्दिन-क्रिया-संवादाः (Action Dialogues)

### Dialogue 1: At the Sports Complex & Activity Center (क्रीडा-केन्द्रे)
* **अजयः (Ajay):** भो विजय! भवान् अत्र किम् करोति?
  > *Bho Vijaya! Bhavān atra kim karoti?*
  > *Vijay! What are you doing here?*
* **विजयः (Vijay):** अहम् अत्र **तरामि** (I swim) प्रतिदिनम्। भवान् किम् करोति?
  > *Aham atra tarāmi pratidinam. Bhavān kim karoti?*
  > *I swim here every day. What do you do?*
* **अजयः:** अहम् प्राङ्गणे **धावामि** (I run), ततः मम भगिनी सङ्गीतेन **नृत्यति** (dances)।
  > *Aham prāṅgaṇe dhāvāmi, tataḥ mama bhaginī saṅgītena nṛtyati.*
  > *I run on the ground, and then my sister dances to music.*
* **विजयः:** अति-उत्तमम्! शरीर-स्वास्थ्याय **तरणं**, **धावनं**, **नर्तनं** च अति-हितकरम्!
  > *Ati-uttamam! Śarīra-svāsthyāya taraṇaṃ, dhāvanaṃ, nartanaṃ ca ati-hitakaram!*
  > *Excellent! For bodily health, swimming, running, and dancing are very beneficial!*

---

### Dialogue 2: Morning Preparation at Home (प्रातःकाले सज्जीभवनम्)
* **माता (Mother):** रोहन! शीघ्रम् **उत्तिष्ठ**! किम् भवान् **दन्तधावनम्** अकरोत्?
  > *Rohan! Śīghram uttiṣṭha! Kim bhavān dantadhāvanam akarot?*
  > *Rohan! Wake up quickly! Did you brush your teeth?*
* **रोहनः (Rohan):** आम् मातः, अहम् दन्तधावनं **कृतवान्** (did), अधुना **स्नानं करोमि**।
  > *Ām mātaḥ, aham dantadhāvanaṃ kṛtavān, adhunā snānaṃ karomi.*
  > *Yes mother, I brushed my teeth, now I am taking a bath.*
* **माता:** स्नानं **कृत्वा** नूतनं वस्त्रं **धारयतु**, केशान् च **प्रसाधयतु**।
  > *Snānaṃ kṛtvā nūtanaṃ vastraṃ dhārayatu, keśān ca prasādhayatu.*
  > *Having taken a bath, wear clean clothes and comb your hair.*
* **रोहनः:** तथा करोमि मातः! ततः अहम् अल्पाहारं **खादिष्यामि**।
  > *Tathā karomi mātaḥ! Tataḥ aham alpāhāraṃ khādiṣyāmi.*
  > *I will do so mother! Then I will eat breakfast.*
