# Day 012: सप्तमी विभक्तिः (Locative Case - In/At Locations, Schedules & Time)

Welcome to Day 12! Today, we explore **सप्तमी विभक्तिः (Saptamī Vibhakti - Locative Case)**. In Sanskrit grammar, this case marks the **अधिकरण-कारकम् (Adhikaraṇa Kāraka)**—the physical location or surface where an action occurs ("*in / on / at X*"), specific points in time or days of the week, trust (**विश्वसिति**), affection (**स्निह्यति**), and skill/proficiency (**प्रवीणः / कुशलः**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> जले तैलं खले गुह्यं पात्रे दानं मनागपि ।
> प्राज्ञे विद्या स्वयं याति विस्तारं वस्तु-शक्तितः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *jale tailaṃ khale guhyaṃ pātre dānaṃ manāg api |*
> *prājñe vidyā svayaṃ yāti vistāraṃ vastu-śaktitaḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> A drop of oil **in water** (*jale*), a secret told **to a gossip** (*khale*), even a tiny gift given **to the worthy** (*pātre*), and knowledge imparted **to a wise student** (*prājñe*)—all naturally expand by the inherent power of the recipient!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about an active morning in nature and inside the school:

### **सरोवरे विद्यालये च (At the Lake & in the School)**

**निर्मले **सरोवरे** (in the lake) कमलानि विकसन्ति, हंसाः च **जले** तरन्ति।**
> *Nirmale sarovare kamalāni vikasanti, haṃsāḥ ca jale taranti.*
> **In the pure lake, lotuses bloom and swans swim in the water.**

**वृक्षाणां **शाखासु** (on the branches) पक्षिणः कूजन्ति, नीडेषु च **वसन्ति**।**
> *Vṛkṣāṇāṃ śākhāsu pakṣiṇaḥ kūjanti, nīḍeṣu ca vasanti.*
> **On the branches of the trees, birds chirp and live in their nests.**

**छात्राः **कक्षायाम्** (in the classroom) आसनेषु **उपविशन्ति**।**
> *Chātrāḥ kakṣāyām āsaneṣu upaviśanti.*
> **Students sit on chairs in the classroom.**

**अध्यापकः **संस्कृत-विषये** (in the Sanskrit subject) प्रवीणः अस्ति।**
> *Adhyāpakaḥ saṃskṛta-viṣaye pravīṇaḥ asti.*
> **The teacher is proficient in the subject of Sanskrit.**

**माता **पुत्रे** (upon the son) स्निह्यति, सर्वे **ईश्वरे** (in God) विश्वसन्ति।**
> *Mātā putre snihyati, sarve īśvare viśvasanti.*
> **The mother showers affection upon her son, and all place trust in God.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: कुत्र अस्ति? कदा मेलिष्यामः? (Locations & Scheduling)

In daily life, the Locative Case (*Saptamī*) is used continuously to state where people are and schedule meetings across days and hours:

### 1. Everyday Daily Locations (स्थानानि)

| Location | Sanskrit (Saptamī Case) | IAST | Example Spoken Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **At Home** | **गृहे** | *gṛhe* | अहम् अधुना **गृहे** अस्मि। (I am at home right now.) |
| **In Office** | **कार्यालये** | *kāryālaye* | सः **कार्यालये** कार्यं करोति। (He works in the office.) |
| **In Classroom** | **कक्षायाम्** | *kakṣāyām* | छात्राः **कक्षायाम्** सन्ति। (Students are in the classroom.) |
| **In the Shop** | **आपणे** | *āpaṇe* | आपणिकः **आपणे** उपविशति। (Shopkeeper sits in the shop.) |
| **On the Table** | **उत्पीठिकायाम्** | *utpīṭhikāyām* | सङ्गणकम् **उत्पीठिकायाम्** अस्ति। (Computer is on table.) |
| **In the Room** | **प्रकोष्ठे** | *prakoṣṭhe* | जनकः **प्रकोष्ठे** विश्रामं करोति। (Father rests in room.) |

---

### 2. Daily Time & Scheduling (समयाः वासराः च)

| Schedule / Time | Sanskrit (Saptamī) | IAST | Example Spoken Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **At 7 O'clock** | **सप्तवादने** | *saptavādane* | अहम् **सप्तवादने** आगमिष्यामि। (I will come at 7 o'clock.) |
| **In the Morning** | **प्रातःकाले** | *prātaḥkāle* | **प्रातःकाले** सूर्यः उदेति। (In morning the sun rises.) |
| **In the Evening** | **सायङ्काले** | *sāyaṅkāle* | वयम् **सायङ्काले** क्रीडामः। (We play in the evening.) |
| **At Night** | **रात्रौ** | *rātrau* | सर्वे **रात्रौ** शयनं कुर्वन्ति। (Everyone sleeps at night.) |
| **On Sunday** | **रविवासरे** | *ravivāsare* | **रविवासरे** अवकाशः (holiday) अस्ति। (On Sunday is a holiday.) |
| **On Monday** | **सोमवासरे** | *somavāsare* | **सोमवासरे** वयं मेलिष्यामः। (On Monday we will meet.) |

---

### 3. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Scheduling a Meeting

* **विक्रमः (Vikram):** भो मित्र! भवान् इदानीं **कुत्र** अस्ति? गृहे वा कार्यालये वा?
  > *Bho mitra! Bhavān idānīṃ kutra asti? Gṛhe vā kāryālaye vā?*
  > *Friend! Where are you right now? At home or in the office?*
* **प्रमोदः (Pramod):** अहम् **कार्यालये** अस्मि। किम् अभवत्?
  > *Aham kāryālaye asmi. Kim abhavat?*
  > *I am in the office. What happened?*
* **विक्रमः:** वयम् **कदा** मेलिष्यामः (when shall we meet)?
  > *Vayam kadā meliṣyāmaḥ?*
  > *When shall we meet?*
* **प्रमोदः:** वयम् **सायङ्काले षड्वादने** (at 6 PM in evening) उद्याने मेलिष्यामः।
  > *Vayam sāyaṅkāle ṣaḍvādane udyāne meliṣyāmaḥ.*
  > *We will meet at 6 PM in the evening in the park.*
* **विक्रमः:** उत्तमम्! तत्रैव मेलिष्यामः।
  > *Uttamam! Tatraiva meliṣyāmaḥ.*
  > *Excellent! We shall meet right there.*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **हंसाः सरोवरे तरन्ति (haṃsāḥ sarovare taranti)**
   * **सरोवरे**: Location / Adhikaraṇa (Saptamī Sg. Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-e*)
   * *Rule*: The substrate/container of action takes **Saptamī**.

2. **अध्यापकः संस्कृत-विषये प्रवीणः (adhyāpakaḥ saṃskṛta-viṣaye pravīṇaḥ)**
   * **प्रवीण-योगे सप्तमी**: The adjective **प्रवीणः / कुशलः (skilled/proficient)** takes **Saptamī** on the domain of skill.

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Transform into Saptamī Vibhakti
1. सः _________ (गृह) अस्ति।
2. छात्राः _________ (कक्षा - fem) पठन्ति।
3. वयं _________ (रविवासर) मेलिष्यामः।
4. माता _________ (पुत्र) स्निह्यति।
5. जलम् _________ (कूपी - fem) अस्ति।

### Exercise 2: Translate into Sanskrit for Spoken Practice
1. Where are you? $\rightarrow$ I am in the office.
2. We will meet at 7 o'clock in the evening.
3. The book is on the table.
4. On Sunday there is a holiday.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. सः **गृहे** अस्ति।
2. छात्राः **कक्षायाम्** पठन्ति।
3. वयं **रविवासरे** मेलिष्यामः।
4. माता **पुत्रे** स्निह्यति।
5. जलम् **कूप्याम्** अस्ति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **भवान् कुत्र अस्ति? $\rightarrow$ अहम् कार्यालये अस्मि।**
2. **वयम् सायङ्काले सप्तवादने मेलिष्यामः।** (*Vayam sāyaṅkāle saptavādane meliṣyāmaḥ.*)
3. **पुस्तकम् उत्पीठिकायाम् अस्ति।** (*Pustakam utpīṭhikāyām asti.*)
4. **रविवासरे अवकाशः अस्ति।** (*Ravivāsare avakāśaḥ asti.*)
