# Day 007: द्वितीया विभक्तिः (Accusative Case - Daily Activities & Destinations)

Today, we explore **द्वितीया विभक्तिः (Dvitīyā Vibhakti - Accusative Case)**. In Sanskrit grammar, this case marks the **कर्म-कारकम् (Karma Kāraka)**—the direct object of an action (what or whom the action directly affects) as well as the destination of physical movement in daily life.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> रूप-यौवन-सम्पन्ना विशाल-कुल-सम्भवाः ।
> विद्याहीना न शोभन्ते निर्गन्धा इव किंशुकाः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *rūpa-yauvana-sampannā viśāla-kula-sambhavāḥ |*
> *vidyā-hīnā na śobhante nirgandhā iva kiṃśukāḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> Even those endowed with beauty and youth, born into noble lineages, do not shine if bereft of learning—just like vibrant Palasa flowers that possess no fragrance!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about an afternoon in the life of a dedicated student, Devadatta (*देवदत्तस्य दिनचर्या*):

### **ज्ञान-प्राप्तिः (Attaining Knowledge)**

**देवदत्ततः ग्रामम् त्यजति, नगरम् गच्छति च।**
> *Devadattaḥ grāmam tyajati, nagaram gacchati ca.*
> **Devadatta leaves the village and goes to the city.**

**सः गुरुकुलम् प्रविशति, आचार्यम् च नमति।**
> *Saḥ gurukulam praviśati, ācāryam ca namati.*
> **He enters the Gurukula and bows to the teacher.**

**आचार्यः छात्रान् पश्यति, तान् पाठयति च।**
> *Ācāryaḥ chātrān paśyati, tān pāṭhayati ca.*
> **The teacher looks at the students and teaches them.**

**देवदत्ततः ग्रन्थम् पठति, श्लोकम् स्मरति, लेखम् च लिखति।**
> *Devadattaḥ grantham paṭhati, ślokam smarati, lekham ca likhati.*
> **Devadatta reads the scripture, memorizes the verse, and writes an essay.**

**सायङ्काले सः गृहम् प्रति गच्छति, मातरम पितरम् च वन्दते।**
> *Sāyaṅkāle saḥ gṛham prati gacchati, mātaram pitaram ca vandate.*
> **In the evening, he heads towards home and honors his mother and father.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम् (Everyday Activities & Hospitality)

In everyday speech, nearly every sentence with a transitive verb requires the **Accusative Case (Dvitīyā)** on the object:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Action Phrases (द्वितीया-प्रयोगाः)

| Daily Action | Sanskrit Phrase (Dvitīyā Object + Verb) | IAST | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Drinking / Eating** | अहम् **चायं** पिबामि / **भोजनं** खादामि। | *Aham cāyaṃ pibāmi / bhojanaṃ khādāmi.* | I drink tea / I eat food. |
| **Reading / Writing** | सः **वार्ता-पत्रिकां** (newspaper) पठति। | *Saḥ vārtā-patrikāṃ paṭhati.* | He reads the newspaper. |
| **Watching / Calling** | सा **दूरदर्शनं** (television) पश्यति। | *Sā dūradarśanaṃ paśyati.* | She is watching TV. |
| **Contacting Friends** | अहम् **मित्रम्** आह्वयामि (call) / **सन्देशं** प्रेषयामि। | *Aham mitraṃ āhvayāmi / sandeśaṃ preṣayāmi.* | I call my friend / I send a message. |
| **Going Somewhere** | वयम् **कार्यालयं / आपणं** गच्छामः। | *Vayam kāryālayaṃ / āpaṇaṃ gacchāmaḥ.* | We are going to the office / market. |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Hospitality & Offering Food

#### Scenario A: Offering Tea or Coffee to a Guest (अतिथि-सत्कारः)
* **गृहस्थः (Host):** स्वागतम् भोः! उपविशतु। भवान् **किम्** पिबति? चायं वा काफीं वा?
  > *Svāgatam bhoḥ! Upaviśatu. Bhavān kim pibati? Cāyaṃ vā kāphīṃ vā?*
  > *Welcome sir! Please sit down. What will you drink? Tea or coffee?*
* **अतिथिः (Guest):** कृपया **चायम्** ददातु।
  > *Kṛpayā cāyam dadātu.*
  > *Please give tea.*
* **गृहस्थः:** किञ्चित् **मधुरम्** (sweets) अपि स्वीकरोतु।
  > *Kiñcit madhuram api svīkarotu.*
  > *Please accept some sweets as well.*
* **अतिथिः:** धन्यवादः! बहु मधुरम् अस्ति।
  > *Dhanyavādaḥ! Bahu madhuram asti.*
  > *Thank you! It is very delicious.*

#### Scenario B: Commuting to Work / School
* **सुनीलः (Sunil):** भवान् इदानीं **कुत्र** गच्छति?
  > *Bhavān idānīṃ kutra gacchati?*
  > *Where are you going right now?*
* **रमेशः (Ramesh):** अहम् **कार्यालयम्** गच्छामि। भवान् कुत्र गच्छति?
  > *Aham kāryālayam gacchāmi. Bhavān kutra gacchati?*
  > *I am going to the office. Where are you going?*
* **सुनीलः:** अहम् **आपणम्** (market) गच्छामि, **शाकम्** (vegetables) च क्रीणामि।
  > *Aham āpaṇam gacchāmi, śākam ca krīṇāmi.*
  > *I am going to the market and buying vegetables.*

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **देवदत्ततः नगरम् गच्छति (devadattaḥ nagaram gacchati)**
   * **देवदत्ततः**: Subject (Prathamā Sg.)
   * **नगरम्**: Destination of Motion (Dvitīyā Sg. $\rightarrow$ *-am*)
   * *Rule*: Verbs of movement (like *गम् / gam*) place the destination into **Dvitīyā**.

2. **सः ग्रन्थम् पठति (saḥ grantham paṭhati)**
   * **सः**: Subject (He)
   * **ग्रन्थम्**: Direct Object (Dvitīyā Sg. Masc. $\rightarrow$ *-am*)
   * **पठति**: Verb (reads)

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Dvitīyā Case Endings)

### 1. Complete द्वितीया विभक्तिः (Dvitīyā Vibhakti) Declensions

| Stem Type | Singular (एक.) | Dual (द्वि.) | Plural (बहु.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masc. in -a** (देव) | **देवम्** (devam) | **देवौ** (devau) | **देवान्** (devān) |
| **Fem. in -ā** (बालिका) | **बालिकाम्** (bālikām) | **बालिके** (bālike) | **बालिकाः** (bālikāḥ) |
| **Fem. in -ī** (नदी) | **नदीम्** (nadīm) | **नद्यौ** (nadyau) | **नदीः** (nadīḥ) |
| **Neut. in -a** (फल) | **फलम्** (phalam) | **फले** (phale) | **फलानि** (phalāni) |

### 2. Special Words requiring Dvitīyā (उपपद-द्वितीया)
* **प्रति (prati - towards)**: *सः गृहम् प्रति गच्छति।* (He goes towards home).
* **परितः (paritaḥ - around)**: *देवालयम् परितः वृक्षाः सन्ति।* (Around the temple are trees).
* **विना (vinā - without)**: *जलम् विना जीवनं न भवति।* (Without water there is no life).

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. बालकः **पुस्तकम्** पठति।
2. अहम् **मित्रम्** पश्यामि।
3. सा **चायम्** पिबति।
4. सा **वाटिकाम्** प्रति गच्छति।
5. जनाः **ईश्वरम्** नमन्ति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **अहम् जलम् पिबामि।** (*Aham jalam pibāmi.*)
2. **सः कार्यालयम् गच्छति।** (*Saḥ kāryālayam gacchati.*)
3. **कृपया चायम् ददातु।** (*Kṛpayā cāyam dadātu.*)
4. **सा मित्राय सन्देशम् लिखति।** (*Sā mitrāya sandeśam likhati.*)
