## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> बुद्धिर्यस्य बलं तस्य निर्बुद्धेस्तु कुतो बलम् ।
> वने सिंहो मदोन्मत्त शशकेन निपातितः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *buddhir yasya balaṃ tasya nirbuddhes tu kuto balam |*
> *vane siṃho madonmattaḥ śaśakena nipātitaḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> He who possesses intellect possesses true strength; whence comes strength to one devoid of intellect? In the forest, an arrogant, furious lion was brought down by a tiny rabbit!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Classic Panchatantra Prose)

### **सिंह-शशक-कथा (The Lion and the Rabbit)**

**एकस्मिन् वने भासुरकः नाम महाबलः सिंहः वसति स्म।**
> *Ekasmin vane Bhāsurakaḥ nāma mahābalaḥ siṃhaḥ vasati sma.*
> In a certain forest, there lived a mighty lion named Bhasuraka.

**सः नित्यम् वने बहून् मृगान् हन्ति।**
> *Saḥ nityam vane bahūn mṛgān hanti.*
> He would daily kill many animals in the forest.

**अतः सर्वे पशवः मिलित्वा सिंहस्य समीपम् अगच्छन्, अवदन् च—**
> *Ataḥ sarve paśavaḥ militvā siṃhasya samīpam agacchan, avadan ca—*
> Therefore, all animals assembled, went near the lion, and said—

**"भोः मृगराज! वयं तुभ्यम् प्रतिदिनम् एकम् पशुम् प्रेषयामः।" सिंहः तत् अङ्गीकरोति।**
> *"Bhoḥ mṛgarāja! Vayaṃ tubhyam pratidinam ekam paśum preṣayāmaḥ." Siṃhaḥ tat aṅgīkaroti.*
> "O King of Beasts! We shall send one animal to you each day." The lion agreed to that.

**एकदा एकस्य वृद्ध-शशकस्य वारः आगच्छति।**
> *Ekadā ekasya vṛddha-śaśakasya vāraḥ āgacchati.*
> One day, the turn of an old, wise rabbit arrived.

**शशकः मन्दम् मन्दम् गच्छति, विलम्बेन च सिंहस्य पुरतः उपतिष्ठते।**
> *Śaśakaḥ mandam mandam gacchati, vilambena ca siṃhasya purataḥ upatiṣṭhate.*
> The rabbit walked very slowly, and with intentional delay appeared before the lion.

**सिंहः क्रोधात् गर्जति—"रे क्षुद्र शशक! त्वम् किमर्थम् विलम्बेन आगच्छसि?"**
> *Siṃhaḥ krodhāt garjati—"Re kṣudra śaśaka! Tvam kimartham vilambena āgacchasi?"*
> The lion roared in fury—"O tiny rabbit! Why do you arrive with delay?"

**शशकः विनयेन अवदत्—"स्वामिन्! मार्गे अन्यः सिंहः माम् अवदत्—'अहम् अस्य वनस्य राजा' इति।"**
> *Śaśakaḥ vinayena avadat—"Svāmin! Mārge anyaḥ siṃhaḥ mām avadat—'Aham asya vanasya rājā' iti."*
> The rabbit said with humble respect—"My Lord! On the road another lion said to me—'I am the king of this forest.'"

**भासुरकः कोपेन तम् अवदत्—"तम् दुष्टम् कुत्र अस्ति? माम् दर्शय!"**
> *Bhāsurakaḥ kopena tam avadat—"Tam duṣṭam kutra asti? Mām darśaya!"*
> Bhasuraka said with rage to him—"Where is that wicked one? Show him to me!"

**शशकः सिंहम् एकस्य गभीरस्य कूपस्य समीपम् आनयति, अकथयत् च—"महाराज! अस्मिन् कूपे सः निवसति।"**
> *Śaśakaḥ siṃham ekasya gabhīrasya kūpasya samīpam ānayati, akathayat ca—"Mahārāja! Asmin kūpe saḥ nivasati."*
> The rabbit led the lion near a deep well and said—"Great King! He resides in this well."

**सिंहः कूपस्य जले स्व-प्रतिबिम्बम् पश्यति। सः गर्जति, कूपे च कूर्दते, मृतः च भवति।**
> *Siṃhaḥ kūpasya jale sva-pratibimbam paśyati. Saḥ garjati, kūpe ca kūrdate, mṛtaḥ ca bhavati.*
> The lion saw his own reflection in the water of the well. He roared, leaped into the well, and perished.

**सर्वे पशवः शशकेन सह आनन्देन अवसन्।**
> *Sarve paśavaḥ śaśakena saha ānandena avasan.*
> All animals lived happily along with the clever rabbit.

## 🔍 Comprehensive Case Identification in the Story

Look at how the story naturally exercises every single Vibhakti:

| Text Fragment | Case Used | Grammatical Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **एकस्मिन् वने / कूपे / जले** | सप्तमी (Locative) | *Adhikaraṇa* (Location where action occurs) |
| **भासुरकः सिंहः / शशकः** | प्रथमा (Nominative) | *Kartā* (Doer of the actions) |
| **मृगान् / सिंहम् / प्रतिबिम्बम्** | द्वितीया (Accusative) | *Karma* (Direct objects of killing, seeing, leading) |
| **विनयेन / क्रोधात् (कोपेन) / शशकेन** | तृतीया (Instrumental) | *Karaṇa* (With humility, with fury, together with rabbit) |
| **तुभ्यम्** | चतुर्थी (Dative) | *Sampradāna* (Recipient of sending / giving) |
| **क्रोधात् / मार्गात्** | पञ्चमी (Ablative) | *Apādāna* (Out of / source of anger) |
| **सिंहस्य / कूपस्य / अस्य वनस्य** | षष्ठी (Genitive) | *Sambandha* (Possession: of the lion, of the well, of this forest) |
| **भोः मृगराज! / रे शशक! / स्वामिन्!** | सम्बोधन (Vocative) | Direct address to king, rabbit, master |

## 🏆 Phase 2 Mastery Milestone Checklist

You have successfully mastered the complete 8-case system of Sanskrit!
- [x] **Prathamā (1st)**: Identify subjects across Masc, Fem, and Neut nouns.
- [x] **Dvitīyā (2nd)**: Express direct objects and destinations of motion.
- [x] **Tṛtīyā (3rd)**: Use instruments (*with pen, hand*) and accompaniment (*saha*).
- [x] **Caturthī (4th)**: Use recipients (*giving*), goals (*study*), and salutations (*namaḥ*).
- [x] **Pañcamī (5th)**: Express departure (*from tree*), fear (*of lion*), and comparison.
- [x] **Ṣaṣṭhī (6th)**: Express ownership (*Rama's*), relations, and spatial positions (*upari, adhaḥ*).
- [x] **Saptamī (7th)**: Express location (*in lake*), time, affection (*snih*), and trust.
- [x] **Sambodhana (8th)**: Address people respectfully (*he Rāma, he Sīte, he janani*).

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. वने **भासुरकः नाम सिंहः** वसति स्म।
2. **एकस्य वृद्ध-शशकस्य** वारः आगच्छति।
3. सिंहः कूपस्य जले **स्व-प्रतिबिम्बम्** पश्यति।
4. शशकः **सर्वैः पशुभिः सह** आनन्देन वसति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. नृपः स्व-कोशात् छात्रेभ्यः धनम् ददाति। (*Nṛpaḥ sva-kośāt chātrebhyaḥ dhanam dadāti.*)
2. गृहस्य पुरतः वृक्षे खगाः गायन्ति। (*Gṛhasya purataḥ vṛkṣe khagāḥ gāyanti.*)
3. हे मित्र! वयम् शिक्षकेन सह सरोवरम् गच्छाम! (*He mitra! Vayam śikṣakena saha sarovaram gacchāma!*)
