# Day 011: षष्ठी विभक्तिः (Genitive Case - Belongings, Directions & Landscape Vlog)

Welcome to Day 11! Today, we explore **षष्ठी विभक्तिः (Ṣaṣṭhī Vibhakti - Genitive Case)**. In Sanskrit grammar, this case denotes **सम्बन्धः (Sambandha - Possession & Relationship)**—indicating "*whose something is*" or "*of whom/what*".

It is also used with **Positional Adverbs** to give exact spatial directions and camera panning cues in **outdoor travel vlogging** (**पुरतः, पृष्ठतः, दक्षिणतः, वामतः, उपरि, अधः**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> छायामन्यस्य कुर्वन्ति तिष्ठन्ति स्वयमातपे ।
> फलान्यपि परार्थाय वृक्षाः सत्पुरुषा इव ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *chāyām anyasya kurvanti tiṣṭhanti svayam ātape |*
> *phalāny api parārthāya vṛkṣāḥ satpuruṣā iva ||*

**English Translation:**
> Trees provide shade **for others** (*anyasya*), while standing in the scorching heat themselves. Their fruits too are for the benefit of others; trees are verily like noble souls!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about the location and surroundings of a traditional home (*रामस्य गृहम्*):

### **गृहस्य परितः (Around the House)**

**इदम् **रामस्य** गृहम् अस्ति। **गृहस्य पुरतः** (in front of the house) सुन्दरम् उद्यानम् अस्ति।**
> *Idam Rāmasya gṛham asti. Gṛhasya purataḥ sundaram udyānam asti.*
> **This is Rama's house. In front of the house is a beautiful garden.**

**उद्यानस्य **उपरि** (above) खगाः उत्पतन्ति, **वृक्षस्य अधः** (under the tree) च बालकाः खेलन्ति।**
> *Udyānasya upari khagāḥ utpatanti, vṛkṣasya adhaḥ ca bālakāḥ khelanti.*
> **Above the garden, birds fly, and under the tree children play.**

**गृहस्य **दक्षिणतः** (to the right of the house) देवालयः अस्ति, **वामतः** (to the left) च ग्रन्थालयः अस्ति।**
> *Gṛhasya dakṣiṇataḥ devālayaḥ asti, vāmataḥ ca granthālayaḥ asti.*
> **To the right of the house is a temple, and to the left is a library.**

**एषा **रामस्य भगिनी**। एतस्याः नाम सीता। एषः **सीतायाः भ्राता** लक्ष्मणः।**
> *Eṣā Rāmasya bhaginī. Etasyāḥ nāma Sītā. Eṣaḥ Sītāyāḥ bhrātā Lakṣmaṇaḥ.*
> **This is Rama's sister. Her name is Sita. This is Sita's brother Lakshmana.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: स्थान-निर्देशः (Spatial Directions in Daily Speech)

In daily life, the Genitive Case (*Ṣaṣṭhī*) is used continuously to clarify ownership and give navigation directions:

### 1. The 6 Spatial Direction Words (takes Ṣaṣṭhī Case)

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Direction Word} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Example with Genitive (षष्ठी-प्रयोगः)} \\
\hline
\textbf{पुरतः (purataḥ)} & \text{in front of} & \textbf{गृहस्य पुरतः} \text{ कार-यानम् अस्ति। (In front of the house is the car.)} \\
\textbf{पृष्ठतः (pṛṣṭhataḥ)} & \text{behind / at back} & \textbf{कार्यालयस्य पृष्ठतः} \text{ वृक्षः अस्ति। (Behind the office is a tree.)} \\
\textbf{दक्षिणतः (dakṣiṇataḥ)}& \text{to the right of} & \textbf{मार्गस्य दक्षिणतः} \text{ आपणः अस्ति। (To the right of road is a shop.)} \\
\textbf{वामतः (vāmataḥ)} & \text{to the left of} & \textbf{देवालयस्य वामतः} \text{ सरः अस्ति। (To the left of temple is lake.)} \\
\textbf{उपरि (upari)} & \text{above / on top of}& \textbf{उत्पीठिकायाः उपरि} \text{ सङ्गणकम् अस्ति। (On table is computer.)} \\
\textbf{अधः (adhaḥ)} & \text{below / under} & \textbf{आसनस्य अधः} \text{ मार्जारः अस्ति। (Under chair is cat.)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

## 🎥 व्लॉगिङ्ग्-संस्कृतम्: स्थल-अन्वेषणम् (Landscape & Monument Exploration Vlog)

Filming an outdoor landscape or temple exploration vlog? Use these camera panning cues:

### 1. Landscape Exploration Vlog Dialogue

> **"भो दर्शकाः! पश्यन्तु, वयम् अधुना ऐतिहासिक-स्थलस्य (historic site) समीपे स्मः!"**
> *Bho darśakāḥ! Paśyantu, vayam adhunā aitihāsika-sthalasya samīpe smaḥ!*
> **"Hey viewers! Look, we are now near a historic heritage site!"**

> **"देवालयस्य **पुरतः** (in front of the temple) विशालः सरोवरः अस्ति।"**
> *Devālayasya purataḥ viśālaḥ sarovaraḥ asti.*
> **"In front of the temple is a huge lake."**

> **"देवालयस्य **पृष्ठतः** (behind the temple) उन्नत-पर्वतः शोभते!"**
> *Devālayasya pṛṣṭhataḥ unnata-parvataḥ śobhate!*
> **"Behind the temple stands a magnificent mountain!"**

> **"मार्गस्य **दक्षिणतः** (to the right of the path) सुन्दरम् उद्यानम् अस्ति, **वामतः** (to the left) च पारम्परिक-आपणाः सन्ति।"**
> *Mārgasya dakṣiṇataḥ sundaram udyānam asti, vāmataḥ ca pāramparika-āpaṇāḥ santi.*
> **"To the right of the path is a beautiful garden, and to the left are traditional shops."**

> **"एतत् ३६०-डिग्री दृश्यम् (360-degree view) पश्यन्तु! कियत् अद्भुतम्!"**
> *Etat 360-degree dṛśyaṃ paśyantu! Kiyat adbhutam!*
> **"Look at this 360-degree view! How miraculous and stunning!"**

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Case Analysis)

1. **गृहस्य पुरतः उद्यानम् अस्ति (gṛhasya purataḥ udyānam asti)**
   * **गृहस्य**: Genitive Sg. Neut. (*of the house*)
   * **पुरतः**: Directional Avyaya (*in front of*)
   * *Rule*: The word **पुरतः** governs **Ṣaṣṭhī** on the landmark.

2. **एषा सीतायाः भगिनी (eṣā sītāyāḥ bhaginī)**
   * **सीतायाः**: Genitive Sg. Fem. (*of Sita / Sita's*)
   * **भगिनी**: Sister

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. एतत् **कृष्णस्य** गृहम् अस्ति।
2. **देवालयस्य** पुरतः विशालः सरोवरः अस्ति।
3. उत्पीठिकायाः **उपरि** सङ्गणकम् अस्ति।
4. मार्गस्य **वामतः** आपणः अस्ति।
5. एषा **राधायाः** माता अस्ति।

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **देवालयस्य पुरतः सुन्दरम् उद्यानम् अस्ति।** (*Devālayasya purataḥ sundaram udyānam asti.*)
2. **पर्वतस्य पृष्ठतः सूर्यः उदेति।** (*Parvatasya pṛṣṭhataḥ sūryaḥ udeti.*)
3. **मार्गस्य दक्षिणतः उपहारगृहम् अस्ति।** (*Mārgasya dakṣiṇataḥ upahāragṛham asti.*)
4. **एतत् दृश्यं पश्यन्तु, कियत् अद्भुतम्!** (*Etat dṛśyaṃ paśyantu, kiyat adbhutam!*)
