# Day 027: पञ्चतन्त्रम् - ब्राह्मण-नकुल-कथा (The Brahmana & Mongoose: Ktvā Sequential Actions)

Welcome to Day 27! Today, we explore one of the most famous stories from the fifth book of the Panchatantra (*अपरीक्षित-कारकम् - Action without Investigation*): **ब्राह्मण-नकुल-कथा (The Brahmana and the Loyal Mongoose)**.

We also focus deeply on **क्त्वा प्रत्ययः (Ktvā Gerunds)** to express smooth sequential actions in daily speech ("*having done X, I do Y*") without stacking dozens of clumsy full verbs.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

This classic verse warns against acting impulsively in haste:

**संस्कृतम्:**
> सहसा विदधीत न क्रियां विवेकः परमापदां पदम् ।
> वृणुते हि विमृश्यकारिणं गुणलुब्धाः स्वयमेव सम्पदः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *sahasā vidadhīta na kriyām avivekaḥ paramāpadāṃ padam |*
> *vṛṇute hi vimṛśyakāriṇaṃ guṇalubdhāḥ svayam eva sampadaḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> One should never perform an action in rash haste. Lack of deliberation is the greatest abode of disaster. Wealth and success, enamored of virtues, naturally choose of their own accord one who acts with thoughtful reflection!

## 📖 मूल-कथा (Original Panchatantra Prose)

### **ब्राह्मणः नकुलः च (The Brahmana and the Mongoose)**

**एकस्मिन् नगरे देवशर्मा नाम एकः विप्रः (ब्राह्मणः) अवसत्।**
> *Ekasmin nagare Devaśarmā nāma ekaḥ vipraḥ (brāhmaṇaḥ) avasat.*
> In a city, there lived a Brahmana named Devasarma.

**तस्य भार्या एका सुपुत्रम् प्रासूत। तस्मिन्नेव गृहे एका नकुली स्व-शावकम् (नकुलम्) अपि तत्याज।**
> *Tasya bhāryā ekā suputram prāsūta. Tasminn eva gṛhe ekā nakulī sva-śāvakam (nakulam) api tatyāja.*
> His wife gave birth to a beloved son. In that very house, a female mongoose also gave birth to a baby mongoose and abandoned it.

**ब्राह्मणी तं नकुलम् स्व-पुत्रवत् स्नेहेन अपालयत्।**
> *Brāhmaṇī taṃ nakulam sva-putravat snehena apālayat.*
> The Brahmana's wife raised that mongoose with affection just like her own son.

**एकदा ब्राह्मणी जलम् आनेतुम् **नदीम् गत्वा** भर्तारम् (पतिम्) अवदत्—"स्वामिन्! अहम् नदीम् गच्छामि, त्वम् शिशुम् रक्ष।"**
> *Ekadā brāhmaṇī jalam ānetum nadīm gatvā bhartāram (patim) avadat—"Svāmin! Aham nadīm gacchāmi, tvam śiśum rakṣa."*
> Once, the wife, **having gone to the river** to fetch water, told her husband—"Husband! I am going to the river, please protect our baby."

**तदनन्तरम् ब्राह्मणः अपि भिक्षार्थम् नगरे **गत्वा** गृहात् बहिः अगच्छत्।**
> *Tadanantaram brāhmaṇaḥ api bhikṣārtham nagare gatvā gṛhāt bahiḥ agacchat.*
> Thereafter, the Brahmana too, **having gone** into the town for alms, departed from the house.

**तस्मिन् अवसरे एकः कृष्ण-सर्पः बिलम् त्यक्त्वा शिशोः शय्यायाः समीपम् आगच्छत्।**
> *Tasmin avasare ekaḥ kṛṣṇa-sarpaḥ bilam tyaktvā śiśoḥ śayyāyāḥ samīpam āgacchat.*
> At that moment, a black cobra, **having left** its hole, approached near the child's cradle.

**नकुलः तं सर्पं **दृष्ट्वा** तस्य उपरि आक्रम्य तं खण्डशः **कृत्वा** अमारयत्।**
> *Nakulaḥ taṃ sarpaṃ dṛṣṭvā tasya upari ākramya taṃ khaṇḍaśaḥ kṛtvā amārayat.*
> The mongoose, **having seen** the snake, attacked it, **having torn** it to pieces, and killed it.

**रक्त-रञ्जित-मुखः नकुलः स्वामिनीम् **दृष्ट्वा** आनन्देन तस्याः प्रत्युद्गमनाय धावत्।**
> *Rakta-rañjita-mukhaḥ nakulaḥ svāminīm dṛṣṭvā ānandena tasyāḥ pratyudgamanāya adhāvat.*
> The mongoose, with its face smeared with blood, **having seen** the mistress return, ran with joy to greet her.

**ब्राह्मणी तस्य रक्त-मुखम् **दृष्ट्वा** "मम पुत्रः अनेन हतः" इति **मत्वा** क्रोधात् जल-कुम्भम् तस्य उपरि अक्षिपत्।**
> *Brāhmaṇī tasya rakta-mukham dṛṣṭvā "Mama putraḥ anena hataḥ" iti matvā krodhāt jala-kumbham tasya upari akṣipat.*
> The mother, **having seen** its bloody mouth and **thinking** "My son has been killed by this creature", threw the heavy water pot on it in blind rage.

**नकुलः तत्क्षणादेव मृतः अभवत्।**
> *Nakulaḥ tat-kṣaṇād eva mṛtaḥ abhavat.*
> The mongoose died that very instant.

**यदा सा अन्तः **प्रविश्य** पश्यति, तदा शिशुः सुखेन क्रीडति, भूमौ च मृतः सर्पः पतितः आसीत्।**
> *Yadā sā antaḥ praviśya paśyati, tadā śiśuḥ sukhena krīḍati, bhūmau ca mṛtaḥ sarpaḥ patitaḥ āsīt.*
> When she entered inside and looked, her baby was playing peacefully, and on the ground lay the dead serpent.

**सा महता दुःखेना आत्मानम् अनिन्दत्, उच्चैः च अरोदत्।**
> *Sā mahatā duḥkhena ātmānam anindat, uccaiḥ ca arodat.*
> She blamed herself in immense sorrow and wept bitterly.

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: दैनन्दिन-कार्याणां क्रमः (Daily Sequential Activities with Ktvā)

In daily speech, whenever you describe doing one thing after another, use **Ktvā (क्त्वा)**:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Sequential Templates

| Sequential Daily Actions | Sanskrit Spoken Formula (Ktvā) | IAST | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bath $\rightarrow$ Worship** | अहम् **स्नानं कृत्वा** पूजां करोमि। | *Aham snānaṃ kṛtvā pūjāṃ karomi.* | Having bathed, I perform worship. |
| **Breakfast $\rightarrow$ Office** | सः **अल्पाहारं खादित्वा** कार्यालयं गच्छति।| *Saḥ alpāhāraṃ khāditvā kāryālayaṃ gacchati.* | Having eaten breakfast, he goes to office. |
| **Reading $\rightarrow$ Sleep** | वयम् **पुस्तकं पठित्वा** शयनं कुर्मः। | *Vayam pustakaṃ paṭhitvā śayanaṃ kurmaḥ.* | Having read the book, we go to sleep. |
| **Seeing $\rightarrow$ Smiling** | सा **मित्रं दृष्ट्वा** हसति। | *Sā mitraṃ dṛṣṭvā hasati.* | Having seen her friend, she smiles. |
| **Hearing $\rightarrow$ Joy** | अहम् **वार्तां श्रुत्वा** प्रसन्नः अस्मि। | *Aham vārtāṃ śrutvā prasannaḥ asmi.* | Having heard the news, I am joyful. |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogue: What Did You Do This Morning?

* **अमितः (Amit):** भवान् प्रातःकाले **किम् कृत्वा** (after doing what) अत्र आगच्छत्?
  > *Bhavān prātaḥkāle kim kṛtvā atra āgacchat?*
  > *Having done what did you come here this morning?*
* **सुमितः (Sumit):** अहम् प्रातः **योगाभ्यासं कृत्वा**, **चायं पीत्वा** च अत्र आगच्छम्।
  > *Aham prātaḥ yogābhyāsaṃ kṛtvā, cāyaṃ pītvā ca atra āgaccham.*
  > *Having done yoga and having drunk tea in the morning, I came here.*
* **अमितः:** उत्तमम्! अधुना **अल्पाहारं खादित्वा** वयं कार्यम् आरभामहै।
  > *Uttamam! Adhunā alpāhāraṃ khāditvā vayam kāryam ārabhāmahai.*
  > *Great! Now having eaten breakfast, let us begin our work.*

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Combine two sentences into one using Ktvā (क्त्वा)
1. सा जलम् पिबति। सा गच्छति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
2. नकुलः सर्पम् पश्यति। नकुलः आक्रमति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
3. विप्रः पुस्तकम् पठति। विप्रः पाठयति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
4. अहम् स्नानं करोमि। अहम् भोजनं करोमि। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________

### Exercise 2: Translate into Spoken Sanskrit
1. Having eaten food, he went to office.
2. Having seen his friend, the boy smiled.
3. Having drunk water, she sat down.
4. Having heard the story, we were pleased.

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. सा जलम् **पीत्वा** गच्छति। (*Sā jalam pītvā gacchati.*)
2. नकुलः सर्पम् **दृष्ट्वा** आक्रमति। (*Nakulaḥ sarpam dṛṣṭvā ākramati.*)
3. विप्रः पुस्तकम् **पठित्वा** पाठयति। (*Vipraḥ pustakam paṭhitvā pāṭhayati.*)
4. अहम् स्नानं **कृत्वा** भोजनं करोमि। (*Aham snānaṃ kṛtvā bhojanaṃ karomi.*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **भोजनम् खादित्वा सः कार्यालयम् अगच्छत्।** (*Bhojanam khāditvā saḥ kāryālayam agacchat.*)
2. **मित्रम् दृष्ट्वा बालकः अहसत्।** (*Mitram dṛṣṭvā bālakaḥ ahasat.*)
3. **जलम् पीत्वा सा उपाविशत्।** (*Jalam pītvā sā upāviśat.*)
4. **कथाम् श्रुत्वा वयम् प्रसन्नाः अभवाम।** (*Kathām śrutvā vayam prasannāḥ abhavāma.*)
