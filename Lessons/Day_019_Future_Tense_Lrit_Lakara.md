# Day 019: लृट्-लकारः (Future Tense - Tomorrow's Plans & Aspirations)

Welcome to Day 19! Today, we explore **लृट्-लकारः (Lṛṭ Lakāra - Simple Future Tense)**. This tense allows you to make appointments, discuss weekend plans, make weather predictions, and share your goals and aspirations using future time markers like **श्वः (śvaḥ - tomorrow)** and **परश्वः (paraśvaḥ - day after tomorrow)**.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> श्वः कार्यमद्य कुर्वीत पूर्वाह्णे चापराह्णिकम् ।
> न हि प्रतीक्षते मृत्युः कृतमस्य न वा कृतम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *śvaḥ kāryam adya kurvīta pūrvāhṇe cāparāhṇikam |*
> *na hi pratīkṣate mṛtyuḥ kṛtam asya na vā kṛtam ||*

**English Translation:**
> What is to be done tomorrow should be accomplished today; what is slated for the afternoon should be completed in the morning. For time and death never wait to see whether one's work is finished or unfinished!

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read about what the students will do tomorrow (*श्वः छात्राः किम् करिष्यन्ति?*):

### **श्वस्तनी योजना (Tomorrow's Plans)**

**श्वः सोमवासरः भविष्यति।**
> *Śvaḥ somavāsaraḥ bhaviṣyati.*
> **Tomorrow will be Monday.**

**अहम् प्रातःकाले पञ्चवादने उत्थास्यामि, सूर्यनमस्कारं च करिष्यामि।**
> *Aham prātaḥkāle pañcavādane utthāsyāmi, sūryanamaskāraṃ ca kariṣyāmi.*
> **I will wake up at 5 AM in the morning, and do Sun Salutations.**

**आवाम् विद्यालये नूतनं पाठं **पठिष्यावः**, पुस्तिकासु **लेखिष्यावः** च।**
> *Āvām vidyālaye nūtanaṃ pāṭhaṃ paṭhiṣyāvaḥ, pustikāsu lekhiṣyāvaḥ ca.*
> **We two will study a new lesson in school, and write in our notebooks.**

**छात्राः क्रीडाङ्गणे कन्दुकेन **क्रीडिष्यन्ति**, जलं च **पास्यन्ति**।**
> *Chātrāḥ krīḍāṅgaṇe kandukena krīḍiṣyanti, jalaṃ ca pāsyanti.*
> **The students will play with a ball on the playground, and drink water.**

**सायङ्काले सर्वे बालकाः गृहं **गमिष्यन्ति**, माता च मिष्टान्नं **दास्यति**।**
> *Sāyaṅkāle sarve bālakāḥ gṛhaṃ gamiṣyanti, mātā ca miṣṭānnaṃ dāsyati.*
> **In the evening, all the boys will go home, and mother will give sweets.**

## 🗣️ व्यावहारिक-सम्भाषणम्: श्वः किम् करिष्यति? (Making Future Plans & Schedules)

In daily life, discussing what will happen tomorrow or this coming weekend is essential:

### 1. High-Frequency Spoken Future Actions (लृट्-लकार-प्रयोगाः)

| Future Action | Sanskrit Future Sentence | IAST | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tomorrow's Weather** | श्वः **वृष्टिः भविष्यति**। | *Śvaḥ vṛṣṭiḥ bhaviṣyati.* | Tomorrow it will rain. |
| **Travel Plan** | परश्वः अहम् **दिल्ली-नगरं गमिष्यामि**।| *Paraśvaḥ aham Dillī-nagaraṃ gamiṣyāmi.* | Day after tomorrow I will go to Delhi. |
| **Dining Together** | वयम् सायं **एकत्र भोजनं करिष्यामः**।| *Vayam sāyaṃ ekatra bhojanaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.* | We will eat dinner together in the evening. |
| **Calling Tomorrow** | अहम् श्वः **दूरवाणीं करिष्यामि**। | *Aham śvaḥ dūravāṇīṃ kariṣyāmi.* | I will call you tomorrow. |
| **Studying for Exam** | सा श्वः **परीक्षा-कृते पठिष्यति**। | *Sā śvaḥ parīkṣā-kṛte paṭhiṣyati.* | She will study for the exam tomorrow. |

---

### 2. Conversational Mini-Dialogues: Weekend Planning

* **समीरः (Sameer):** भो मित्र! श्वः रविवासरे भवान् **किम् करिष्यति**?
  > *Bho mitra! Śvaḥ ravivāsare bhavān kim kariṣyati?*
  > *Friend! What will you do tomorrow on Sunday?*
* **नकुलः (Nakul):** श्वः अहम् प्रातःकाले **ग्रन्थालयम् गमिष्यामि**। भवान् किम् करिष्यति?
  > *Śvaḥ aham prātaḥkāle granthālayam gamiṣyāmi. Bhavān kim kariṣyati?*
  > *Tomorrow in the morning I will go to the library. What will you do?*
* **समीरः:** श्वः सायं वयम् **उद्याने मेलिष्यामः**। किम् भवान् आगमिष्यति?
  > *Śvaḥ sāyaṃ vayam udyāne meliṣyāmaḥ. Kim bhavān āgamiṣyati?*
  > *Tomorrow evening we will meet in the park. Will you come?*
* **नकुलः:** आम्! अहम् अवश्यम् **आगमिष्यामि** (I will definitely come)!
  > *Ām! Aham avaśyam āgamiṣyāmi!*
  > *Yes! I will definitely come!*

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (The Complete 3x3 Future Tense Matrix)

In the Future Tense, verbs insert **-इष्य- (-iṣya-)** or **-स्य- (-sya-)** before the standard endings:

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Person (पुरुषः)} & \textbf{Singular (एक.)} & \textbf{Dual (द्वि.)} & \textbf{Plural (बहु.)} \\
\hline
\textbf{3rd (प्रथमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{पठिष्यति} \text{ (will read)} & \textbf{पठिष्यतः} \text{ (2 will read)} & \textbf{पठिष्यन्ति} \text{ (they will read)} \\
\textbf{2nd (मध्यमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{पठिष्यसि} \text{ (you will read)} & \textbf{पठिष्यथः} \text{ (you 2 will read)}& \textbf{पठिष्यथ} \text{ (you all will read)} \\
\textbf{1st (उत्तमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{पठिष्यामि} \text{ (I will read)} & \textbf{पठिष्यावः} \text{ (we 2 will read)}& \textbf{पठिष्यामः} \text{ (we all will read)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

### High-Frequency Irregular Future Verbs:
* **गम् $\rightarrow$ गमिष्यति** (will go)
* **पा $\rightarrow$ पास्यति** (will drink)
* **दृश् $\rightarrow$ द्रक्ष्यति** (will see)
* **दा $\rightarrow$ दास्यति** (will give)
* **कृ $\rightarrow$ करिष्यति** (will do)
* **भू $\rightarrow$ भविष्यति** (will be / become)

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. सः **गमिष्यति** | अहम् **गमिष्यामि**
2. सः **लेखिष्यति** | अहम् **लेखिष्यामि**
3. सः **पास्यति** | अहम् **पास्यामि**
4. सः **करिष्यति** | अहम् **करिष्यामि**

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. **श्वः अहम् कार्यालयम् गमिष्यामि।** (*Śvaḥ aham kāryālayam gamiṣyāmi.*)
2. **श्वः भवान् किम् करिष्यति?** (*Śvaḥ bhavān kim kariṣyati?*)
3. **वयम् सायङ्काले अवश्यम् मेलिष्यामः।** (*Vayam sāyaṅkāle avaśyam meliṣyāmaḥ.*)
4. **श्वः अवकाशः भविष्यति।** (*Śvaḥ avakāśaḥ bhaviṣyati.*)
