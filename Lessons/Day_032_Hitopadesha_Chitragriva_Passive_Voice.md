# Day 032: हितोपदेशः - चित्रग्रीव-कथा (King Chitragriva: Passive Voice)

Welcome to Day 32! Today, we study the inspiring story of unity from Hitopadesha: **कपोतराजः चित्रग्रीवः (Chitragriva and the Pigeon Flock)**.

We also unlock **कर्मणि प्रयोगः (Karmaṇi Prayoga - The Passive Voice)**: the elegant voice transformation where verbs take **-यते (-yate)** and agree with the object (**क्रियते, दृश्यते, पठ्यते, दीयते**).

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> संहतिः श्रेयसी पुंसां स्वकुलैरल्पकैरपि ।
> तुषेणापि परित्यक्ता न प्ररोहन्ति तण्डुलाः ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *saṃhatiḥ śreyasī puṃsāṃ svakulair alpakair api |*
> *tuṣeṇāpi parityaktā na prarohanti taṇḍulāḥ ||*

**English Translation:**
> Unity among people, even with small and humble kin, brings highest welfare. Verily, rice grains stripped of their protective husk can never sprout again!

---

## 📖 मूल-कथा (Original Hitopadeśa Prose)

### **जाल-बद्ध-कपोताः (The Pigeons in the Net)**

**एकदा व्याधेन अरण्ये तण्डुल-कणाः (rice grains) विकीर्णाः, जालम् च विस्तारितम्।**
> *Ekadā vyādhena araṇye taṇḍula-kaṇāḥ vikīrṇāḥ, jālam ca vistāritam.*
> Once, rice grains were scattered and a net was spread in the forest by a hunter.

**तण्डुलान् दृष्ट्वा कपोताः लोभेन भूतले **अवतताः**। सर्वे कपोताः जाले **बद्धाः अभवन्**।**
> *Taṇḍulān dṛṣṭvā kapotāḥ lobhena bhūtale avatatāḥ. Sarve kapotāḥ jāle baddhāḥ abhavan.*
> Seeing the rice grains, the pigeons descended to the ground in greed. All pigeons became trapped in the net.

**तदा कपोतराजः चित्रग्रीवः अवदत्—"अस्माभिः विषादः न **कर्तव्यः** (should not be made). सर्वे युगपत् जालम् आदाय **उत्पतन्तु**!"**
> *Tadā kapotarājaḥ Citragrīvaḥ avadat—"Asmābhiḥ viṣādaḥ na kartavyaḥ. Sarve yugapat jālam ādāya utpatantu!"*
> Then the pigeon-king Chitragriva said—"Sorrow should not be entertained by us. May everyone fly up together carrying the net!"

**कपोतैः एकचित्तीभूय जालम् आदाय आकाशे **उड्डीयते** (उद्डीतम्)।**
> *Kapotaiḥ ekacittībhūya jālam ādāya ākāśe uḍḍīyate.*
> By the united pigeons, carrying the net, flight was taken into the sky.

**चित्रग्रीवस्य मित्रम् हिरण्यकः नाम मूषकराजः आसीत्।**
> *Citragrīvasya mitram Hiraṇyakaḥ nāma mūṣakarājaḥ āsīt.*
> Chitragriva had a friend named Hiranyaka, a king of mice.

**हिरण्यकेन स्व-तीक्ष्ण-दन्तैः जालम् **छिद्यते** (was cut)।**
> *Hiraṇyakena sva-tīkṣṇa-dantaiḥ jālam chidyate.*
> By Hiranyaka, with his sharp teeth, the net is cut.

**सर्वैः कपोतैः मुक्तिः **प्राप्यते**। तैः चित्रग्रीवस्य जयघोषः **क्रियते**।**
> *Sarvaiḥ kapotaiḥ muktiḥ prāpyate. Taiḥ Citragrīvasya jayaghoṣaḥ kriyate.*
> Freedom is attained by all pigeons. The victory shout of Chitragriva is made by them!

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Mastering कर्मणि प्रयोगः - Passive Voice)

In Sanskrit, shifting from **Active (कर्तरि)** to **Passive (कर्मणि)** follows a simple mathematical rule:

$$\begin{array}{|l|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Voice} & \textbf{Subject (कर्ता)} & \textbf{Object (कर्म)} & \textbf{Verb (क्रिया)} \\
\hline
\textbf{Active (कर्तरि)} & \textbf{1st Case (प्रथमा)} & \textbf{2nd Case (द्वितीया)} & \text{Agrees with \textbf{Subject} (-ति/-न्ति)} \\
\text{Example:} & \text{रामः} & \text{ग्रन्थम्} & \text{पठति।} \\
\hline
\textbf{Passive (कर्मणि)} & \textbf{3rd Case (तृतीया)} & \textbf{1st Case (प्रथमा)} & \text{Root} + \mathbf{-य-} + \text{Ātmanepada (-ते/-न्ते)} \\
\text{Example:} & \textbf{रामेण} & \textbf{ग्रन्थः} & \textbf{पठ्यते।} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

---

### Key Passive Verb Conjugations

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Root} & \textbf{Active Form (कर्तरि)} & \textbf{Passive Form (कर्मणि)} & \textbf{Passive Meaning} \\
\hline
\textbf{पठ् (paṭh)} & \text{पठति (reads)} & \textbf{पठ्यते} \text{ (paṭhyate)} & \text{is read} \\
\textbf{लिख् (likh)} & \text{लिखति (writes)} & \textbf{लिख्यते} \text{ (likhyate)} & \text{is written} \\
\textbf{कृ (kṛ)} & \text{करोति (does)} & \textbf{क्रियते} \text{ (kriyate)} & \text{is done} \\
\textbf{दृश् (dṛś)} & \text{पश्यति (sees)} & \textbf{दृश्यते} \text{ (dṛśyate)} & \text{is seen} \\
\textbf{श्रु (śru)} & \text{शृणोति (hears)} & \textbf{श्रूयते} \text{ (śrūyate)} & \text{is heard} \\
\textbf{दा (dā)} & \text{ददाति (gives)} & \textbf{दीयते} \text{ (dīyate)} & \text{is given} \\
\textbf{गम् (gam)} & \text{गच्छति (goes)} & \textbf{गम्यते} \text{ (gamyate)} & \text{is gone} \\
\textbf{खाद् (khād)} & \text{खादति (eats)} & \textbf{खाद्यते} \text{ (khādyate)} & \text{is eaten} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

*Plural Object Example*: **छात्रेण पुस्तकानि पठ्यन्ते।** (*Books are read by the student $\rightarrow$ verb takes -न्ते to match plural books!*).

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Transform Active (कर्तरि) sentences into Passive (कर्मणि)
*Example*: बालकः ग्रन्थम् पठति। $\rightarrow$ **बालकेन ग्रन्थः पठ्यते।**
1. सीता पत्रम् लिखति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
2. रामः ईश्वरम् नमति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
3. छात्राः पाठम् शृण्वन्ति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________
4. जननी भोजनम् पचति। $\rightarrow$ ___________________________

### Exercise 2: Translate into Sanskrit using Passive Voice
1. The book is read by me.
2. The song is heard by everyone.
3. Good work is done by the king.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. **सीतया पत्रम् लिख्यते।** (*Sītayā patram likhyate.*)
2. **रामेण ईश्वरः नम्यते।** (*Rāmeṇa īśvaraḥ namyate.*)
3. **छात्रैः पाठः श्रूयते।** (*Chātraiḥ pāṭhaḥ śrūyate.*)
4. **जनन्या भोजनम् पच्यते।** (*Jananyā bhojanam pacyate.*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. मया पुस्तकम् पठ्यते। (*Mayā pustakam paṭhyate.*)
2. सर्वैः गीतम् श्रूयते। (*Sarvaiḥ gītam śrūyate.*)
3. नृपेण सत्कार्यम् क्रियते। (*Nṛpeṇa satkāryam kriyate.*)
