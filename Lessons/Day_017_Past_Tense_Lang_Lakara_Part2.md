# Day 017: लङ्-लकारः - द्वितीयभागः (Past Tense - Part 2: 1st & 2nd Persons)

Welcome to Day 17! In Day 16, we conquered 3rd-person past tense forms. Today, we complete the full **$3\times3$ Past Tense Matrix (लङ् लकारः)** by adding the **2nd Person (You)** and **1st Person (I / We)** conjugations.

---

## 🌟 Subhāṣitam (Good Saying of the Day)

**संस्कृतम्:**
> हस्तस्य भूषणं दानं सत्यं कण्ठस्य भूषणम् ।
> श्रोत्रस्य भूषणं शास्त्रं भूषणैः किं प्रयोजनम् ॥

**Transliteration (IAST):**
> *hastasya bhūṣaṇaṃ dānaṃ satyaṃ kaṇṭhasya bhūṣaṇam |*
> *śrotrasya bhūṣaṇaṃ śāstraṃ bhūṣaṇaiḥ kiṃ prayojanam ||*

**English Translation:**
> Charity is the true ornament of the hand; truth is the ornament of the throat; listening to sacred wisdom is the ornament of the ears. What is the need for other external ornaments?

## 📖 गद्य-पाठः (Gadhya Patha - Prose Reading)

Read this conversation between teacher and disciples reviewing yesterday's pilgrimage (*ह्यस्तनी यात्रा*):

### **यात्रायाः स्मरणम् (Remembering the Journey)**

**शिक्षकः:** भोः शिष्याः! **ह्यः यूयम् कुत्र अगच्छत?**
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Bhoḥ śiṣyāḥ! Hyaḥ yūyam kutra agacchata?*
> **Teacher:** O disciples! **Where did you all go yesterday?**

**शिष्यौ:** गुरुदेव! **आवाम् पर्वतारोहणम् अकुर्व, मन्दिरम् च अगच्छाव।**
> *Śiṣyau: Gurudeva! Āvām parvatārohaṇam akurva, mandiram ca agacchāva.*
> **Two Disciples:** Respected teacher! **We two climbed the mountain and went to the temple.**

**शिक्षकः:** राम! **त्वम् तत्र किम् अपश्यः?**
> *Śikṣakaḥ: Rāma! Tvam tatra kim apaśyaḥ?*
> **Teacher:** Rama! **What did you see there?**

**रामः:** **अहम् तत्र सूर्योदयम् अपश्यम्, ईश्वराय पुष्पाणि च अयच्छम्।**
> *Rāmaḥ: Aham tatra sūryodayam apaśyam, īśvarāya puṣpāṇi ca ayaccham.*
> **Rama:** **I saw the sunrise there and offered flowers to God.**

**सर्वे शिष्याः:** **वयम् आनन्देन अभ्रमाम, मिष्टान्नम् च अखादाम।**
> *Sarve śiṣyāḥ: Vayam ānandena abhramāma, miṣṭānnam ca akhādāma.*
> **All Disciples:** **We wandered with joy and ate sweets.**

---

## 🔍 पदविभागः & अन्वयः (Word Breakdown & Analysis)

1. **त्वम् किम् अपश्यः? (tvam kim apaśyaḥ?)**
   * **त्वम्**: 2nd Person Singular Subject (*You*)
   * **अपश्यः**: 2nd Person Sg. Past Tense ending in **-ः (-ḥ)** $\rightarrow$ *You saw*

2. **अहम् अपश्यम् (aham apaśyam)**
   * **अहम्**: 1st Person Singular Subject (*I*)
   * **अपश्यम्**: 1st Person Sg. Past Tense ending in **-अम् (-am)** $\rightarrow$ *I saw*

3. **वयम् अखादाम (vayam akhādāma)**
   * **वयम्**: 1st Person Plural Subject (*We all*)
   * **अखादाम**: 1st Person Pl. Past Tense ending in **-आम (-āma)** $\rightarrow$ *We ate*

## 💡 व्याकरण-विशेषः (Grammatical Notes)

### 1. The Complete $3\times3$ Past Tense Suffix Matrix (लङ् लकारः)

$$\begin{array}{|l|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Person (पुरुषः)} & \textbf{Singular (एक.)} & \textbf{Dual (द्वि.)} & \textbf{Plural (बहु.)} \\
\hline
\textbf{3rd (प्रथमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{-त्} \text{ (-t)} & \textbf{-ताम्} \text{ (-tām)} & \textbf{-अन्} \text{ (-an)} \\
\hline
\textbf{2nd (मध्यमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{-ः} \text{ (-ḥ)} & \textbf{-तम्} \text{ (-tam)} & \textbf{-त} \text{ (-ta)} \\
\hline
\textbf{1st (उत्तमपुरुषः)} & \textbf{-अम्} \text{ (-am)} & \textbf{-आव} \text{ (-āva)} & \textbf{-आम} \text{ (-āma)} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

---

### 2. Full Conjugation of Root **पठ्** (paṭh - to read/study)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3rd (सः/सा/तत्)** | **अपठत्** (*apaṭhat* - he read) | **अपठताम्** (*apaṭhatām*) | **अपठन्** (*apaṭhan*) |
| **2nd (त्वम्/युवाम्/यूयम्)** | **अपठः** (*apaṭhaḥ* - you read) | **अपठतम्** (*apaṭhatam*) | **अपठत** (*apaṭhata*) |
| **1st (अहम्/आवाम्/वयम्)** | **अपठम्** (*apaṭham* - I read) | **अपठाव** (*apaṭhāva*) | **अपठाम** (*apaṭhāma*) |

---

### 3. Full Conjugation of Root **गम् $\rightarrow$ गच्छ्** (gam/gacch - to go)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3rd (प्रथमः)** | **अगच्छत्** (*agacchat*) | **अगच्छताम्** (*agacchatām*) | **अगच्छन्** (*agacchan*) |
| **2nd (मध्यमः)** | **अगच्छः** (*agacchaḥ*) | **अगच्छतम्** (*agacchatam*) | **अगच्छत** (*agacchata*) |
| **1st (उत्तमः)** | **अगच्छम्** (*agaccham*) | **अगच्छाव** (*agacchāva*) | **अगच्छाम** (*agacchāma*) |

---

### 4. Past Tense of Irregular Auxiliary Root **अस्** (as - to be)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3rd Person** | **आसीत्** (*āsīt* - was) | **आस्ताम्** (*āstām* - two were) | **आसन्** (*āsan* - were) |
| **2nd Person** | **आसीः** (*āsīḥ* - you were) | **आस्तम्** (*āstam* - you two were)| **आस्त** (*āsta* - you all were)|
| **1st Person** | **आसम्** (*āsam* - I was) | **आस्व** (*āsva* - we two were) | **आस्म** (*āsma* - we all were) |

## 📝 अभ्यासः (Exercises)

### Exercise 1: Fill in the correct verb form in Laṅ Lakāra
1. अहम् पत्रम् _________ (लिख् $\rightarrow$ I wrote)।
2. त्वम् कुत्र _________ (गम् $\rightarrow$ you went)?
3. वयम् सङ्गीतम् _________ (श्रु $\rightarrow$ we listened to - अशृणुम)।
4. युवाम् पाठम् _________ (पठ् $\rightarrow$ you two read)।
5. ह्यः अहम् नगरे _________ (अस् $\rightarrow$ I was)।

### Exercise 2: Translate into Sanskrit
1. I read two books yesterday.
2. What did you (singular) eat?
3. We two went to the river.
4. You all played in the playground.

---

## 🔑 Answers to Exercises

### Answers to Exercise 1:
1. अहम् पत्रम् **अलिखम्**। (*Aham patram alikham.*)
2. त्वम् कुत्र **अगच्छः**? (*Tvam kutra agacchaḥ?*)
3. वयम् सङ्गीतम् **अशृणुम / अशृणवाम**। (*Vayam saṅgītam aśṛṇuma.*)
4. युवाम् पाठम् **अपठतम्**। (*Yuvām pāṭham apaṭhatam.*)
5. ह्यः अहम् नगरे **आसम्**। (*Hyaḥ aham nagare āsam.*)

### Answers to Exercise 2:
1. ह्यः अहम् द्वे पुस्तके अपठम्। (*Hyaḥ aham dve pustake apaṭham.*)
2. त्वम् किम् अखादः? (*Tvam kim akhādaḥ?*)
3. आवाम् नदीम् अगच्छाव। (*Āvām nadīm agacchāva.*)
4. यूयम् क्रीडाङ्गणे अक्रीडत। (*Yūyam krīḍāṅgaṇe akrīḍata.*)
