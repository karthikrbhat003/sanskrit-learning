# Noun Declensions (Vibhakti - विभक्ति)

In Sanskrit, nouns, pronouns, and adjectives change their endings based on their role in a sentence. This is called declension. A noun has:
1.  **Gender (Linga)**: Masculine (Pullinga), Feminine (Strīlinga), or Neuter (Napuṃsakalinga).
2.  **Number (Vacana)**: Singular (Ekavacana), Dual (Dvivacana), or Plural (Bahuvacana).
3.  **Case (Vibhakti)**: 7 cases + 1 Vocative (calling/addressing).

---

## 📌 The Eight Cases & Their Meanings

| Case (Vibhakti) | Sanskrit Name | English Equivalent | Primary Meaning / Helper Words |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Case** | Prathamā (प्रथमा) | Nominative | **The Subject** (who/what does the action) |
| **2nd Case** | Dvitīyā (द्वितीया) | Accusative | **The Direct Object** (to whom/to what) |
| **3rd Case** | Tṛtīyā (तृतीया) | Instrumental | **By / With** (instrument/means of action) |
| **4th Case** | Caturthī (चतुर्थी) | Dative | **To / For** (recipient of action/purpose) |
| **5th Case** | Pañcamī (पञ्चमी) | Ablative | **From / Than** (source of separation or comparison) |
| **6th Case** | Ṣaṣṭhī (षष्ठी) | Genitive | **Of / 's** (shows possession or relationship) |
| **7th Case** | Saptamī (सप्तमी) | Locative | **In / On / At** (location in space or time) |
| **8th Case** | Sambodhana (सम्बोधन) | Vocative | **O ...! / Hey ...!** (used for addressing) |

---

## 📘 Model Declension 1: Masculine nouns ending in '-a' (अकारान्त-पुल्लिङ्गः)
*Example word: **देव (deva)** - god/divine being*

| Vibhakti | Singular (Ekavacana) | Dual (Dvivacana) | Plural (Bahuvacana) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prathamā** | देवः (devaḥ) | देवौ (devau) | देवाः (devāḥ) |
| **Dvitīyā** | देवम् (devam) | देवौ (devau) | देवान् (devān) |
| **Tṛtīyā** | देवेन (devena) | देवाभ्याम् (devābhyām) | देवैः (devaiḥ) |
| **Caturthī** | देवाय (devāya) | देवाभ्याम् (devābhyām) | देवेभ्यः (devebhyaḥ) |
| **Pañcamī** | देवात् (devāt) | देवाभ्याम् (devābhyām) | देवेभ्यः (devebhyaḥ) |
| **Ṣaṣṭhī** | देवस्य (devasya) | देवयोः (devayoḥ) | देवानाम (devānām) |
| **Saptamī** | देवे (deve) | देवयोः (devayoḥ) | देवेषु (deveṣu) |
| **Sambodhana** | हे देव (he deva) | हे देवौ (he devau) | हे देवाः (he devāḥ) |

---

## 📙 Model Declension 2: Feminine nouns ending in '-ā' (आकारान्त-स्त्रीलिङ्गः)
*Example word: **लता (latā)** - creeper/vine*

| Vibhakti | Singular (Ekavacana) | Dual (Dvivacana) | Plural (Bahuvacana) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prathamā** | लता (latā) | लते (late) | लताः (latāḥ) |
| **Dvitīyā** | लताम् (latām) | लते (late) | लताः (latāḥ) |
| **Tṛtīyā** | लतया (latayā) | लताभ्याम् (latābhyām) | लताभिः (latābhiḥ) |
| **Caturthī** | लतायै (latāyai) | लताभ्याम् (latābhyām) | लताभ्यः (latābhyaḥ) |
| **Pañcamī** | लतायाः (latāyāḥ) | लताभ्याम् (latābhyām) | लताभ्यः (latābhyaḥ) |
| **Ṣaṣṭhī** | लतायाः (latāyāḥ) | लतयोः (latayoḥ) | लतानाम् (latānām) |
| **Saptamī** | लतायाम् (latāyām) | लतयोः (latayoḥ) | लतासु (latāsu) |
| **Sambodhana** | हे लते (he late) | हे लते (he late) | हे लताः (he latāḥ) |

---

## 📗 Model Declension 3: Neuter nouns ending in '-a' (अकारान्त-नपुंसकलिङ्गः)
*Example word: **फल (phala)** - fruit*
*(Notice: Only Prathamā and Dvitīyā are different from masculine 'deva'; Tṛtīyā to Saptamī are identical to the masculine declension!)*

| Vibhakti | Singular (Ekavacana) | Dual (Dvivacana) | Plural (Bahuvacana) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prathamā** | फलम् (phalam) | फले (phale) | फलानि (phalāni) |
| **Dvitīyā** | फलम् (phalam) | फले (phale) | फलानि (phalāni) |
| **Tṛtīyā** | फलेन (phalena) | फलाभ्याम् (phalābhyām) | फलैः (phalaiḥ) |
| **Caturthī** | फलाय (phalāya) | फलाभ्याम् (phalābhyām) | फलेभ्यः (phalebhyaḥ) |
| **Pañcamī** | फलात् (phalāt) | फलाभ्याम् (phalābhyām) | फलेभ्यः (phalebhyaḥ) |
| **Ṣaṣṭhī** | फलस्य (phalasya) | फलयोः (phalayoḥ) | फलानाम् (phalānām) |
| **Saptamī** | फले (phale) | फलयोः (phalayoḥ) | फलेषु (phaleṣu) |
| **Sambodhana** | हे फल (he phala) | हे फले (he phale) | हे फलानि (he phalāni) |
