# Verb Conjugations (Dhātu Rūpa - धातुरूपाणि)

In Sanskrit, verbs are conjugated from basic root meanings called **Dhātus** (धातु). Verbs change their endings according to:
1.  **Tense or Mood (Lakāra - लकार)**: Sanskrit has 10 Lakāras, though 5 are most commonly used in literature.
2.  **Person (Puruṣa - पुरुष)**: Third Person (Prathama Puruṣa), Second Person (Madhyama Puruṣa), or First Person (Uttama Puruṣa).
3.  **Number (Vacana - वचन)**: Singular (Ekavacana), Dual (Dvivacana), or Plural (Bahuvacana).
4.  **Voice (Pada - पद)**: Parasmaipada (active/other-benefitting) or Ātmanepada (middle/self-benefitting). Some verbs use both (Ubhayapada).

---

## 📅 The 5 Common Tenses & Moods (Lakāras)

*   **Laṭ Lakāra (लट्-लकारः)**: Present Tense (*e.g., he reads*)
*   **Laṅ Lakāra (लङ्-लकारः)**: Past Tense (*e.g., he read*)
*   **Lṛṭ Lakāra (लृट्-लकारः)**: Future Tense (*e.g., he will read*)
*   **Loṭ Lakāra (लोट्-लकारः)**: Imperative Mood (Command / Request; *e.g., Read!*)
*   **Vidhilin Lakāra (विधिलिङ्-लकारः)**: Potential Mood (Advice / Possibility; *e.g., he should read*)

---

## 📘 Model Verb 1: पठ् (paṭh) - to read / to study
*Class 1 Parasmaipada (भ्वादिगण-परस्मैपद)*

### 1. Laṭ Lakāra (लट्) - Present Tense
| Puruṣa (Person) | Singular (Ekavacana) | Dual (Dvivacana) | Plural (Bahuvacana) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prathama** (3rd) | पठति (paṭhati) <br> *He/She/It reads* | पठतः (paṭhataḥ) <br> *They two read* | पठन्ति (paṭhanti) <br> *They all read* |
| **Madhyama** (2nd) | पठसि (paṭhasi) <br> *You read* | पठथः (paṭhathaḥ) <br> *You two read* | पठथ (paṭhatha) <br> *You all read* |
| **Uttama** (1st) | पठामि (paṭhāmi) <br> *I read* | पठावः (paṭhāvaḥ) <br> *We two read* | पठामः (paṭhāmaḥ) <br> *We all read* |

## 📙 Model Verb 2: भू (bhū) - to be / to become
*Class 1 Parasmaipada (भ्वादिगण-परस्मैपद)*

### 1. Laṭ Lakāra (लट्) - Present Tense
| Puruṣa (Person) | Singular (Ekavacana) | Dual (Dvivacana) | Plural (Bahuvacana) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prathama** (3rd) | भवति (bhavati) <br> *He/She/It becomes* | भवतः (bhavataḥ) <br> *They two become* | भवन्ति (bhavanti) <br> *They all become* |
| **Madhyama** (2nd) | भवसि (bhavasi) <br> *You become* | भवथः (bhavathaḥ) <br> *You two become* | भवथ (bhavatha) <br> *You all become* |
| **Uttama** (1st) | भवामि (bhavāmi) <br> *I become* | भवावः (bhavāvaḥ) <br> *We two become* | भवामः (bhavāmaḥ) <br> *We all become* |

## 🔑 Subject-Verb Agreement Pattern

In a sentence, the verb's person and number must match the subject. Pronouns align as follows:

| Subject Pronoun | Sanskrit Pronoun | Verb Form | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **He / She / It** | सः / सा / तत् (saḥ / sā / tat) | Prathama Puruṣa | सः पठति (He reads) |
| **You** | त्वम् (tvam) | Madhyama Puruṣa | त्वम् पठसि (You read) |
| **I** | अहम् (aham) | Uttama Puruṣa | अहम् पठामि (I read) |
